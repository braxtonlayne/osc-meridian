## 1. The indicator

The platform indicator lists the services due at this platform for the rest of the day, arriving and departing, with the time in platform local time and the consist number. Services entered by Transit Control outside the timetable are shown as SPECIAL.

## 2. Status words

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| BOOKED | Due to run at the time shown |
| APPROACHING | On the way to this platform |
| DEPARTED | Left this platform |
| ARRIVED | At or through this platform |
| HELD | Stopped on instruction. It will run when released |
| CANCELLED | Will not run today |

## 3. When services are held

When the SERVICES HELD lamp is lit, stand clear of the platform edge and the screen doors and wait for the duty supervisor. Do not board or leave a consist standing at the platform unless told to.

During a seal drill the tunnel portal door at the end of the platform is closed and services stay at the platform until the drill stands down. Go to your muster point if the drill calls for it. The portal door is not opened from the platform.

## 4. Faults

Report an indicator fault to Facilities Engineering on the site extension. Do not report it to Transit Control.
