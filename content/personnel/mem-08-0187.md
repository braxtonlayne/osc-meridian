This memorandum reports the result of the monthly reconciliation of the pay run to the facility strength returns required by :x[OSC-DIR-0233], and requests direction on a difference that I have not been able to reconcile or carry forward with a stated reason.

Pay run 08-07 carries total personnel :c[headline.staffTotal], which agrees with the tier figures in :x[OSC-PER-IDX]. The facility strength returns as at 31 MAR 2008, received from all :c[headline.facilities] installations by the due date, account for total posted staff :c[headline.staffPosted]. Every return was checked to the personnel number register and no return is in error.

| Facility class | Posted (returns as at 31 MAR 2008) |
|---|---|
| ALPHA | :c[classes.ALPHA.statedStaff] |
| BETA | :c[classes.BETA.statedStaff] |
| GAMMA | :c[classes.GAMMA.statedStaff] |
| DELTA | :c[classes.DELTA.statedStaff] |
| Total posted staff | :c[headline.staffPosted] |
| Pay run 08-07, all tiers | :c[headline.staffTotal] |
| Net difference | :c[headline.staffUnposted] |

The whole of the difference is carried in a single payroll category, DETACHED / UNPOSTED (:c[headline.staffUnposted] persons). No person in the category appears on any facility return. The category has no site, no division and no cost centre other than the central account, and is paid on the same run and at the same grades as posted personnel.

I have not been able to find the issuance that establishes the category. :x[OSC-DIR-0233] defines POSTED in paragraph 3, and its paragraph 6, Categories other than POSTED, is printed as reserved. The Glossary (:x[OSC-SYS-GLO]) defines the category as a payroll category and refers to personnel accounting; the entry for personnel accounting refers to the governing payroll issuance. I have asked the Comptroller's payroll section for that issuance by number and was told that the category is set up at the Personnel & Vetting end.

The category count has not decreased at any pay run in the period I have reviewed (pay run 08-01 to pay run 08-07). Persons enter the category with an effective date and their personnel number is not reissued. I have found no entry that leaves it.

The STRENGTH utility at the workstation command line returns the category total without breakdown. Run as `strength unposted`, it gives the same figure as the pay run and no other detail. The names and last postings in Attachment A were therefore taken from the pay register line by line.

Unposted Status Declarations (:x[OSC-F-131]) continue to arrive at this office from facility command sections. The form, edition 11/2005, carries no instructions block, and I cannot tell from it who is required to declare, what a declaration is used for, or whether a declaration should change the figures in this reconciliation. Collection for the current period closes 27 MAY 2008 (:x[OSC-SYS-CAL]). Declarations received so far are held unopened in the query file pending your direction.

Under paragraph 5 of :x[OSC-DIR-0233] a difference must be reconciled to named posts or carried forward with a stated reason. I can do neither for this category. I therefore raise query PV-Q-08-0031 and request: (a) the number of the issuance that governs the category; (b) direction on whether the category is to be reconciled, and against what; and (c) instructions for the handling of OSC-F-131 declarations held in this office.

Suspense requested: 30 APR 2008, so that the April return can be completed. I am available on extension 6-2214 in the Level 6 accounts room, 0700Z to 1530Z.
