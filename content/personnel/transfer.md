## 1. Purpose

This record sets out the procedure for transferring personnel between facilities and for the probationary period that follows every transfer. The policy is in :x[OSC-DIR-0247]; where this record and the directive differ, the directive governs.

## 2. Requests

2.1. A transfer is requested on OSC-F-122 (:x[OSC-F-122]) by the person or by the losing facility commander, and is approved by the Director, Personnel & Vetting. A transfer directed by Personnel & Vetting needs no request.

2.2. A person on probation may not request a transfer. A request submitted during probation is refused under :x[OSC-DIR-0247] and the refusal is recorded.

## 3. Debriefing and briefing

3.1. Before departure the person is debriefed from every compartment held at the losing facility, except compartments held network-wide by appointment.

3.2. On arrival the person is given a new compartment briefing at the gaining facility, for the compartment of the division to which they are posted, before any record of that compartment is displayed to them (:x[OSC-DIR-0412]). Records Division personnel at ALPHA-03, for example, are read into the records compartment on posting.

3.3. Further compartments are not given during probation except as provided in :x[OSC-DIR-0412] para 4.

## 4. Arrival

4.1. The gaining command section issues a badge, a locker and a berthing allocation on the day of arrival.

4.2. The gaining division names a supervisor of record, Tier 2, who signs the probation reports.

4.3. The person is carried under the supervisor of record in the Personnel Directory until the final review.

4.4. A MERIDIAN workstation is issued from the facility pool by the Service Desk. Previous user profiles are archived before reissue and are not available to the new user. The workstation number is entered on the access record and does not change for the period of probation.

## 5. Probationary period

5.1. Every transfer is followed by a probationary period of 90 days. The effective date of the transfer is day 0.

5.2. Interim reviews are held at day 30 and day 60, and a final review at day 90. Each review is completed by the supervisor of record on the review form and countersigned by the deputy commander of the gaining facility.

5.3. During probation the person:

- may not hold an acting credential, except as provided in :x[OSC-DIR-0395] para 3(b);
- may not request a transfer or approve their own leave (:x[OSC-DIR-0189]);
- has their workstation audit trail reviewed daily by the Resident Assurance Officer (:x[OSC-ASR-PSEC]);
- is reinvestigated only on a trigger (:x[OSC-PER-VET]).

5.4. Example of the register entry and review dates for a transfer:

| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| Personnel number | P-3-YY-NNNN |
| Tier | 3 |
| Gaining facility, division | ALPHA-NN, Records Division |
| Effective date (day 0) | 14 APR |
| Day 30 interim review | 14 MAY |
| Day 60 interim review | 13 JUN |
| Day 90 final review | 13 JUL |
| Supervisor of record | P-2-YY-NNNN |
| Workstation | A0N-L-NNN |

## 6. Outcome of reviews

6.1. At an interim review the supervisor records SATISFACTORY, SATISFACTORY WITH ACTION or UNSATISFACTORY, and lists outstanding items (training, medical readiness, briefings) with suspense dates.

6.2. At the final review the supervisor recommends one of: probation completed; probation extended by up to 90 days; return to the losing facility; or reassignment as directed by Personnel & Vetting. Extension, return and reassignment are approved by the Director, Personnel & Vetting.

6.3. Exception. Where the gaining branch has no Tier 2 reviewer available for a duty, the supervisor of record may at an interim review recommend that a person on probation be given an acting designation for that duty. The designation may be issued only where the Deputy Commander of the gaining facility certifies operational necessity under :x[OSC-DIR-0395] para 3(b). Application is made on OSC-F-126 (:x[OSC-F-126]) with the supervisor of record as sponsor, or as provided in :x[OSC-DIR-0395] para 7. The acting designation does not shorten or interrupt probation.

6.4. A person whose probation is not completed remains on the pay run in their current category until the outcome is approved.
