## Target

The phase target is :c[phases.4.targetPct] per cent of sustained capacity :c[headline.capacitySustained], that is :c[phases.4.targetCount] personnel by the end of 2012. Sustained capacity is the planning basis for all recruitment targets and is defined, with surge berthing, in :x[OSC-MEM-07-0612] and the Glossary (:x[OSC-SYS-GLO]). Targets are not to be stated against berthing.

Strength at the start of the phase is taken as the cumulative series at the close of Phase 3 (:x[OSC-PER-PH3]).

## Objectives as stated

- Target staffing: :c[phases.4.targetPct] per cent of capacity, :c[phases.4.targetCount] personnel
- Full operational capability and recruitment acceleration
- Complete six additional GAMMA-class facilities, with emphasis on West Coast density
- Establish ALPHA-06, the Alaska strategic reserve facility
- Expand BETA-03 biomedical research capacity
- Integrate satellite communication redundancy
- Accelerate Tier 3 personnel recruitment, target 800 per year

## Planning notes

Investigation capacity under the OSC supplement is to be increased before the first year of the phase. The Recruitment Branch has asked for two additional investigator posts and an annex of interview rooms at a BETA site. Personnel costs for the phase are carried in :x[OSC-LDG-CAT-PERSONNEL] at the FY2008 rate and are restated when the Council approves the phase.

## Recruitment sources

:::redact{tier=2 auth="PV" ref="OSC-PER-PH4"}
Phase 4 adds two sources to those used in Phase 3: the families of serving personnel, who are already partly investigated under the multi-generational element of the supplement, and the cover workforces at ALPHA and BETA surface facilities, whose members already hold cleared access. Approaches to family members are made by the serving relative's facility commander. Cover workforce candidates are identified by Cover Operations and are not approached until the cover review for their facility has been completed.
:::
