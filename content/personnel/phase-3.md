## Record

Phase 3 recruitment is directed at the analysis cells at BETA sites and the fusion floor (:x[OSC-PER-BR-OPS]), and at reactor operators, tunnel engineers and communications specialists (:x[OSC-PER-BR-TEC]). GAMMA superintendents and duty officers were appointed in the first half of 2007 so that each shelter had a nominated superintendent before it was declared operational.

The intake target for the phase was set at the outset and is reported as met. Recruitment continues against vacancies only. Quarterly intake reports are issued on the first working day of the quarter.

## Recruitment sources

:::redact{tier=2 auth="PV" ref="PV-06-203"}
Analysts are recruited from the intelligence community by referral from serving OSC analysts, who are asked once a year to name colleagues they would trust with their own work. Referees are not told the outcome. Technical recruits come from naval nuclear propulsion, tunnelling contractors and the communications staffs of the military services. Phase 3 is the first phase in which a recruit's family was interviewed during the investigation, under the multi-generational element of the supplement.
:::
