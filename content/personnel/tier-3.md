## What functional awareness means

You are told what your duties require. You are not told the location, purpose or connections of installations other than your own, the strength of other installations, or the content of compartments you do not hold. A refusal on your workstation means the item is outside your tier or compartments; it is not a fault and is not to be reported to the Service Desk. Refusals are recorded (:x[OSC-DIR-0412] para 8).

If a duty needs access you do not have, your supervisor of record may sponsor a request on OSC-F-126 (:x[OSC-F-126]) for a compartment they hold. Do not ask colleagues to open or print items for you.

## Compartments

Tier 3 personnel hold one or more compartments, each by a recorded briefing. The compartment of your division is given on posting. Further compartments are given only in the ways listed in :x[OSC-DIR-0412] para 4. Holding a compartment does not tell you anything about another compartment, including its name.

## Transfers and probation

A transfer to another facility requires a new compartment briefing at the gaining facility and a 90-day probationary period, with interim reviews at day 30 and day 60 and a final review at day 90. Compartments held at the losing facility are withdrawn at debriefing. The rules are in :x[OSC-PER-TRF] and :x[OSC-DIR-0247].

## Standing requirements

- Annual security refresher, completed by the suspense set by your division (:x[OSC-DIR-0288]). Print release is suspended while modules are incomplete.
- Medical readiness certification, renewed annually.
- Periodic reinvestigation under the OSC supplement every five years, or earlier on a trigger (:x[OSC-PER-VET]).
- Personal notes and notebooks are subject to :x[OSC-DIR-0519].
