## Strength and composition

Tier 2 strength is :c[tiers.2.count] (:x[OSC-PER-IDX]). The tier is composed of:

- facility commanders (ALPHA and BETA) and site superintendents (GAMMA);
- deputy commanders and chiefs of staff;
- senior intelligence officers;
- directors and deputy directors of the thirteen divisions, and the Secretary to the Council (:x[OSC-PER-ORG]).

Clearance is :c[tiers.2.clearance]. Knowledge scope, as stated in the Facility Inventory: :c[tiers.2.knowledgeScope]

"Immediate connections" means the installations directly linked to the assigned facility by rail segment or tram link as shown in the facility's own connection list. It does not include installations reached through another installation.

## Breakdown by installation

:::redact{tier=2 auth="PV" ref="OSC-DIR-0233"}
| Class | Posts at each installation | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| ALPHA | Commander, deputy commander, chief of staff, senior intelligence officer; resident division directors and deputies | ALPHA-01 also carries the Secretary to the Council, OIA and the network division directors. |
| BETA | Commander, deputy commander, senior intelligence officer | The BETA-03 commander is also scientific director of the biomedical programme. |
| GAMMA | Site superintendent | Deputy duties are taken by the senior duty officer, Tier 3. |
| DELTA | None | DELTA sites are under the parent BETA command. |

Tier 2 posts at each installation are shown on the Command Staff line of the facility strength return. Vacant Tier 2 posts are not shown on the return and are reported to Personnel & Vetting by the facility commander within five working days.
:::

## Acting credentials

A Tier 3 officer may be given the access of Tier 2 for a named duty. :x[OSC-DIR-0395] defines the acting credential as follows (also at :x[OSC-SYS-GLO]):

> :c[glossary.ACTING CREDENTIAL.def]

An acting Tier 2 officer is not counted in Tier 2 strength and remains on the Tier 3 line of the pay run. The acting credential is recorded in the workstation audit trail. A person on probationary transfer may not hold an acting credential except as provided in :x[OSC-DIR-0395] para 3(b) (:x[OSC-PER-TRF]).

## Obligations particular to Tier 2

Tier 2 officers sign the probation reports of the personnel they supervise, sponsor compartment requests on OSC-F-126 where they hold the compartment, and certify the strength return of their command. A Tier 2 officer who signs on behalf of another officer signs "for" that officer; the officer named remains responsible for the content. Delegations of signature are notified to Records Division, which holds the signature specimens.
