## When this form is used

A single print release copy of a record for the reader's own use is made from the workstation under :x[OSC-DIR-0319] and does not need this form. This form is used for anything more: a second or further copy, copies for other readers, an extract, or a replacement cover sheet for a copy whose sheet has been damaged.

Each copy made is numbered and added to the record's copy count. A copy made without a request is an unauthorized copy, however it was made.

## What is never reproduced

Material marked UMBRA//OSC EYES ONLY is not reproduced in any form: not printed, not photocopied, not scanned, not transcribed and not summarized (:x[OSC-DIR-0319]). Requests naming such material are refused at once and the refusal is recorded. There is no exception and no authority below the Council can grant one.

Compartmented material is reproduced only for persons who hold the compartment (:x[OSC-DIR-0412]). Name them in block 4.

## Completing the blocks

**Block 1.** Entered from the session. Copies are released only to the device shown.

**Block 2.** Give the document number exactly as it appears in the control strip, and the revision. For a long record, reproduce only the pages needed.

**Block 3.** Ten copies is the most that may be requested on one form. A larger run is arranged with the releasing office directly and needs the head of the originating division's agreement.

**Block 4.** State why the copies are needed and who will hold them. A copy may be held only by a person named here.

**Block 5.** Read the undertaking. Copies are destroyed on :x[OSC-F-137] when no longer needed.

## Routing

Supervisor of record (comment); registry of the originating division, which is the releasing office; Communications Security for the print release log.

## Processing time

Decided within two working days. Released copies are collected from the device in the same duty period. Uncollected jobs are purged and the request lapses.
