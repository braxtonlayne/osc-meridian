## Who needs an escort

A visitor is anyone, including a member of the Office, who does not hold unescorted access to the area to be entered. Personnel from another site, contractors and persons whose access is limited to other levels of the same site are all visitors for this purpose.

The escort must hold unescorted access to every area named in block 3 and must be at least the tier of the most restricted area visited.

## Completing the blocks

**Block 1.** One form per party. Where the party is more than one person, name the first visitor here and the others in block 3 detail, with their numbers. A contractor gives the pass number shown on the contractor pass, not a company employee number.

**Block 2.** You may escort up to four visitors at once on the levels open to Tier 4. On any level above that, the limit is two. Name an alternate escort for any visit expected to last more than four hours; the visitor may not be left with someone who is not on the form.

**Block 3.** Tick each area and say why it is needed. "Other" is used for rooms on levels not listed; give the level and room number. Requests that name a level without a reason are returned.

:::redact{tier=2 auth="SFC" ref="SFC-06-0163"}
Visits to reactor plant levels, to cold storage holding biological material and to any level with an armed post are not requested on this form. They are arranged by the Deputy Commander's office of the site, with a Tier 2 escort and an armed security forces escort for the duration, and are entered on the access roster by the security office directly.
:::

**Block 4.** Give site local time. The visitor is met at the portal by the escort at the arrival time shown and is taken back to the portal by the departure time. A visit that runs over is reported by the escort to the security office before the departure time, not after.

**Block 5.** Cameras, recording devices and personal electronic equipment are not brought in. Media and documents brought in by a visitor are inspected at the portal and may be held there until departure.

**Block 6.** The sponsoring branch head approves the visit. Escorts may not sponsor their own visits.

## During the visit

The escort keeps the visitor in sight at all times, including in washrooms on levels where the visitor is not cleared for the corridor. The visitor log at the portal is signed on arrival and departure by both. Lost visitor badges are reported to the security office at once.

## Routing

Sponsoring branch head; site security office (Security Forces Command); portal security post.

## Processing time

Requests received by 1600 site time are on the next day's roster. The security office does not take same-day requests on this form.
