## Purpose

This form is used to ask to be read into a compartment that you need for your work and that you were not read into on posting. It is also the application form for an acting Tier 2 credential.

No compartmented material may be shown to a person who has not been read in (:x[OSC-DIR-0412]). Para 4 of that directive lists the ways a person is read in. This form serves para 4(b) only: read-in on request, sponsored by a holder. A request on this form for a compartment that is read in only in another way is returned.

## The compartments

| Compartment | Scope | Briefing | Read in on request |
|---|---|---|---|
| KESTREL | Transit engineering: segment sheets, tunnel environmental data, rolling stock technical records, possession planning, commissioning files | OSC-CB-011 | Yes |
| HOLLOW | Personnel matters concerning personnel carried without a posting: status declarations, strength reconciliations | OSC-CB-012 | No |
| SEXTANT | Network geography beyond the holder's assigned site | OSC-CB-013 | Yes |
| TALLOW | Reserves positions by site and reactor operations | OSC-CB-014 | No |
| BRIAR | Assurance contingencies, inquiry files and dispositions | OSC-CB-015 | No |
| QUARRY | Council and governance records above UMBRA//OSC RESTRICTED | OSC-CB-016 | No |

LANTERN is granted on posting to the Records Division and is not requested on this form.

## Completing the blocks

**Block 1.** Entered from the session.

**Block 2.** Choose one compartment. A person who needs two compartments makes two requests, which may have different sponsors. "For a stated task" means the read-in is withdrawn when the task ends; give the end date in block 4.

**Block 3.** The sponsor must hold the compartment requested at the time the request is made. The Personnel Directory (:x[OSC-PER-DIR]) shows, against each entry, the compartments the person holds, where the reader is entitled to know them. Agree the request with the sponsor before submitting it. The sponsor acknowledges it at their own workstation; a sponsor who has not been asked may decline, and the request then lapses.

**Block 4.** Say what work you need the compartment for, and cite by document number at least one record in the compartment's scope that you need to read. A justification that cites no record, or cites only records outside the compartment's scope, is returned. Do not quote compartmented text in the justification.

**Block 5.** Read the undertaking. Your signature is applied from your token.

## Acting credentials

An acting credential confers the clearance of a higher tier without its briefings, for a stated purpose, on the terms of :x[OSC-DIR-0395]. Select ACTING-T2 in block 2. The sponsor in block 3 is your supervisor of record, and block 4 states the duty for which the credential is needed.

A person in a probationary period is not eligible for an acting credential, except where the Deputy Commander of the site certifies operational necessity under :x[OSC-DIR-0395] para 3(b). The certification is obtained by the supervisor of record, not by the applicant. Applications from probationers without it are returned with the reason.

## Routing

Sponsor (acknowledgement at the workstation); OIA compartment registry at ALPHA-01; supervisor of record (notified of the outcome).

## Processing time

A request that meets the conditions is decided on receipt and the briefing is sent to the applicant's workstation for acknowledgement. Requests the registry refers for review are decided within five working days. Access begins when the briefing is acknowledged, not when the request is approved.
