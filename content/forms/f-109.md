## Purpose

Authorization for a passenger journey by inter-hub rail or regional tram. One form covers one passenger and one outward journey; a return journey booked at the same time is entered in block 2.

This form is not used for the movement of stores, records consignments or rolling stock, which are booked by the originating division directly with Transit Operations.

## How seats are allocated

Seats are allocated by Transit Operations against the paths in the current working timetable, :x[OSC-TRN-TT-08-02], in the priority order set by :x[OSC-DIR-0262]. The traveller does not choose the path. The preferred departure window in block 2 is taken into account where the timetable allows.

Passengers are not told the consist identity before boarding. Do not telephone Network Control to ask for it.

## Completing the blocks

**Block 1.** Entered from the session. One passenger per form; a party travels on one form each, with the party leader named in block 3 of every form.

**Block 2.** Origin and destination are chosen from the list. The list shows the sites served from the origin by paths on the Central Network (:x[OSC-TRN-NET-CEN]) and by the inter-hub segments that serve this site. Other destinations are shown where the passenger holds the briefing for them.

:::redact{compartment=SEXTANT auth="TO" ref="TO-07-313"}
Journeys to ALPHA-02 are booked over segments 01 and 02 by way of ALPHA-01; journeys to ALPHA-04 continue from ALPHA-02 over segment 03. No journey to ALPHA-02 is booked by way of the Southern hub. Journeys to a site on the Eastern, Southern or Mountain/Western Network are booked to the hub of that network by rail and onward by tram; the booking desk enters the tram link, not the passenger.
:::

Leave "Routing requested" blank. It is used only where a particular routing is required for the purpose of the journey, for example an escort duty that joins a consist at an intermediate platform, and then only with the segment number or tram link as shown in the working timetable. A routing that has no paths in the working timetable is returned at once with the timetable reference.

**Block 3.** State the purpose plainly. "Official duty" requires a line of detail sufficient for the approving official to judge whether the journey is necessary. Training and briefing journeys give the course or briefing number. Medical journeys give the clinic referral number only, not the condition.

**Block 4.** Passenger baggage is limited by :x[OSC-DIR-0262]. Records carried in hand are listed by number. Records marked UMBRA//OSC EYES ONLY are moved by courier under the Council Secretariat's arrangements and never by a passenger.

**Block 5.** The approving official is the supervisor of record, entered from the session, unless the journey is made on another division's business, in which case it is the head of the branch paying for it.

## Routing

Approving official; Transit Operations booking desk, Network Control (TO/ALPHA-01); security posts at origin and destination platforms.

## Processing time

The booking desk confirms the allocation by 1800Z on the working day before travel. Authorizations received after 1200Z for travel on the following day are allocated only if seats remain.
