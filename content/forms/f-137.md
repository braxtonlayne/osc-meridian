## Purpose

This certificate records the destruction of a record at the end of its retention period. Once it is accepted the accession is closed and the record ceases to exist for the purposes of the Office. A record destroyed without a certificate is treated as lost, and the loss is reported on :x[OSC-F-114].

## Conditions

1. Only records whose retention period has ended under the schedule in :x[OSC-DIR-0354] are destroyed. If the schedule item cannot be identified, the record is not destroyed.
2. Destruction is witnessed by two persons, neither of whom is the destroying officer, both holding access to the records destroyed. The witnesses watch the records go into the equipment and inspect the residue.
3. The certificate is completed and signed at the time. Certificates are not signed after the fact, and a witness who was not present does not sign.
4. Records Division holds witnessing sessions in the destruction room on Level 7 on dates published in the site calendar, normally for two hours in the morning. Divisions bringing records to a session book a slot with the accessions desk the day before.

## Council copies

Council copies are numbered copies issued to Council seats. Their succession and destruction are administered by the Council Secretariat under its own procedure and are not certified on this form (:x[OSC-DIR-0354]). A Council copy found in a division's custody is not destroyed. It is secured and reported.

## Completing the blocks

**Block 1.** Give the accession number from the custody receipt (:x[OSC-DIR-0336]) and the document number from the record. One document per certificate. Where several copies of the same document are destroyed together, give each copy number. Print release copies are destroyed with their cover sheets.

**Block 2.** The schedule item is the line in the retention schedule under which the record is held. The date is the day the retention period ended, not the day of destruction.

**Block 3.** Use approved equipment only. Shredder and pulper plant numbers are on the plate on each machine. Magnetic media are degaussed before shredding. Paper and media are not destroyed together.

**Block 4.** Zulu time.

**Block 5.** Both witnesses enter their numbers at the workstation in the destruction room before the destroying officer signs.

**Block 6.** The destroying officer is the person who placed the records in the equipment.

## Routing

Destroying officer; Records Division accession register; copy to the registry of the originating division.

## Processing time

Accessions are closed in the register on receipt. Defective certificates are returned within two working days and the records they name are treated as not destroyed until a correct certificate is received.
