## General

Use this form for office equipment, workstation peripherals and consumables. Furniture, tools and protective equipment for plant work are requested from Facilities Engineering on a work order. Workstations, tokens and print devices are issued by Communications Security through the MERIDIAN Service Desk.

## Completing the blocks

**Block 1.** The branch named is charged for the items. Give the room or workstation where they are used.

**Block 2.** One item per form. Where the item is not on the list, choose "Other" and describe it; the stores clerk will tell you if it can be supplied. Toner requests give the print device number from the label on the device.

**Block 3.** A replacement for a faulty item is issued on return of the faulty item. A replacement for a lost item is issued only after the loss is reported to the branch head; lost lockable cases and torches are also reported to the site security office.

**Block 4.** Signature from token.

## Collection

Items are collected from the site stores counter by the requester. The counter hours are printed on the receipt. Stores do not deliver to office levels, except paper and archive boxes in quantity, which are delivered on the morning trolley round.
