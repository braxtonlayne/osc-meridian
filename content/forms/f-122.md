## Who may apply

Any member of the Office who has completed probation at the present site may apply. A person in a probationary period may not request a transfer; this follows from the conditions of probation in :x[OSC-DIR-0247] and there is no exception to it. Requests from probationers are not forwarded.

Transfers directed by the Office for its own reasons are not requested on this form and do not use it.

## What a transfer involves

A transfer to another site is a new posting. It requires a new compartment briefing for the gaining site, a new probationary period with interim reviews, and a new supervisor of record (:x[OSC-PER-TRF]). An acting credential held at the losing site lapses on transfer and is not carried across (:x[OSC-DIR-0395]).

## Completing the blocks

**Block 1.** Entered from the session. "Date posted to present site" is the start of your present posting, not your date of entry to the Office.

**Block 2.** Name a site, not a region. Sites are given by facility designation. Give a second choice if you have one; the board considers the second choice only where the first cannot be met. Postings to :r[DELTA sites, and to the Special Collections Annex at ALPHA-03,]{tier=1 auth="COUNCIL" ref="OSC-CNL-RES-06-02"} are not requested on this form.

**Block 3.** Give one reason category and a short statement. Medical requests give the clinic referral number only. Compassionate requests may be supported by a letter to the postings desk, which is not attached to the form.

**Block 4.** Read and tick the conditions. Your signature is applied from your token.

## Routing

Supervisor of record (comment); head of the losing division (comment); Personnel & Vetting postings desk; head of the gaining division (comment, only where the board is minded to approve).

## Processing time

Considered at the monthly postings board. The outcome is notified in writing after the board. No reasons are given for a refusal.
