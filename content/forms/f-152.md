## Purpose

Every member of the Office posted below grade, to a transit crew or escort, or to an armed post, holds a medical readiness certification renewed annually (:x[OSC-DIR-0204]). This form books the examination and records the health declaration that goes with it.

The certification is not a general health check. It confirms fitness for the post: enclosed-space tolerance, respiratory protection fit, vision, hearing and, for armed posts, the weapons standard.

## When to apply

Apply in the month before your certification expires. The site clinic sets aside one examination day each month for certifications expiring that month; the appointments offered in block 2 are on that day. Where none suits, choose the last option and the clinic will offer another time.

A person whose certification lapses is removed from the seal roster on the day after expiry. Access to below-grade posts is suspended thirty days after lapse, until a new certification is entered.

## Completing the blocks

**Block 1.** Entered from the session and the readiness register. Check the expiry date against your last certificate and tell the clinic if they differ. Choose the post type that applies to your present post, not a post you expect to hold.

**Block 2.** Choose one appointment. Appointments are twenty minutes. Arrive ten minutes early.

**Block 3.** Answer every question. A "Yes" does not of itself prevent certification. Give details briefly; the examining officer will ask about them. Do not describe the work of your post.

**Block 4.** Signature from token.

## Routing

Site clinic; Medical Services readiness register.

## Processing time

The appointment is confirmed by workstation message. The certification is entered in the register on the day of the examination, or the person is told what further assessment is needed.
