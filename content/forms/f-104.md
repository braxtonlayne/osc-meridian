## General

1. Use this form to request any period of leave of half a working day or more. Leave of less than half a day is arranged with the supervisor of record and is not recorded on this form.
2. Submit the form at the workstation. A numbered receipt is issued on submission and the request is placed in the supervisor's queue. Keep the receipt number until the leave has been taken; the leave clerk will ask for it if the record is queried.
3. Leave is not to be taken until the request shows as approved. An approval in the queue has the same effect as a signed form.
4. Leave over a federal holiday is governed by the holiday minimum manning arrangements in :x[OSC-DIR-0189]. Requests for leave on a holiday on which minimum manning applies are confirmed separately by the division leave clerk, after the approval shows in the queue.

## Personnel in a probationary period

5. Probationers may request leave on this form in the ordinary way. Leave taken during probation does not extend the probationary period unless it exceeds ten working days in total, in which case the interim review dates are moved by the supervisor of record and the individual is told the new dates in writing. See :x[OSC-DIR-0247] and :x[OSC-PER-TRF].
6. Tick the box in block 5 if you are in a probationary period. Do not leave it blank because you are unsure; ask the supervisor of record.

## Completing the blocks

**Block 1.** Your personnel number and site are entered from your session. Check them. If the site shown is not your site of posting, stop and contact the MERIDIAN Service Desk; do not overwrite it.

**Block 2.** Choose one type of leave per form. A period that combines annual and unpaid leave needs two forms. "Sick (pre-arranged treatment)" covers appointments booked in advance only; unplanned sickness is reported by telephone to the site duty officer on the first morning and recorded by the clinic, not on this form. Count working days only. Half days are entered as 0.5.

**Block 2, surface leave.** Personnel leaving the facility for surface leave travel under the cover arrangements published by the site. Do not describe the facility, your post or your division in any arrangement made for the leave.

**Block 3.** Name the person who will cover your work, where there is one, and list any items held on charge (keys, tokens, files signed out to you) and any suspense that falls due while you are away. If no cover is needed, write "None required" and give the reason.

**Block 4.** "On site" means you will remain in berthing and may be recalled. "Off site" means the duty officer holds your contact arrangement. "Not contactable" requires the supervisor's agreement before the request is submitted.

**Block 5.** Your signature is applied by the workstation from your token. It certifies that the entries are correct and that you have arranged cover for your work.

**Block 6.** The supervisor of record is entered from your session. If you are temporarily supervised by someone else, enter the supervisor of record and say so in block 3.

## Routing

7. Supervisor of record; division leave clerk; Pay & Strength Accounting (PV/ALPHA-01) for the strength return.

## Processing time

8. The supervisor acts within three working days. Requests made with less than five working days' notice are taken at the supervisor's discretion and may be refused for that reason alone.
