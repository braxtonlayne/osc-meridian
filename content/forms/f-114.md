## When to report

An incident report is made for any irregular occurrence that affects the security of a facility, a record, a workstation, a consist or the transit network. :x[OSC-DIR-0378] gives examples. They include an unescorted person in a controlled area, a container found unlocked, a record found outside its custody, a token left in a reader, and an indication observed on a MERIDIAN display that does not agree with the working record.

Report on the day. A report made late is still made; give the reason for the delay in block 4.

A breach of a security rule by the reporting person is reported on :x[OSC-F-160], not on this form. Where the same occurrence involves both, make both reports and cross-refer them by receipt number.

## Completing the blocks

**Block 1.** Entered from the session. If the report is made on behalf of another person who has no workstation, enter your own number and name the other person in block 7.

**Block 2.** Give the time in Zulu. If the time is not known, give the earliest and latest times it could have been in block 4 and enter the earliest here.

**Block 3.** Be exact. For a facility, give site, level and room. For the transit network, give the segment or network and the block number as shown on the board (for example, a block on segment 01 is written 01-07). For an indication observed on a display, give the application and view, and the location the indication referred to.

**Block 4.** Describe what you saw or found, in order, without interpretation. Say what you did about it. Do not copy classified text from a record into this block; cite it by number in block 5.

**Block 5.** Give each record by its document number or accession number. Where a record is a numbered copy, give the copy number as it appears on the record, even if only part of it is legible.

**Block 6.** Consist identities are given as displayed, including any that are displayed only in part.

**Block 7.** Personnel numbers only.

## Classification of reports

A report takes the classification of the most highly classified matter it describes. The reporting person is read into the compartment in which the report is held, so that the report can be discussed with them, under :x[OSC-DIR-0412] para 4(c). The Resident Assurance Officer arranges the briefing.

:::redact{compartment=QUARRY auth="OIA" ref="OIA-07-116"}
A report that identifies a numbered Council copy by its copy number is referred by the Office of Internal Assurance to the Council Secretariat for comparison with the copy register. The Secretariat does not reply to the reporting person.
:::

## Routing

Resident Assurance Officer at the site of occurrence; OIA incident register at ALPHA-01; the reporting person's division head is told that a report has been made, but not its content.

## Processing time

Acknowledged within one duty period. Where an incident is opened, its number is given to the reporting person, who is then a witness in it.
