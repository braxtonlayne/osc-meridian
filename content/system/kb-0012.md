## Summary

Most documents in MERIDIAN are released to more than one tier. Rather than keep a separate copy for each tier, MERIDIAN holds one copy and withholds the parts a given reader is not cleared for. Withheld text is shown as a solid black bar. This article answers the questions the Service Desk is most often asked about those bars. None of them indicates a fault.

## 1. What does a black bar mean?

It means text is present in the document at that point and is withheld from you. The withholding is applied to the reader, not to the document: a colleague at a different tier, or holding a different compartment, may see the same paragraph with no bar.

A bar is never a printing defect, a missing page or a damaged file. Please do not open a ticket for a bar.

## 2. Why are some bars longer than others?

Bars are drawn to the length of the text they replace, in the font of the surrounding paragraph. A bar the length of a word replaces a word. A bar that runs for several lines replaces several lines.

Bar length is shown so that the layout of the document, its paragraph numbering and its page count are the same for every reader. This matters when two readers at different tiers discuss the same document by paragraph and page. It does not tell you what was withheld and should not be read as doing so.

## 3. What does the hover show?

Move the pointer over a bar, or move to it with the Tab key, and a tip appears after a short pause. It shows two things only:

- **Withheld**: the code of the withholding authority, the office under whose authority the text is withheld.
- **Ref**: the reference that authority has given, in that authority's reference format (below).

The tip never shows any part of the withheld text, its subject or its length in words. The withheld text is not on your workstation at all: it is not hidden by the display, it was never sent.

## 4. Who are the withholding authorities?

Withholding authorities are shown by division code. The codes in use, with the reference format each uses, are listed in the classification guide, :x[OSC-ASR-CG]. The most common in Records Library are:

| Code | Withholding authority | Reference usually given as |
|---|---|---|
| OIA | Office of Internal Assurance | Incident number `OIA-YY-NNN`, or contingency protocol `OIA-PROT-NN` |
| CS | Council Secretariat | `CS-YY-NNN` |
| PV | Personnel & Vetting | `PV-YY-NNN`, or a query number `PV-Q-YY-NNNN` |
| TO | Transit Operations | `TO-YY-NNN` |
| FE | Facilities Engineering | Work order `FE-YY-NNNN` |
| LR | Logistics & Reserves | Work order `LR-YY-NNNN` |
| CVO | Cover Operations | `CVO-YY-NNN` |
| COUNCIL | The Council | `CC-ART-` and the Charter article, or a resolution number |

Other divisions withhold within their own records using their division code and a serial, `CODE-YY-NNNN`.

The Service Desk is not a withholding authority and cannot release, explain or confirm withheld text.

## 5. How do I get withheld text released?

You do not request release from the Service Desk. Withheld text is released to a reader only by the withholding authority, or by the reader being briefed into the compartment concerned under :x[OSC-DIR-0412]. If you are briefed into a compartment, the passages withheld under it are displayed the next time you open the document. No action is needed on the workstation.

If you believe your work requires withheld material, raise it with your supervisor of record. Do not contact the withholding authority directly unless your supervisor asks you to.

## 6. Is examining a bar recorded?

Yes. Hovering over a bar, or focusing it with the keyboard, is written to your workstation audit trail like any other access. The entry records the document number, the withholding authority and the reference shown. It does not record anything you could not see.

This is routine. The audit trail records windows opened, searches, prints and the withheld items examined, so that the security office has a complete record of access for every operator, and examining a withholding mark is not of itself a violation. Operators who examine bars in quantity, for example by moving the pointer down a heavily withheld table, will find many entries in their trail and should not be concerned by them.

:::redact{tier=2 auth="OIA" ref="OIA-07-118"}
Supervisors of record may ask the Resident Assurance Officer for an extract of a subordinate's examinations of withheld items, by document and authority. Extracts are produced without reference to the withheld text and are not disclosed to the subordinate. A pattern of examinations confined to one authority or one reference is reported to the supervisor without request.
:::

You can read your own audit trail at any time from the Terminal with `audit tail` (see :x[KB-0026]).
