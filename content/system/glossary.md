## Use of this glossary

These are the standard meanings of terms used in OSC documents and in MERIDIAN. Where a document uses a term in a different sense it says so. Where a term is defined in a directive or memorandum, the definition here follows that issuance and the issuance governs.

Terms are listed alphabetically. Terms shown under **See also** are themselves defined in this glossary. Proposed additions and corrections are made to the Council Secretariat by the originating division; the glossary is reissued at the end of each month in which an entry changes.

## Figures in current use

Terms whose definitions state a programme figure carry the figure below. These are the figures to be used in all reporting.

| Term | Figure | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| SUSTAINED CAPACITY | Sustained capacity :c[headline.capacitySustained] persons | Full reserves, planned sustainment period. :x[OSC-MEM-07-0612] |
| SURGE BERTHING | Total surge berthing :c[headline.capacitySurge] berths | Sum of berths across all facilities. :x[OSC-MEM-07-0612] |
| CERTIFIED MILEAGE | Certified mileage :c[headline.tunnelCertifiedMi] miles | As certified to the Council. Not derived from, and not reconciled with, engineering mileage. |
| ENGINEERING MILEAGE | Rail :c[headline.railEngineeringMi] miles; tram :c[headline.tramEngineeringMi] miles | Segment and link survey. :x[OSC-TRN-ENG] |
