## Summary

This article is for operators logging in to MERIDIAN for the first time, or for the first time at a new workstation. It covers what is on the screen after login, how to find a document, how to tell what marking you are working at, and what to do when the workstation refuses you. It does not replace your division's induction or the briefings you have signed.

Read this article once, end to end. Most calls to the Service Desk from new operators in the first week are answered here.

## Before you start

- You need your operator ID and passphrase. New operators receive these on a temporary access card until a permanent smart token is issued. Do not write the passphrase anywhere else. If the card is lost, report it to your site security office the same day.
- The smart token stays in the reader for the whole session. Removing it locks the workstation at once.
- Your workstation has already been accredited and assigned to you. Do not move it, re-cable it, or swap keyboards with a neighbouring position. If equipment is faulty, call the Service Desk.

## The desktop

After login the desktop shows the workspace, the taskbar along the foot of the screen, and a classification banner at the top and bottom.

| Area | What it is |
|---|---|
| Workspace | Where application windows open. Icons on the workspace open the common applications. Right-click an icon for its other actions. |
| Directory | The menu at the left end of the taskbar. Every application and system tool is listed here. |
| Taskbar buttons | One button per open window. Click a button to bring its window forward; click again to minimise it. |
| Notification area | Link state to each hub, unread mail, print jobs held for you, and the session timer. Hover over an item for detail. |
| Clock | Zulu time with site-local time beside it. All MERIDIAN times are Zulu unless stated. |

Windows can be moved, resized, minimised, maximised and snapped to halves or quarters of the screen. Window positions are remembered between sessions. Everything can be done from the keyboard:

| Keys | Action |
|---|---|
| Alt+Home | Open or close the Directory menu |
| Ctrl+K | Search |
| Alt+R | Run (open a document by number) |
| Alt+F6 | Next window (Shift reverses) |
| Alt+F7 / Alt+F8 | Move / size the active window with the arrow keys; Enter to finish |
| Alt+F9 / Alt+F10 | Minimise / maximise the active window |
| Alt+Shift+arrow | Snap the active window to a half or quarter of the screen |
| Alt+Shift+C / Alt+Shift+T | Cascade / tile all windows |
| Alt+minus | Window menu |
| Alt+W | Close the active window |
| Alt+L | Lock the workstation |

On small field terminals MERIDIAN runs in handheld mode: one window at a time, with an application switcher at the foot of the screen. Content and markings are the same in both modes.

## The Directory menu

The Directory menu is the starting point for everything. Applications are listed by function, not by division:

- **Registry, Transit, Personnel, Records, Ledger, Reserves, Assurance** open the applications of those names. What each shows depends on your tier and compartments.
- **Directives** and **Forms** open the policy library and the forms catalogue.
- **Mail** opens Message Traffic.
- **Terminal** opens the command line. Documented commands are listed in :x[KB-0026].
- **System** holds this knowledge base, Settings, the System Monitor, the facility calendar, the glossary, and About This Terminal.
- **Run** (Alt+R) opens a document directly by its document number, for example `OSC-DIR-0412`.

## Finding documents

Press **Ctrl+K** from anywhere on the desktop to open search. Type a document number, a title word, a person's name or a form number. Results are grouped by type: documents, people, facilities, forms.

Search covers only what your tier and compartments allow you to read. The **clearance filter** at the foot of the results box is set to your own access and cannot be raised. Material above your access is not listed, and its absence from the results is not evidence that it does not exist.

Search terms are recorded with the audit trail in the same way as any other workstation activity.

Documents cite each other by document number. Underlined numbers are cross-references: click one to open the cited document in a new window. A cross-reference does not give you access to the document cited. If you are not cleared for it, the window opens as a refusal (see below).

## The classification banner and marking strips

The banner at the top and bottom of the screen shows the **highest marking among the windows you have open**. Open a document at a higher marking and the banner changes at once; close it and the banner returns.

Each window also carries its own marking strip under the title bar. Check the strip before you print, copy text or read aloud in a shared space. The markings, their colours and the material each covers are set out in the classification guide, :x[OSC-ASR-CG].

## Withheld text

Parts of a document you are permitted to open may still be withheld from you. Withheld text is shown as a black bar. Hovering over a bar, or moving to it with the keyboard, shows **only the withholding authority and its reference**. It never shows any part of the withheld text. Black bars are explained in full in :x[KB-0012].

## Refusals

When you open something you are not cleared for, MERIDIAN does not show an error. The window opens as a **refusal**: a panel that states what is required and the directive that requires it. Read the whole line. The examples below are taken from the 4.1.7 release.

| Refusal as displayed | What it means |
|---|---|
| Requires Tier 2 compartment briefing. Reference DIR-0412. | The document is held at a higher tier than yours. |
| Requires compartment briefing (KESTREL). Reference DIR-0412. | The document is held in a compartment you have not been read into. The compartment name is shown. |
| Not released to your access. Reference DIR-0412. | The document exists and is indexed, but no part of it is released at your tier or in any compartment you hold. |
| Distribution limited. Reference DIR-0412. | The document is issued to a named distribution and you are not on it. |

Every refusal cites :x[OSC-DIR-0412], *Compartment Briefing Requirement and Handling of Compartmented Records*. If you have not read it, read it now. It sets out how a person comes to be briefed into a compartment, and it is the directive your supervisor will expect you to have read before you open any compartmented file.

A refusal is not a fault and the Service Desk cannot override one. Do not call the Service Desk about a refusal; take it to your supervisor of record. Repeated attempts to open the same refused item are recorded.

## Locking and logging out

- Press **Alt+L** or remove your token to lock the workstation when you leave it, even briefly.
- The workstation locks itself after a period without input. The time remaining is shown in the notification area. Your windows and their contents are kept while locked; re-enter your passphrase to continue.
- Log out from the Directory menu at the end of your duty period. Do not leave a session locked overnight.

## Getting help

MERIDIAN Service Desk, ext. 1-7700, 0600Z to 1800Z (0200 to 1400 EDT). Outside those hours, the site duty officer holds the out-of-hours fault log and will pass workstation faults to the desk at the start of the next day.

When you call, have your operator ID and the workstation number ready. The workstation number is on the label on the front of the unit and in **System > About This Terminal**. Never read your passphrase to anyone, including the Service Desk. The desk will never ask for it.
