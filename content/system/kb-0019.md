## Summary

MERIDIAN does not print directly. Every print job is held on the print server until you release it in person at a secure release printer by presenting your badge. Every job prints behind a cover sheet in the colour of its marking. Printing is governed by :x[OSC-DIR-0319]; this article covers the workstation side only.

## Printing a document

1. Open the document. Check the marking strip under the window title.
2. Choose **Print** from the File menu of the document window.
3. The print dialog shows the document number, marking, page count and the release printers approved for that marking. Printers not approved for the marking are not listed.
4. Choose **Print**. The job is queued and given a job number in the form **PQ-NNNN**. The number is shown in the dialog, in the notification area, and on the cover sheet when it prints.
5. Go to the printer and present your badge at the reader. Held jobs for your badge are listed on the printer panel by PQ number. Select the job and release it.
6. Collect the output at once, cover sheet first. Do not leave a released job on the output tray.

One copy is printed per job. Printing more than one copy of a controlled document, or printing any part of a document onto removable media, requires an approved reproduction request on :x[OSC-F-142] before the job is sent.

## The four-hour purge

A job not released within **four hours** of being queued is purged from the print server. The purge is logged against your operator ID with the PQ number and document number. A purged job cannot be recovered; print it again.

Jobs queued during print server maintenance are held and released to the printer queue when the maintenance window ends. The four hours run from the end of the window, not from the time you sent the job.

## Cover sheets

The cover sheet is generated by the print server and prints first. It shows the marking in bands at the top and foot, the document number, title, revision, page count, PQ number, your operator ID, the release printer and the time of release, and the distribution limitation for the document. It cannot be suppressed.

Cover sheet stock is coloured by marking. Release printers draw from the tray loaded for the marking of the job.

| Marking | Cover sheet stock |
|---|---|
| :c[markings.TSSCI-OSC.label] | Slate grey |
| :c[markings.UMBRA-R.label] | Ochre |
| :c[markings.UMBRA-C.label] | Oxblood, white lettering |
| :c[markings.UMBRA-U.label] | Violet, white lettering |
| :c[markings.UMBRA-EO.label] | No workstation stock issued |

No cover sheet stock is issued to workstation printers for UMBRA//OSC EYES ONLY. Material bearing that marking is held in physical form only and is not printed from MERIDIAN.

The cover sheet stays attached to the printout for as long as the printout is outside a container. Return the cover sheet copy to your Records Division counter with the printout when it is destroyed.

## Withheld text in printouts

A printout shows exactly what your window shows. Withheld text prints as a black bar of the same length (see :x[KB-0012]). Printing a document does not release anything.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| Job not listed at the printer | Badge presented at a printer not approved for the marking, or the job has been purged | Check the PQ number in the notification area. If it is shown as PURGED, print again. Otherwise use a printer listed in the print dialog. |
| "Release suspended" at the printer panel | Annual security refresher overdue | Release is suspended while any refresher module is overdue. Complete the refresher; release resumes the next working day. The Service Desk cannot lift the suspension. |
| Printer panel shows the job but will not release | Badge not yet enrolled at that printer | New operators' badges are enrolled at their home printers within one working day of account issue. Call ext. 1-7700 if it is longer. |
| Cover sheet in the wrong colour | Tray loaded with the wrong stock | Do not use the printout. Return it to the Records Division counter for destruction, tell the counter staff which printer it came from, and print again at another printer. |
| Print dialog lists no printers | No release printer approved for that marking at your site | The document cannot be printed at your site. Read it on screen. |
| Two cover sheets with one printout | Job sent twice | Both jobs are logged. Return the duplicate to the Records Division counter. |

## Points to remember

- Every print is recorded in your audit trail with its PQ number, document number, printer and time of release.
- A printout is controlled in the same way as the original. Destroy it through Records Division, never in an office shredder or a burn bag you did not sign for.
- Do not release another operator's job for them, even at their request.
