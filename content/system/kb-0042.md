## Summary

The Network Control board in the Transit application is the dispatcher's picture of the rail and tram networks, repeated read-only to operators who have board access. It shows which blocks are occupied, which consist is in each, the scheduled headway on each line, and any possessions in force. This article explains what the indications mean. It is not a substitute for the working timetable, which remains the authority for what is booked to run.

You cannot change anything on the board. Nothing you do in the board view is seen by Network Control.

## Layout

Each rail segment and each tram link is drawn as a line divided into **blocks**. A block is a length of line bounded by signals that may be occupied by one consist at a time. Rail blocks are numbered by segment and position, for example `03-07` is the seventh block of segment 03. Tram blocks are numbered by network and link.

A solid line is in revenue service. A **dashed** line is complete but not in revenue service and has no paths in the timetable.

## Block indications

| Indication | Meaning |
|---|---|
| CLEAR | No consist detected in the block. |
| OCCUPIED | A consist is detected in the block. The consist ID is shown against the block at the head of the consist. |
| POSSESSED | The block is under possession by engineering (see below). A block under possession shows POSSESSED whether or not anything is detected in it. |

## Headway and speed

Each line shows the scheduled headway, the interval between successive consists over the same block in the same direction, as booked in the current working timetable. Speed is shown for each consist in motion. Where a line has no booked paths, or a consist is not running to a booked path, headway and speed are shown as `--`.

## Consist IDs

Every movement on the board carries a consist ID:

- **Rail**: `R` followed by the segment number of the consist's first booked run and a three-digit serial, for example `R01-214`. A rail consist keeps its ID when it works onto another segment, so an R01 consist on segment 05 is normal.
- **Tram**: `T` followed by the network letters (EA, SO, MW or CE) and a three-digit serial, for example `TEA-031`.

The register of consists and their vehicles is the rolling stock register, :x[OSC-TRN-RS]. Board viewers can look up a consist from the Terminal with `consist <id>`, for example `consist R01-214`. The lookup returns the vehicles, home depot and current booking. It is provided by Transit Operations and is not listed by `help` (see :x[KB-0026]).

### Suppressed consist IDs

A consist ID shown as R04-\*\*\* or similar is suppressed by Transit Control on instruction; no action is required of the viewer.

Suppression replaces the serial with asterisks for the duration of the movement. The segment prefix is left in place. A suppressed ID cannot be looked up and `consist` returns `NO RECORD` for it. Headway and speed for the movement are shown as `--`, and the timetable column shows `UNBOOKED`.

In movement tables a suppressed consist appears as `R04-***` (or the prefix of its segment followed by three asterisks). On the line diagram it is drawn as a hatched block labelled `########` in place of the consist ID, occupying the blocks the movement holds.

:::redact{compartment=KESTREL auth="TO" ref="TO-08-019"}
Suppression is applied to movements in the consist series reserved to Transit Control. Series reserved to Transit Control are not allocated to vehicles in the timetable and do not appear in the rolling stock register.
:::

## Possessions

A possession is the exclusive occupation of a block, segment or link by engineering for maintenance, inspection or testing. No revenue movement is permitted on possessed track. On the board the blocks concerned show POSSESSED and the possession number is shown against the line, in the form `POS 08-007`.

Possessions are granted by Transit Control with stated limits and times, and are listed in the possession register. The record for each possession shows the work, the party and the limits. Engineering movements inside a possession run under Transit Control's authority and are not booked in the timetable.

## Indications that are not faults

| What you see | Explanation |
|---|---|
| A dashed line with no paths | Complete, not in revenue service. Shown for completeness. |
| A block shows OCCUPIED briefly with no consist ID, then CLEAR | Track circuit test by the signalling maintainers. |
| `--` for headway on a whole line | No booked paths on that line in the current timetable. |
| An interval missing when the board is replayed | Network Control may withdraw an interval of indications from the record, for example after a signalling test. Withdrawn intervals are not replayed. |
| A gap in incident log serial numbers | Entries withdrawn by Transit Operations. The serial is not reused. |

None of these should be reported to the Service Desk. Network Control does not take calls from board viewers. Operators with a working question about a movement should raise it through their supervisor with Transit Operations.
