## Summary

Facilities Engineering (FE) and Logistics & Reserves (LR) work orders can be looked up from the Terminal without opening the maintenance management client. The lookup is provided by Facilities Engineering and is not listed by `help`. This article is published by the Service Desk at FE's request.

## Symptoms this article answers

- You have a work order number from a plant status page, a cable or a telephone call and want its current status.
- You are on shift without access to the maintenance management client.
- You are not FE or LR staff but have been given a work order number for a job affecting your area.

## Using the lookup

Open the Terminal (**Directory > Terminal**) and enter `wo` followed by the full work order number, including the division prefix and year:

```
> wo FE-07-0233
WORK ORDER    FE-07-0233
RAISED        02 FEB 2007 1312Z (0812 EST)  FE/ALPHA-03 SHIFT MAINT
TITLE         LUMINAIRES 5C-14 TO 5C-22 INTERMITTENT, CORRIDOR 5-C
LOCATION      ALPHA-03 LEVEL 5 CORRIDOR 5-C
PRIORITY      ROUTINE
STATUS        CLOSED 09 FEB 2007  BALLASTS REPLACED (9)
DISTRIBUTION  FE/ALPHA-03 SHIFT MAINT
              FE/ALPHA-03 ELECTRICAL
              ALPHA-03 LEVEL 5 BUILDING WARDEN
```

Work order numbers take the form `FE-YY-NNNN` or `LR-YY-NNNN`. The lookup does not accept partial numbers or titles. Use the Records Library search for those.

## Fields

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| RAISED | When and by which work centre the order was raised. |
| TITLE | The fault or task as entered by the raising work centre. |
| LOCATION | Site, level and space. Locations in plant spaces you are not permitted to see are withheld. |
| PRIORITY | ROUTINE, PRIORITY or ESSENTIAL BUS. ESSENTIAL BUS orders affect plant that must run when the facility is sealed. |
| STATUS | RAISED, ASSIGNED, PARTS HELD, IN PROGRESS, COMPLETE or CLOSED, with the date and a closing note where one was entered. |
| DISTRIBUTION | Work centres and workstations that receive a message when the status changes. |

Plant status pages print the numbers of open work orders against the plant beside the item affected, with the lookup in brackets. See for example the reactor status page :x[OSC-RSV-RX-A03].

## Note on distribution

Viewing a work order may add your workstation to its distribution.

Open orders at PRIORITY or ESSENTIAL BUS keep every workstation that looks them up on their distribution until they close, so that anyone who has needed the status of the job is told when it changes. Messages arrive in Message Traffic from the work order register. Closed orders are not affected.

If you have been added to a distribution in error, ask the raising work centre to remove you. The Service Desk cannot amend work order distributions.

## Resolution of common problems

| Symptom | Cause | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| `NO RECORD` | Number mistyped, or the order was raised before the register moved to MERIDIAN (orders before 2005) | Check the prefix and year. Older orders are held on paper by the raising work centre. |
| `UNKNOWN COMMAND` | Workstation profile issued before 4.1.4 | Call the Service Desk, ext. 1-7700, to have the profile refreshed. |
| LOCATION shows a black bar | The space is in a plant area withheld from your access | No action. The rest of the order is valid. |
