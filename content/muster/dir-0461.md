## Purpose

This directive sets how the staff posted to a facility are accounted for at a muster: who directs it, how badge reads at the assembly readers are counted, how sweep teams account for the rest, and how a muster is closed out and reported.

## Applicability

This directive applies at every staffed facility on the plant network. Sites not on the plant network muster under their own post orders and are not shown on the Muster Board.

## Definitions

**Posted staff** are the personnel posted to a facility on its strength return. Total posted staff for the programme is :c[headline.staffPosted]; total personnel on the tier strength is :c[headline.staffTotal].

Each person posted to a facility is **assigned** to one assembly point at that facility. The assignments at a facility sum to its posted staff, and each point's capacity exceeds its assignment.

Personnel carried as DETACHED / UNPOSTED (:c[headline.staffUnposted]) are not posted to a facility, hold no assembly point and are not mustered. A muster does not account for them and the board does not show them.

**Not on posted strength** describes a badge read at an assembly reader that is not held against the posted staff of the facility where it was read.

## Authority to direct a muster

A muster is initiated, sweep teams are tasked and the close-out is signed by the facility commander or deputy commander, or by the duty officer acting for them, at that facility only.

At a GAMMA site the duty watch holds the same authority for its own site.

Other operators with board access hold read access. They may look up a badge and print a close-out report. They do not direct a muster.

The Office of Internal Assurance reviews muster records and close-out reports. It does not initiate musters, task sweep teams or sign close-out.

## Initiation

One muster is open at a facility at a time. A new muster is initiated only after the current one is closed out.

A seal drill starts a muster at each site when the site acknowledges the drill order. The drill muster is closed out like any other.

The muster is initiated from the board by lifting the INITIATE cover and holding the switch. The muster alarm sounds on all levels until acknowledged at the board.

## Badge reads and directory display

The muster badge carries the intake year and serial of the holder's personnel number. The tier digit is not encoded on the card.

The board shows the directory entry of a badge holder only where the operator's access permits. Holders above the operator's tier are shown by badge number only. Tier 1 holders are not shown.

A read that is not on posted strength is recorded as read. It is not removed from the count and it is not reassigned. It is reported to the Badge and Pass Office and stated on the close-out report.

## Sweep teams

Staff who have not badged are accounted for by sweep teams tasked from the board to the area of an assembly point.

One team is tasked to an area per muster. A team reports the area clear when it has located everyone assigned to it who is on site.

The number of teams a site can field is shown on the board. A further area is tasked when a team reports clear.

## Report and close-out

The site reports to command at the report time shown on the board. At the report time, staff recorded on the duty roster as on leave or temporary duty are accounted for by the roster. The close-out is signed at or after the report time.

The close-out report states the assigned staff, those badged present, located by sweep and accounted by roster, those unaccounted for, visitors, and reads not on posted strength. Any difference is recorded as a discrepancy. A close-out with discrepancies is copied to the Office of Internal Assurance.

A muster is not stood down without a signed close-out.

## Network isolation

A facility's muster server is local to it. While the network is isolated the local board continues to operate; boards of other facilities show no data until the hub circuits are restored (:x[OSC-DIR-0447]).

## Exercise time

Exercise and drill musters are run on exercise time at the seal-drill time scale, so that a full muster and close-out can be practised within a watch. Times on the badge log are real Zulu times.
