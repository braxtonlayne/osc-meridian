## Summary

The Muster Board shows the muster at your site: how many staff are assigned to each assembly point, how many have badged at the assembly readers, how many have been located by sweep teams or accounted for by the duty roster, and how many are still unaccounted for. The procedure is :x[OSC-DIR-0461]. This article explains the board.

## Totals

The six displays at the top of the board read, left to right: ASSIGNED, PRESENT, SWEEP / ROSTER, UNACCOUNTED, VISITORS and NOT ON STRENGTH. Dashes mean no muster is open or on record. The EXERCISE CLOCK shows minutes since the muster alarm on exercise time.

ASSIGNED does not change during a muster. PRESENT counts staff badges read. Visitors badge with visitor cards and are counted separately.

## Annunciator

| Window | Lit when |
|---|---|
| MUSTER ALARM | A muster is open. Sounds the horn until ACK. |
| PERSONNEL UNACCOUNTED | Report time has passed and staff are still unaccounted for. |
| BADGE NOT ON STRENGTH | A reader has logged a badge that is not held against posted staff at this site. |
| REPORT DUE | Report time has passed and the close-out is not signed. |
| POINT OVER CAPACITY | More badges read at a point than its capacity. |
| SWEEP TEAMS OUT | A sweep team is deployed. |
| READER NOT IN SERVICE | A reader at this site is out of service. |
| SEAL DRILL MUSTER | The open muster was started by a seal drill. |
| HUB CIRCUIT DOWN | The board cannot reach the site's muster server. |

A new condition flashes. ACK makes it steady and silences the horn. A window that has cleared stays dim until RESET. LAMP TEST lights every window.

## Readers and the badge log

Select an assembly point and press Enter to see its readers and the badge log. Selecting a reader filters the log to that reader. Each line shows the Zulu time of the read, the exercise clock, the badge number and the directory entry where your access permits.

To check a badge, select its line and choose Look up badge. A read marked NOT ON POSTED STRENGTH stays on the log and in the count; do not attempt to correct it. It is reported automatically.

A reader marked NOT IN SERVICE is not used for assignment. Reads it logs are counted.

## Sweep teams

Turn the ASSEMBLY POINT selector to the point whose area is to be swept and press DISPATCH SWEEP. TEAMS OUT / HELD shows how many teams are deployed and how many the site holds. When the team reports clear, staff in that area who had not badged are shown as located.

## Close-out

At or after report time, open the close-out report, check the figures and sign. Differences are listed as discrepancies on the report. A close-out with discrepancies is copied to the Office of Internal Assurance. Print the report from the close-out view or with Ctrl+P.

If a control is refused, the board states why. Do not ask another operator to act for you.
