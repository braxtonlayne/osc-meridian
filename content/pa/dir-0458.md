## Purpose

This directive sets how the public address system is used at staffed facilities: who may originate a call, to which zones, with which recorded messages, and how calls, tests and automatic messages are recorded.

## Applicability

This directive applies to every console connected to a PAC-2 zone controller and to every person who operates one. It does not apply to the intercom stations at doors and airlocks, which are covered by the door controller instructions.

## Equipment

Each staffed facility has one zone controller. It holds the recorded message library, generates the tones and drives the amplifier racks. Each level of a facility is one zone. Assembly areas share the amplifier channel of their level. A site all-call keys every zone of the controller at once.

The controller numbers its zones from 01, so Level 0 is Zone 01. Zone numbers are engraved on the rack and printed in the zone table on each console.

The message library is described in :x[KB-0057]. Messages are recorded scripts. Consoles show the text of a message as it is played; nothing is spoken from the console.

## Consoles and zone assignment

A console originates calls only for its own site's controller. Calls to another site are not made from a console; they are passed to that site's command section by telephone or message.

The command section of a site may call any zone of its site and the site all-call. Facilities Engineering staff on environmental watch may call the zones of their own site except for life safety messages, which are made by the site superintendent or the duty supervisor.

A division console is assigned to its division zone only and carries routine messages only. An attempt to select another zone is refused by the console and reported to the operator's division.

A selective call to a level designated Tier 2 is carried, and the level controller is informed. Where practicable, such calls are routed through the level controller.

## Network all-call

The network all-call keys every site controller over the hub circuits. It is held at Network Control on key :c[apps.pa.networkKey.keyNo]. No other console is issued the key. A network all-call is not available while hub circuits are down.

## Priority and pre-emption

Every library message carries a priority from P1 (life safety) to P5 (test). A call on a free channel is carried at once. A call of higher priority pre-empts a call of lower priority on the same channel; the pre-empted call is not resumed and is recorded as PRE-EMPTED. A call that meets a call of equal or higher priority waits. A waiting call that has not been carried within :c[apps.pa.system.queueLapseSec] seconds lapses and is recorded as LAPSED.

A call may be cancelled by its originator, or at its own site by the command section. Automatic calls cannot be cancelled from a console.

Life safety calls require confirmation at the console before they are keyed.

## Automatic messages

The controller sends the following messages itself and records them as automatic:

| Message | Sent when |
|---|---|
| 31 Seal drill, doors closing | the site receives a seal drill order |
| 32 Seal drill, site sealed | the site reports sealed |
| 33 Seal drill, stand down | the drill is stood down (network) |
| 34 Seal drill, cancelled | the drill is aborted (network) |
| 35 Inter-site communications interrupted | network isolation is initiated (each site, local controller) |
| 36 Inter-site communications restored | hub circuits are restored |

While a seal drill is in progress at a site, routine calls and tone tests are suspended there.

## Tone tests

The weekly tone test is made :c[apps.pa.testWindow.label]. A zone test keys message 90 on the selected zones. Tones heard in a zone outside the test window are taken by personnel in that zone to be a live call, and a test made outside the window is reported to the operator's command section.

A monitor test plays the tones on the console loudspeaker only and may be made at any time.

## Records

The controller keeps a log of every call, test and automatic message for :c[apps.pa.system.logRetentionDays] days. Calls are also recorded in the workstation audit trail of the originating console. Log extracts are issued by Communications Security on request. Console displays are not printed.

The Office of Internal Assurance reviews the log and does not originate calls.
