## Summary

The public address console sends recorded messages to the zones of your site. You select the zones, select a message from the library, and press **BROADCAST**. The console shows the text of the message while the controller plays it. Nothing is spoken from the console.

Your console only offers the zones and messages your post may use. Anything else is refused at the console with the reason.

Messages 01 and 02 are read twice. Messages 14 and 15 are not used at every site. The console shows the full text of each message before you send it.

## Tones

| Tone | Heard | Used with |
|---|---|---|
| Chime | three falling notes | most messages, and the weekly test |
| Alert | alternating high and low tone | smoke, filtered mode, communications interrupted |
| Muster | longer alternating tone | message 01 |
| Seal | chime, then alternating tone | message 31 |
| All clear | two chimes | stand down, restored, end of the weekly test |

## Tone tests

The weekly tone test is made :c[apps.pa.testWindow.label]. Turn **MODE** to **ZONE**, select the zones and press **TEST**. A test made outside the window is heard by everyone in the zone and is treated by them as a live call, so the console asks you to confirm it, and it is reported.

To check the tones without keying a zone, turn **MODE** to **MONITOR**, choose the tone with **TONE** and press **TEST**. Only the console loudspeaker sounds. You may do this at any time.

## Lamps

| Lamp | Lit means |
|---|---|
| ON AIR | a call is being carried on a zone of this site |
| QUEUED | a call is waiting for its zone |
| HUB CCTS | communications with the rest of the network are up |
| DRILL | a seal drill is in progress at this site; routine calls and tests are suspended |
| TEST WINDOW | the weekly test window is open |

## If a call is refused

The console gives the reason and a reference. Do not try another zone to get round a refusal. If a call has to be made that your console will not make, ask the duty supervisor.
