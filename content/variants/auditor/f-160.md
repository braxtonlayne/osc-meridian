## Purpose

A member of the Office who has broken a security, records or workstation rule reports it on this form. The duty to report one's own breach is in :x[OSC-DIR-0378]. A self-report is considered in mitigation in any inquiry or disciplinary proceeding that follows. A breach that comes to notice in another way, and that the person could have reported, is not.

An occurrence that is not the reporting person's own breach is reported on :x[OSC-F-114].

## When to report

As soon as you know. A self-report made after the Office of Internal Assurance has contacted you about the same matter is still recorded, but is given less weight.

If you are unsure whether what you did was a breach, report it. The Resident Assurance Officer will tell you if no breach occurred.

## Completing the blocks

**Block 1.** Entered from the session.

**Block 2.** Choose the description that fits best. Give the date and time of the breach, not of the report. Where the breach continued over a period, give the start in block 2 and the end in block 4.

**Block 3.** Give the record by its document number. Where the record has no document number, give its accession number. Where more than one record is involved, give the most highly classified here and the others in block 4.

**Block 4.** Say what you did, in order, and how it came about. Say how you came to realise it was a breach. Say what you have done since, for example returning the record, securing the container, or locking the session. Do not repeat classified content.

**Block 5.** Signature from token.

## What happens next

The Resident Assurance Officer acknowledges the report and decides whether it is closed with advice, added to an open incident, or made the subject of an inquiry. Where it is added to an open incident, the reporting person is read into the compartment in which the incident is held and may be interviewed.

:::redact{compartment=BRIAR auth="OIA" ref="OIA-PROT-15"}
The disposition of a self-report is recorded on the inquiry file. Where the inquiry is held under an assurance contingency, the disposition is not notified to the reporting person, and the self-report is retained for as long as the contingency file is open.
:::

## Routing

Resident Assurance Officer; OIA incident register at ALPHA-01. The reporting person's division head is not told of a self-report unless an inquiry is opened.

## Processing time

Acknowledged within one duty period.

## Assessor's sheet (OIA use only)

Kept with the Inquiries Section copy of the form. Not issued to reporting persons.

|---|---|---|

| Made after another report, before notice of an inquiry | Ordinary | |

| Names a document and copy number | Raises | Copy numbers are checked against the register |
| Names another person | Neither | The other person is not told of the report |
