## Purpose

This directive requires the reporting of security incidents and other irregular occurrences, provides for the self-report of a person's own violation, and governs the conduct and disposition of inquiries by the Office of Internal Assurance.

## Applicability

This directive applies to all OSC personnel and to contract personnel with unescorted access, at all sites, at all hours. It applies on and off duty where the occurrence concerns OSC information, facilities or personnel.

## Policy

Every irregular occurrence is reported. It is for the Office of Internal Assurance, and not the person who observed it, to decide whether the occurrence is significant, already known, or explained.

Reporting an occurrence is never itself a violation, whatever the report concerns and whatever the reporter's tier.

A person who reports their own violation promptly is treated differently from a person whose violation is found by others.

## Reporting of irregular occurrences

Any irregular occurrence, including an indication observed on a MERIDIAN display, is reported on :x[OSC-F-114] before the end of the duty period in which it was observed. Examples are a container found open, a record found out of place, an unexplained entry in a log, a person in an area not on the access roster, and a display showing a state, movement or record that does not correspond to the published position.

The report states what was seen, where, and at what time, in Zulu with local in brackets. It does not state conclusions. Where the occurrence concerns a document, the document number and any copy number are given.

A report is made even where the reporter believes the occurrence is already known, has already been reported by someone else, or has an ordinary explanation.

Where the occurrence concerns material in a compartment the reporter does not hold, the report is classified in that compartment on receipt and the reporter is read in under :x[OSC-DIR-0412] para 4(c). The reporter is not required to hold the compartment before reporting.

After reporting, the reporter does not investigate further, does not return to the place or record to check, and does not discuss the occurrence with anyone other than the Resident Assurance Officer and their own supervisor.

## Self-reporting

A person who has committed, or believes they may have committed, a security violation reports it on :x[OSC-F-160]. Examples are opening a record outside one's duties, leaving a container unsecured, printing or retaining material without authority, and failing to report an occurrence under para 4.

A self-report is considered in mitigation. A self-report made before the matter is reported by anyone else, or found on review of the audit trail, carries the greatest weight. A self-report made after the person has been notified of an inquiry, or after the person's access to the matter has been recorded under an open inquiry, is not considered in mitigation.

Self-reports are not acknowledged by return mail as a matter of course. The receipt number issued by the workstation is the record of the report.

## Inquiries

An inquiry is opened by the Office of Internal Assurance under a number in the OIA series. The inquiry officer is the action officer named on the incident.

An inquiry is held in the compartment of the material it concerns, or in more than one. Persons interviewed in an inquiry are read into the compartment in which the inquiry is held, under :x[OSC-DIR-0412] para 4(d), for the purpose of the interview.

Persons interviewed answer fully, do not discuss the interview afterwards, and do not make notes of it outside the inquiry file.

Records relevant to an inquiry may be held on the library index pending its close, so that access to them can be recorded. Records Division places the hold on accessions under :x[OSC-DIR-0336].

## Dispositions

The inquiry officer recommends a disposition and the Director, Office of Internal Assurance, decides it. The subject's division head is notified of the disposition. The subject is notified where the disposition requires action of them.

:::redact{compartment=BRIAR auth="OIA" ref="OIA-PROT-15"}
Dispositions are recorded in one of the following forms:

1. **Closed, no further action.** The file is retained by the Office of Internal Assurance. No entry is made on the personnel record.
2. **Closed, administrative action.** Written advice, refresher training, withdrawal of print release or removable media privileges for a stated period. Entered on the personnel record.
3. **Closed, withdrawal of access.** Debriefing from one or more compartments. The subject is not told which compartment occasioned the withdrawal.
4. **Closed, change of category.** The subject is removed from the posted strength of their site and carried in personnel accounting under a category other than POSTED. The inquiry file records the category; the strength return does not record the inquiry. The subject's number is not reissued.

Dispositions in forms 3 and 4 are taken by the Director personally and are not delegated, except that dispositions arising from inquiries into Council records are taken by the Secretary to the Council, and the Director is informed of the disposition.

## Responsibilities

The Director, Office of Internal Assurance, is the office of primary responsibility, opens and closes inquiries and decides dispositions.

Resident Assurance Officers receive reports and self-reports at their site, assign the incident number and action officer, and conduct inquiries at their site unless the Director directs otherwise. They review the workstation audit trail of their site weekly (:x[OSC-ASR-PSEC]).

Supervisors ensure that persons under their supervision know how to report, and do not discourage, delay or screen reports.

Every person reports under para 4 and para 5 without waiting for instruction.

## Procedures

To report an occurrence:

1. Note the time (Zulu and local), the place and what was seen. Do not copy or photograph anything.
2. Complete :x[OSC-F-114] at the workstation. Give document numbers, consist or equipment identities, and copy numbers where seen.
3. Submit before the end of the duty period. Keep the receipt number.
4. If the occurrence is continuing, telephone the Resident Assurance Officer as well as reporting.

To self-report, complete :x[OSC-F-160] and submit it. Do not wait to be asked.
