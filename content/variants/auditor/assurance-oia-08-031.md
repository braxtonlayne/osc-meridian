## Finding note F-031-1

H. L. Vance, Assurance Auditor, 19 MAY 2008.

1. The access log in the site copy of this record begins on 05 MAY 2008. The audit archive retrieved under SD-08-1187 holds three earlier lines, entered above. The worklog entry of 14 MAY 2008 records the first opening only.
2. The line of 02 APR 2008 is from a workstation in the Council Secretariat's group at ALPHA-01. The operator is withheld at origin. The same opening changed the item's index marking. The marking was corrected again by Records Division on 07 MAY 2008.
3. The media in the RAO safe hold five images. Sheets 1, 2, 3 and 31 are on the index. The fifth image, sheet 19 of 31, was not loaded to the index on accession and has not been examined. It is not examined in this review pending the Secretariat's reply.
4. The holder of P-4-07-0219 left the strength return of ALPHA-03 with effect from 28 MAR 2008, the day after the openings at line 1 and 2. That is recorded here as a date, not as a finding.

Recommendation: the access log in the site copy to be brought into line with the archive at the next weekly review. Referred to the Director.
