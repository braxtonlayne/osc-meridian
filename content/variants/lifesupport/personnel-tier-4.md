## Where to go

All personnel questions go to the command section administrative office at your facility. Do not write to divisions directly.

## Your duties

Your supervisor gives you your post orders and your shift. Shifts are posted on the command section board on the Thursday before the rota week. Shift changes are agreed with your supervisor and written on the board, not arranged between yourselves.

You may hold the compartment of your work area. You will be briefed in person and asked to sign. If you are moved to a different work area at the same facility you may be briefed again.

## If something is wrong

If you see something that is not right (a door that does not seal, a badge left unattended, a person you do not recognise in a controlled area) tell your supervisor the same shift. If your supervisor is not available, tell the command section. You will not be in trouble for reporting.

Minimum manning applies on public holidays. Check the board.
