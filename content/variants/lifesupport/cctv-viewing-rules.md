## Purpose

1. This extract gives the parts of the directive that apply to a Tier 4 operator holding the Facility Video client. It sets which cameras the operator may view and how cameras, recorded video and video stills are handled at the workstation. Paragraphs that do not apply at Tier 4 are omitted.

## Ownership

2. The cameras and recorders at the site belong to the site security office. The security control room has first call on every camera. A workstation viewer watches the security office's cameras with its permission and gives way to the control room without argument.

3. Security video is not a record of the viewer's section. It is not copied, recorded or described in a section record except as allowed in para 7.

## Display of video

4. Video is displayed only on the workstation screen. It is not shown to a person not entitled to view it, whether by turning the screen or by any other means.

5. A Tier 4 operator may view the cameras of the site to which the account is posted, and no others. A camera viewing a level held above Tier 4 is not displayed, at the operator's own site as anywhere else.

6. A request the rules do not allow is refused by the client and the refusal is recorded. Repeated refusals are reviewed by the Office of Internal Assurance.

## Control of cameras

7. PTZ cameras at the operator's own site may be moved from the workstation, only to a stored preset or in small steps. Moves are recorded. A camera left off its home preset returns there on its own. Video stills printed from the client are controlled records: each is numbered, its printing is notified to the site security office, and it is handed to the section registry on the day it is printed.

## Recording interruptions

8. A recorder that stops recording on any channel enters the interval in its event list. The interval shows as NO RECORDED VIDEO in playback. An operator who notices an interruption reports it to the site security office on the day, with the channel and the times.

## Camera schedule

9. Every recorder channel in use has a line in the site camera schedule, held by the site security office and loaded into the client. A channel without a line in the schedule is a spare and carries no picture. A picture on a spare channel is reported to the site security office.
