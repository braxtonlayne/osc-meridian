## When to report

Use this form when something at the site is not right and you think someone should know: a door that does not seal or does not show its true state, a badge left unattended, a person you do not recognise in a controlled area, a log entry you did not make, or a panel reading that does not match what you see at the plant.

Report the same shift. A late report is still made; give the reason in block 4.

If you have broken a rule yourself, tell your supervisor. Your supervisor will tell you whether a second form is needed.

## Completing the blocks

**Block 1.** Entered from the session.

**Block 2.** The time, in Zulu. The clock on the duty officer's desk shows Zulu and site time.

**Block 3.** Level and room, or the door, camera or plant item by its tag (for example G08-BD3, AHU-2).

**Block 4.** What you saw, in order, and what you did. Do not say what you think it means.

**Block 5.** Any log or record by its number, for example the door log page.

**Block 6.** Leave blank unless the report is about the tram platform.

**Block 7.** Personnel numbers only.

## What happens next

The site duty officer receives the report, tells you it has been received, and sends it on. You will be told if anyone needs to speak to you. You will not be in trouble for reporting.

Do not discuss the report with anyone except the duty officer and your supervisor.

## Routing

Site duty officer; the Office of Internal Assurance, by the duty officer; your supervisor is told that you made a report.
