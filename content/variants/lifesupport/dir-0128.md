*Extract for life support technicians. Paragraphs that apply only to other classes of facility, and Annexes A and B, are not reproduced. The site annex at the end is issued by the site.*

## Purpose

This directive sets the replacement cycles for particulate pre-filters, final filters and gas-phase beds in air handling units, when a filter bank may be opened, and the notice given to the people in the zones affected.

## Policy

| Filter stage | Standard cycle | Change earlier at |
|---|---|---|
| Particulate pre-filter | 90 days | 1.0 in. w.g. differential pressure |
| Final (high-efficiency) filter | 24 months | 2.0 in. w.g. differential pressure |
| Gas-phase bed | 180 days | Failed breakthrough sample |

Units serving seal-capable zones are on shortened cycles: particulate pre-filters every 60 days and gas-phase beds every 120 days. Every zone at this site is seal-capable.

No filter bank is opened while the site is SEALED or SEALED (DRILL). A change falling due then is made on the first working day after, and the deferral is written in the filter change log.

While the site is CONDITIONED, a bank is opened only with the duty officer's approval, entered in the duty log with the unit and the time of isolation.

No zone loses more than one unit at a time. Where a zone has one unit, the interruption is not more than 60 minutes for a particulate change and 4 hours for a gas-phase change.

Spent gas-phase media are bagged at the unit, labelled with the unit and the date, and handled as controlled waste.

## Procedures

People in each zone affected get not less than seventy-two hours' notice, by MERIDIAN mail and on the board at the entry level.

1. Confirm the seal state of every zone the unit serves.
2. Isolate the unit at the local panel and attach the isolation tag.
3. Record the differential pressure across the bank before removal.
4. Remove the spent filters or media. Bag and label media at the unit.
5. Fit replacements from current stock and record the lot numbers.
6. Restore the unit, record the new differential pressure and remove the tag.

A gas-phase bed is sampled for breakthrough at the midpoint of its cycle. A failed sample brings the change forward to the next working day.

The filter change log is kept for the life of the unit.
