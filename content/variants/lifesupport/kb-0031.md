## Summary

Work orders raised at your site can be looked up from the Terminal without opening the maintenance management client. This extract is issued to Tier 4 workstation profiles. It covers your own site's register only.

## Using the lookup

Open the Terminal (**Directory > Terminal**) and enter `wo` followed by the full work order number, including the division prefix and year, for example `wo FE-08-0112`.

Work order numbers take the form `FE-YY-NNNN` or `LR-YY-NNNN`. The lookup does not accept partial numbers or titles.

## Fields

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| WORK ORDER | The number and its status: OPEN or CLOSED. |
| TITLE | The fault or task as entered by the raising work centre. |
| LOCATION | Level and space at your site. Spaces you are not permitted to see are withheld. |
| RAISED BY | The work centre that raised the order. |
| CLOSED | Date of closure, for closed orders only. |
| DISTRIBUTION | Work centres and workstations that receive a message when the status changes. |

## Note on distribution

Viewing an open work order against plant that must run when the site is sealed adds your workstation to its distribution until the order closes. Messages arrive in Message Traffic from the work order register.

If you have been added to a distribution in error, ask the raising work centre to remove you. The Service Desk cannot amend work order distributions.

## Resolution of common problems

| Symptom | Cause | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| `no record of ...` | Number mistyped, or the order is not on your site's register | Check the prefix and year. Orders held at another site are not displayed on Tier 4 profiles; ask your site superintendent. |
| LOCATION shows a black bar | The space is withheld from your access | No action. The rest of the order is valid. |
