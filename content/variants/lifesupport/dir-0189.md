## Purpose

This directive sets the rules for requesting and approving annual and sick leave, publishes the federal holidays observed in 2008, and fixes the minimum manning to be held at each facility on those holidays.

## Applicability

This directive applies to all OSC personnel at every staffed facility.

Cover facility staff observe the holidays and leave rules of the cover agency. Where a cover facility closes for a holiday, the arrangements for access to the facility below it are made under paragraph 5.5.

## Policy

Annual leave is requested on :x[OSC-F-104] and approved by the immediate supervisor, except as provided in paragraph 3.2. No person approves their own leave.

Leave for personnel within a probationary period is approved by the head of the receiving division, not by the immediate supervisor, as set out in :x[OSC-DIR-0247].

Leave is not approved where it would bring a section below the manning set for it in the duty roster. Where two requests compete, the earlier request is approved.

Personnel on leave remain subject to recall. Recall is issued by the duty officer on the authority of the Facility Commander or the Council, and overrides leave without notice. A person on leave who is recalled reports to their facility by the quickest available means.

Personnel on leave may travel freely within the sustainment radius of their facility. Travel beyond it, and any travel outside the United States, is notified to the facility security officer before the leave begins, with dates and an address.

Sick leave is reported by telephone to the immediate supervisor before the start of the shift. Sick leave of more than three consecutive working days is supported by a note from the site medical officer or a physician, which is passed to the site medical officer.

On a federal holiday each facility is held at minimum manning: the lowest staffing at which it can keep its life support, power and security watches. Every other post closes for the day. Minimum manning is not reduced by leave.

The federal holidays observed in 2008 are:

| Holiday | Date | Manning |
|---|---|---|
| New Year's Day | 01 JAN 2008 | Minimum |
| Birthday of Martin Luther King, Jr. | 21 JAN 2008 | Minimum |
| Washington's Birthday | 18 FEB 2008 | Minimum |
| Memorial Day | 26 MAY 2008 | Minimum |
| Independence Day | 04 JUL 2008 | Minimum |
| Labor Day | 01 SEP 2008 | Minimum |
| Columbus Day | 13 OCT 2008 | Minimum |
| Veterans Day | 11 NOV 2008 | Minimum |
| Thanksgiving Day | 27 NOV 2008 | Minimum |
| Christmas Day | 25 DEC 2008 | Minimum |

The holiday runs from 0000 to 2359 site-local time on the date shown. Watches spanning midnight are manned at the holiday level for the whole watch.

Personnel who work on a holiday are given a day off in lieu within the following sixty days, to be agreed with their supervisor. Days in lieu not taken within sixty days lapse.

## Responsibilities

The Director, Personnel & Vetting, owns this directive and publishes the holiday calendar each January.

The Facility Commander sets the minimum manning for the facility by watch and approves the holiday manning roster.

Division heads prepare the holiday manning roster for their division and approve leave for probationary personnel posted to them.

Supervisors approve leave, keep the leave record for their section, and report sick leave to the command section on the day it begins.

Duty officers issue recalls and post the duty officer contact for each holiday at the operations desk.

## Procedures

Leave of one to five days is requested not less than five working days in advance. Leave of more than five days is requested not less than twenty working days in advance. The approved copy of :x[OSC-F-104] is returned to the requester and a copy is kept by the supervisor.

Leave that has been approved may be cancelled by the approving officer for operational need. The reason is given in writing, and the leave is restored to the requester's balance.

Leave records are reconciled to the pay run each month and reported to Personnel & Vetting with the strength return under :x[OSC-DIR-0233].

The holiday manning roster for each holiday is posted at the operations desk and on MERIDIAN not less than ten working days before the holiday. Personnel named on it confirm to their supervisor that they have seen it.

Where the cover facility is closed for a holiday, personnel on the holiday roster enter and leave by the route set by the facility security officer for that day, and do not use the cover facility's public entrance.

The dining room keeps holiday hours under :x[OSC-DIR-0141] and the duty officer contact is posted in each dining room.
