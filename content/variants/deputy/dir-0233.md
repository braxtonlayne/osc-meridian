## Purpose

This directive is the governing payroll issuance for personnel accounting. It defines the pay statuses, sets the form and timetable of the monthly strength return, governs the reconciliation of each pay run to the strength returns, and sets the procedure for personnel queries.

## Applicability

This directive applies to Personnel & Vetting, to the Comptroller in respect of payroll disbursement, to the command section of every staffed facility, and to every division head.

It applies to every person who holds a personnel number, whatever their tier, posting or status. Strength at Tier 1 is accounted by the Council Secretariat and enters the reconciliation as a single line without breakdown.

## Policy

Every person who holds a personnel number is in exactly one pay status at the close of each pay period.

The pay run is the authority for tier strength. The strength returns are the authority for posted strength. Neither is amended to agree with the other.

A personnel number is issued once and is not reissued.

## Status POSTED

A person is POSTED when they are entered on the strength return of a facility by its command section. POSTED includes personnel on leave, in a probationary period, and on temporary duty of thirty days or less.

## The Monthly Strength Return

Each facility command section renders a strength return as at 2359Z on the last day of the month. The return lists personnel numbers by tier and by function line of the standard ratio model. It does not list names. It is signed by the Facility Commander or the Deputy Commander.

## Categories other than POSTED

6.1. **DETACHED.** A person is DETACHED when they are released from the strength return of their facility for duty that is not entered on any strength return, on the instruction of the Director, Personnel & Vetting. The person is carried centrally and paid on the run at their grade.

6.2. **UNPOSTED.** A person is UNPOSTED when their posting ends and they are not posted elsewhere, pending :r[a decision of the Director under the inquiry procedure of the Office of Internal Assurance]{tier=2 auth="PV" ref="PV-I-05-007"}.

6.3. **Entry.** Entry to either category is by declaration on :x[OSC-F-131], made by the command section of the facility of last posting, on the instruction of the Deputy Director, Personnel & Vetting, and forwarded to the Deputy Director by hand of officer. The declaration states the effective date given in the instruction. It is not sent to Pay & Strength Accounting.

6.4. **Exit.** A person leaves either category by posting order or by record of separation.

6.5. **Reporting.** Both categories are reported in the strength summary as one line, without breakdown by site, division or tier. Enquiries about a named individual in either category are not answered.

## Pay Run Reconciliation

Pay & Strength Accounting reconciles each pay run against the strength returns for the same period, by personnel number, within five working days of the pay run. A difference accounted for by a pay status entered in the pay run requires no reconciliation action.

## Personnel Queries

A personnel query is raised by memorandum to the Deputy Director, Personnel & Vetting, and is answered or closed within ten working days of receipt.

## Procedures

Changes of pay status are entered by Personnel & Vetting only, on the authority of a posting order, a record of separation, or a declaration under paragraph 6.3.
