1\. In response to reference (a), the engineering lengths below are provided for the FY2008 transit cost tables. Lengths are surveyed route-miles at track centreline as closed out under reference (b).

2\. Rail segments. Lengths are given for all six segments, irrespective of revenue status. Segment 04 is included at its surveyed length (reference (c)).

::canon-table{source="railSegments" cols="no:Seg,a:From,b:To,miles:Route-miles,status:Status"}

Rail, six segments: :c[headline.railEngineeringMi] route-miles.

3\. Regional tram networks: :c[headline.tramEngineeringMi] route-miles, being the stated totals of the four network sheets.

Engineering total, rail and tram: :c[anomalies.TUNNEL_847.figures.engineeringTotalMi] route-miles.

4\. The certified mileage (:c[headline.tunnelCertifiedMi] miles) reported to the Council by Transit Operations remains as returned and is not affected by this memorandum. For the Comptroller's information, it is the sum of the five tram links that had a witnessed acceptance run before the first cut-off of the return: ALPHA-01 to BETA-02, BETA-02 to GAMMA-03, BETA-02 to GAMMA-04, BETA-10 to GAMMA-07 and ALPHA-05 to GAMMA-09. It has not been amended at any later quarter. No inter-hub rail segment is included in it, whether or not in revenue service. Engineering lengths are not to be substituted for it in Council material.

5\. Portal environmental notes. Portal blocks at both ends of every segment are logged for CO2, temperature and relative humidity on the FE walk-down sheets and are not entered in the length tables. Segment 04 portal seals (ALPHA-02 portal and ALPHA-05 platform 7) are walked down monthly under possession booked by FE; readings during a possession are recorded against the possession number, not against a consist. Segment 04 ventilation runs on the possession schedule only. No change to the Segment 04 length results from the walk-downs.

6\. Lengths are stated to the nearest whole mile and are not to be re-derived from schedule times or design speeds. Queries on the lengths go to the FE action officer, O. Garrity, Tunnel Structures Engineer, extension 2-3318. Queries on the certified figure go to Transit Operations. Suspense for the FY2008 cost tables is 30 NOV 2007, per reference (a).
