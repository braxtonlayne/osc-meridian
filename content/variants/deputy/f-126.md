## Certifying officer's guidance

Issued by the OIA compartment registry to deputy commanders, 01 APR 2008, with edition 04/2008 of the form. Not issued to applicants.

1. A request reaches your approvals queue when the applicant's facility names you as certifying officer: on posting to your facility (:x[OSC-DIR-0395] para 3(b)), or where the registry has routed another facility's requests to you. The registry does not tell the applicant who the certifying officer is.
2. You see blocks 1 to 4 and the sponsor's acknowledgement. You do not see the compartmented records cited in block 4 unless you hold the compartment. You are not required to hold it to certify.
3. Approve where the sponsor holds the compartment, the sponsor has acknowledged, and block 4 cites a record within the compartment's scope. Deny where any of these fails. Do not refer a request back to the applicant for more detail.
4. A request for KESTREL whose justification cites a segment sheet, a possession record or a commissioning file for a segment not in revenue service is decided by you on the same terms as any other. It is not referred to Transit Operations.
5. A request for ACTING-T2 from a person in a probationary period is approved only on your certification of operational necessity, entered in the decision note. A supervisor's sponsorship alone is not enough.
6. Your decision is written to the applicant's mail at their next sign-in, in the registry's name and not in yours.

## The compartments

| Compartment | Scope | Read in on request |
|---|---|---|
| KESTREL | Transit engineering | Yes |
| HOLLOW | Personnel carried without a posting | No |
| SEXTANT | Network geography beyond the holder's site | Yes |
| TALLOW | Reserves positions and reactor operations | No |
| BRIAR | Assurance contingencies and inquiry files | No |
| QUARRY | Council and governance records | No |

## Routing

Sponsor (acknowledgement at the workstation); OIA compartment registry at ALPHA-01; certifying officer (approvals queue); supervisor of record (notified of the outcome).
