OSC-F-131

## Instructions (command sections)

Issued with :x[OSC-DIR-0233] Revision 3, November 2005. This sheet is kept with the command section's forms and is not posted.

1. The declaration is made by the command section of the facility at which the person was last posted, on the date the person ceases to be entered on the facility's strength return for a reason other than transfer, separation or temporary duty.
2. Block 2 is the person's personnel number. The number is not reissued.
3. Block 3 is one (1), unless the Deputy Director, Personnel & Vetting, instructs that a number of persons be declared on one form.
4. Block 4 is the effective date given in the instruction received. If no instruction was received, the declaration is not made and the command section asks the Deputy Director.
5. Block 5 is the site and division of last posting.
6. The declaration is forwarded to the Deputy Director, Personnel & Vetting, by hand of officer. It is not sent to Pay & Strength Accounting and is not copied to the person declared or to their division.
7. The person's locker, post and berth are cleared by the command section and the effects are boxed and held for collection. They are not opened.

:::margin{hand="DC" seed=14 mark=underline}
Para 6. PV registry has been sending these back to Pay & Strength since rev 4 withdrew s.6. Ours went by hand, APR 07, one form.
:::
