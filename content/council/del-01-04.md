This record is made under authority delegated by the Council (:x[OSC-CNL-CHR-III]). Under the terms of the delegation the names of those present are not recorded, the time and place are not recorded, and no clerk attends. The record states the number of seats present and the business concluded. It is kept on paper, in one copy (:x[OSC-CNL-CHR-VII]).

## Item 1. Record of No. 3 of 2001

Agreed. The record of No. 3 of 2001 was read and returned to the Secretary's register.

## Item 2. Phase 1 objectives: review

The objectives of :c[phases.1.title] assigned to this meeting were reviewed. See :x[OSC-PER-PH1] for the objectives as published to the Office.

| Objective | Description | Status |
|---|---|---|
| P1-1 | Constitution of the Council and issue of the distribution copies | Met |
| P1-2 | Construction authority and first excavation contracts | Met |
| P1-3 | Site acquisition, first five sites | In hand |
| P1-4 | :r[Legislative and appropriations conditions for the continuity build and its authorities]{compartment=QUARRY auth="CS" ref="CS-01-DEL"} | **Met** |
| P1-5 | Command structure and critical facility staff | In hand |

:::redact{compartment=QUARRY auth="CS" ref="CS-01-DEL"}
P1-4 was recorded as met in two conditions. The first condition was met on the appropriations of :c[realEvents.RE-12.dateText]. The second condition was met on :c[realEvents.RE-11.dateText]. The objective is closed. No further action is required of the Office under it.
:::

:::redact{tier=1 auth="COUNCIL" ref="CC-ART-III"}
The meeting recorded that the event on which both conditions depended took place on the date planned and in the form planned under OSC-CP-08. The meeting recorded that no person admitted to the Office was identified in the public investigation. The meeting did not record a discussion.
:::

## Item 3. Contingency OSC-CP-08

The contingency is to be closed and withdrawn. Copies issued are to be certified by the Secretary, retained or destroyed as the meeting directed (action D-01-04/1). The register entry is kept by the Office of Internal Assurance for completeness checks only (:x[OSC-ASR-CP-08]).

:::redact{tier=1 auth="COUNCIL" ref="CC-ART-III"}
Those read into the contingency are to be carried in personnel accounting as a change of category, with effect from 01 DEC 2001.
:::

## Item 4. Report to the Council

The Secretary is to report to the Council at Session 6 of 2001 that the Phase 1 objectives held under the delegation are met or in hand. No particulars are to be laid. The item is to be recorded in the register copy of the minutes only (:x[OSC-CNL-MIN-01-06]).

Five seats present at the opening and at the close.
