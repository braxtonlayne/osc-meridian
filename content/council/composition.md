This register sets out the composition of the Council under Article II of the Charter (:x[OSC-CNL-CHR-II]) and the numbered distribution copies issued to its members. A seat belongs to the office it represents. Holders are entered by office only.

Distribution copy numbers correspond to seat numbers. The holder of Seat 01 receives copy 01 of each numbered Council record, the holder of Seat 02 receives copy 02, and so on through Seat 11 and copy 11. A copy number is not reassigned between seats.

## Notes on the register

1. On succession the departing member's copy of every Council record is destroyed before the successor's copy is issued (Article II). Destruction is witnessed by two officers of the Secretariat and recorded on :x[OSC-F-137]. Certificates are filed with the Secretariat; the retention period is set by :x[OSC-DIR-0354].
2. Copies are sighted by the Secretary at the annual custody audit. The 2007 audit was held 01 to 05 OCT 2007. All issued copies were sighted.
3. Register entries for a given numbered document can be looked up from a QUARRY-accredited workstation with the Secretariat's register utility (`copies`, followed by the document number). The utility returns the register as it stood at the last Secretariat entry.
4. Corrections to this register are made by the Secretary only, by a new revision. Enquiries to the Council Secretariat, ALPHA-01, a.penhallow@a01.cs.mer.
