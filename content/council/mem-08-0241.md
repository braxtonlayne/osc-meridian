The extract gives the operative paragraphs of three notes. Passes, courier, parking and telephone paragraphs are omitted except where marked.

## Note 06/09, 13 MAR 2006

1\. The reauthorization was signed on 09 MAR 2006, as scheduled. Legislative particulars are entered in :x[OSC-MEM-01-0388] at Rev 2.

2\. Principal not briefed on programme.

3\. Parking. The desk's bay on the east side has been reallocated to the host office's new deputy. The desk now parks off site and walks; allow fifteen minutes on courier days.

## Note 07/18, 11 MAY 2007

1\. The national continuity policy, NSPD-51 / HSPD-20, was issued on :c[realEvents.RE-08.dateText] over the principal's signature. The Council will note it at Session 3 (:x[OSC-CNL-MIN-07-03]).

2\. Principal not briefed on programme.

:::redact{compartment=QUARRY auth="CS" ref="CS-LIA-07"}
3\. Principal's attention directed to the final draft in the week of 30 APR, with the host office's summary on top. The summary was prepared by the desk. Text as issued is consistent with programme requirements. The two paragraphs on alternate facilities that the desk asked to have generalised were generalised.
:::

## Note 08/06, 11 FEB 2008

1\. Transition planning for the change of administration in JAN 2009 has begun in the host office. The desk will be asked for continuity material for the incoming team. Standard continuity material only.

2\. The primary campaigns are followed from the public record. No candidate is to be briefed on programme, before or after the election.

:::redact{tier=1 auth="COUNCIL" ref="CC-ART-III"}
3\. The desk's standing instruction carries over to the next principal unchanged. The desk is to be in post before the inauguration and is not to be the subject of any transition paper.
:::

4\. Parking unchanged.
