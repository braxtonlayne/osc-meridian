## Standing committee on reserves

The holder chairs the Standing Committee on Reserves. The committee secretary is provided by Logistics & Reserves, ALPHA-05. The committee meets on the morning of each Council session in Conference Room 6-014, ALPHA-01, from 1230Z (0830 EDT; 0730 EST in winter months).

Committee papers:

| Paper | Source | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Strategic reserves position | :x[OSC-RSV-IDX] | Each session |
| Basis of reserve figures | :x[OSC-MEM-07-0612] | On revision |
| Reactor plant summary | Logistics & Reserves | Quarterly |
| Generator fuel and rotation stocks | Logistics & Reserves | Quarterly |

Committee papers are returned to the committee secretary at the close of the meeting and travel back to ALPHA-05 by the next booked tram path with a Security Forces escort.

## Succession history

:::redact{compartment=QUARRY auth="COUNCIL" ref="CC-ART-II"}
No succession. The holder has occupied the seat since the Council was constituted. The holder is named in the Seat 01 succession pack to take the chair in the interval before a successor to Seat 01 is admitted.
