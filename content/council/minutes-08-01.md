No recording equipment was present in the chamber. These minutes were prepared by N. Fenwick, Minutes Clerk, from the Secretary's notes, and are signed by the Secretary. Attendance is recorded by seat. Seven members constitute a quorum.

## Item 1. Quorum and apologies

Nine members were present; a quorum was declared at 1503Z (1003 EST). Apologies were received from Seat 06 (illness) and Seat 09 (travel; rail path from the western hub cancelled for a possession).

## Item 2. Minutes of Session 6 of 2007

Approved without amendment. Action 07-06/5 (anteroom heating) is carried forward as 08-01/4.

## Item 3. Recruitment Phase 3: progress report

The Director, Personnel & Vetting, reported on Phase 3 (:x[OSC-PER-PH3]). At the December pay run total personnel stood at :c[headline.staffTotal], the Phase 3 cumulative figure of :c[phases.3.cumulative]. The strength summary is issued each pay run under :x[OSC-PER-IDX]. A strength return for the phase will be laid at Session 2 (action 08-01/1).

Seat 02 asked whether the figure included personnel not posted to a facility. The Director said that it did, and that personnel in categories other than posted were carried in the total in accordance with standing practice. The Council noted the report.

## Item 4. Facility Inventory: draft revision 2008-Q2

The Secretary tabled the draft of revision 2008-Q2 of the Facility Inventory, prepared from the facility returns of the fourth quarter of 2007. Revision is by a majority of the full Council (:x[OSC-CNL-CHR-III]). Tabling copies were read in the chamber and returned to the Secretary at the close of the session; none left the chamber.

Members' comments are to be given to the Secretary in person by 07 MAR 2008 (action 08-01/2). The Secretariat will prepare issue arrangements for approval at Session 2 (action 08-01/3).

:::redact{compartment=QUARRY auth="CS" ref="OSC-CNL-SEAT-00"}
Seat 07 asked whether copies of the current revision would be withdrawn before the new revision was issued. The Secretary said that the current revision would be destroyed by the Secretariat on issue of the new revision, copy for copy, against the register, and that certificates would be laid at the session following issue.
:::

## Item 5. Declarations of interests, 2008

The Secretary reported that the 2008 declarations for Seats 10 and 11 were received on 09 JAN 2008 and 08 JAN 2008 respectively, nil change in each case (:x[OSC-CNL-SEAT-10], :x[OSC-CNL-SEAT-11]).

## Item 6. :r[Personnel accounting: carriage of personnel without posting at month end]{compartment=QUARRY auth="CS" ref="OSC-CNL-RES-08-01"}

:::redact{tier=1 auth="CS" ref="OSC-CNL-RES-08-01"}
The Director, Personnel & Vetting, presented a paper on the personnel carried in the total without a posting, by site of last posting and by date of change of category. The paper was read in the chamber and returned. The Council agreed the recommendations in paragraphs 4 to 7 of the paper, and directed that the matter return at Session 2 of 2008 for a date of effect.
:::

## Item 7. Session arrangements: anteroom heating, winter menu

The anteroom was again cold on the morning of the session. A second heater is to be fitted and the thermostat checked by the works office on the day before each session (action 08-01/4). Members thanked Food Service for the hot soup. The winter menu continues to the end of March.

The session adjourned at 1810Z (1310 EST).
