## Presiding duties

The holder of this seat presides at every session and settles the agenda with the Secretary not later than five working days before the session. Agenda papers are issued to the presiding member one day ahead of the other members.

The presiding member votes only to break a tie (Article III; see :x[OSC-CNL-CHR-III]). Casting votes are entered in the minutes of the session concerned.

## Succession history

:::redact{compartment=QUARRY auth="COUNCIL" ref="CC-ART-II"}
No succession. The holder has occupied the seat since the Council was constituted. No read-out or reissue of copy 01 has been required. The Secretariat holds a sealed succession pack for this seat (instructions for the presiding duties to pass to the Seat 03 holder in the interval before admission of a successor), last reviewed 14 SEP 2007.
:::

## Committee assignments

:::redact{compartment=QUARRY auth="CS" ref="CS-06-140"}
Ex officio member of all standing committees. Sits on the Standing Committee on Custody and Records (chair: Seat 06).
:::

## Administration

- Arrival at ALPHA-01 is by road. The holder's vehicle is received at the surface gate and is not logged against the surface facility's visitor book; the gate log entry reads "Secretariat, by arrangement".
- Session papers are carried by the Secretary in person and do not pass through the Secretariat courier.
- Dietary requirements and medication held for this seat are on file with the ALPHA-01 Food Service manager and Medical Services. Updated 14 SEP 2007.
