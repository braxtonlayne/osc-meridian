No recording equipment was present. These minutes were prepared by N. Fenwick, Minutes Clerk, from the Secretary's notes, and are signed by the Secretary. Attendance is recorded by seat. Seven members constitute a quorum. This library extract is made from the members' text.

## Item 1. Quorum and apologies

Ten members were present; a quorum was declared at 1503Z (1003 EST). Apologies were received from Seat 07 (travel). The Secretary recorded the attendance of the holder of Seat 06 at a full session for the first time since admission on 24 SEP 2001 (:x[OSC-CNL-SEAT-06]).

## Item 2. Minutes of Session 5 of 2001

Approved without amendment.

## Item 3. Construction: first-year return

The Chief Engineer reported excavation in progress at the first sites and the first obligations of the programme year (:x[OSC-LDG-FY01]): $:c[budget.fiscalYears.FY2001.totalM] million in FY2001. The Council noted the return (action 01-06/1).

Seat 10 recused himself from the discussion of prime contracts and waited in the works office. The time of leaving and return was recorded by the Secretary: 1611Z to 1634Z (1111 to 1134 EST).

## Item 4. Recruitment Phase 1: progress

Recruitment under :c[phases.1.title] continues by nomination (:x[OSC-PER-PH1]). The Council agreed that a Personnel & Vetting division should be proposed for the second year of the phase, so that investigations no longer depend on interim arrangements (action 01-06/2).

## Item 5. Report of the Secretary

*Item 5 is not reproduced in members' copies. Register copy only.*

:::redact{compartment=QUARRY auth="CS" ref="CS-01-DEL"}
Register text. The Secretary reported, in the form agreed under authority delegated by the Council (:x[OSC-CNL-CHR-III]), that the Phase 1 objectives held under the delegation were met or in hand. No particulars were laid. None were requested.
:::

## Item 6. Custody audit 2001

The Secretary will sight every issued copy in the second week of January. Members will be told the date of the visit by the Secretariat liaison line.

## Item 7. Session arrangements

The temporary chamber was cold. Two portable heaters are to be obtained and the works contractor asked to keep the access road gritted on session days (action 01-06/3). Members thanked the works canteen for the soup.

The session adjourned at 1905Z (1405 EST).
