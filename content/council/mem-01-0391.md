1\. **Passes.** Building passes for the desk were renewed on 24 OCT under the new screening arrangements. The desk's second pass holder is still waiting; expect two weeks. Visitor access for the Secretariat courier now requires a named escort from the host office. Courier days move from Tuesday to Wednesday until further notice.

2\. **Communications.** The desk's secure fax was taken out of service on 22 OCT for re-keying with the host office's equipment. Traffic for the desk goes by courier until the line is restored. Telephone practice as :x[OSC-DIR-0447].

3\. **Principal's schedule.** The principal's public schedule for the week was as published by the press office. The signing was scheduled for 26 OCT and was held as scheduled. Legislative particulars are in :x[OSC-MEM-01-0388].

4\. **Programme.** Principal not briefed on programme. Standing instruction unchanged: not to be briefed.

:::redact{compartment=QUARRY auth="CS" ref="CS-LIA-01"}
5\. **Reading.** Principal's attention directed to the domestic security summaries for the week and to the signing papers. Continuity of government papers from the interagency review were held back to the week of 05 NOV, so that they did not compete with the signing for the principal's time. Nothing touching a programme site or line reached the principal's reading.
:::

:::redact{tier=1 auth="COUNCIL" ref="CC-ART-III"}
6\. **For the delegation.** Signature confirmed 26 OCT. Second condition met. The principal's remarks at the signing were his own and are on the public record; the desk supplied nothing for them.
:::

7\. Next note 06 NOV.
