## Holdings and purpose

The collection holds :r[:c[reserves.items.MED-GENETIC.value]{auth="RD" ref="RD-07-0231"} samples]{compartment=LANTERN auth="RD" ref="RD-07-0110"} on Level 9. It is a Records Division holding. It is not medical stock and is not drawn on for clinical use; see :x[OSC-RSV-MED].

:::redact{tier=2 auth="RD" ref="RD-07-0231"}
The collection is the physical holding of the genetic and biometric database for "essential personnel". It supports verification of the identity of designated persons and their dependants on admission under seal, and verification of family lines under the OSC supplement to the background investigation (:x[OSC-PER-VET]). Selection of donors is made on the lists issued to the Chief, Records Division, and is not made by the collection.
:::

## Accession numbering

Accession numbers take the form **BSC-YY-NNNNNN**: BSC for the biological sample collection, YY the year of receipt, and a six-digit serial within the year. Aliquots of one accession share its number with a two-digit aliquot suffix (-01 to -12). Donor names are not recorded in the catalogue. The donor reference is held by Personnel & Vetting and is matched to the accession only on the Personnel & Vetting register.

## Receipt

Samples are drawn at site clinics, frozen at the clinic, and moved to ALPHA-03 by tram in dry-ice shippers under chain of custody form RD-17. The vault custodian checks the seal number and the shipper temperature log against the RD-17 on receipt and signs the custody copy for return to the clinic. Shippers arriving with a broken seal or a temperature excursion are quarantined unopened and reported to the Chief, Records Division.

April 2008: 31 shippers received, 30 accepted, 1 quarantined (seal number not matching RD-17; clerical error at the sending clinic, confirmed by cable and accepted 22 APR 2008).

A donor recorded in more than one category is entered under the first code in this list. A change of category is entered by the Chief, Records Division, on notice from Personnel & Vetting; the accession is not re-numbered and the sample is not moved.
:::

## Storage conditions

1. Whole blood and buffy coat are held in vapour-phase nitrogen dewars at -150 C or colder (dewar room, D-01 to D-06). Extracted DNA and buccal material are held in mechanical freezers at -80 C (freezer hall, F-01 to F-24).
2. Freezer alarms are set at -70 C. Dewar alarms are set on nitrogen level and on vapour temperature at -135 C. Alarms annunciate at the Level 9 lobby, the ALPHA-03 operations desk and the vault custodian's pager.
3. Liquid nitrogen is produced by the Level 9 nitrogen plant and transferred to the dewars weekly on Tuesdays. The plant is on the essential bus.
4. Aliquots are drawn only for verification requests authorised by the Chief, Records Division. Each draw is entered against the accession with the request reference. An accession is never reduced below two aliquots.
5. The freezer hall and dewar room are entered by two persons. Oxygen monitors are fitted in the dewar room; entry is not permitted while the low-oxygen alarm is sounding.

Freezer F-07 duty compressor was replaced on 15 APR 2008 under work order FE-08-0388. The contents were held in F-08 and F-09 for 26 hours and returned. No excursion was recorded.
