This memorandum fixes the two capacity figures used on reserve records and the basis on which reserve quantities and burn-down figures are stated. It is issued because the May 2007 site returns stated reserve sufficiency on three different bases, and two returns compared reserve holdings against berth counts.

Sustained capacity is :c[headline.capacitySustained] persons. It is the number of persons the network can keep alive for the planned sustainment period at full reserves. It is the figure at which the Council inventory states reserve quantities, and it is the figure Logistics & Reserves uses for every reserve holding, allocation and burn-down calculation.

Total surge berthing is :c[headline.capacitySurge] berths. It is the physical count of berths across all facilities, being the sum of the class figures: ALPHA :c[classes.ALPHA.statedCapacity], BETA :c[classes.BETA.statedCapacity], GAMMA :c[classes.GAMMA.statedCapacity] and DELTA :c[classes.DELTA.statedCapacity]. Berthing is a facility figure. It is not supported by reserves at full occupancy and is not to be used as a basis for any reserve quantity.

All reserve quantities and burn-down figures are stated against the sustained figure. Where a record gives a duration (meals, pharmaceutical supply, generator runtime), the duration is the period the holding supports at that scale. Facility records continue to give their own berthing figures and are not to be altered to agree with this memorandum.

Meal stocks are stated against the Council figure. Site returns are not to restate the ration basis.

:::redact{compartment=TALLOW auth="LR" ref="LR-07-0314"}
Reserve sufficiency is stated for designated persons only; berths above the sustained figure are not provisioned. The sustained figure is distributed by site in Attachment 1 against the designation lists held for each site, and site allocations of food, medical stock and fuel are sized to that distribution and not to site berthing. Where a site's designated persons exceed its share at the time of a seal, the excess is met by movement from the Reserves Stacks at ALPHA-05 and not by drawing on another site's allocation.
:::

In correspondence and returns, the words "capacity" and "berths" are not to be used without the qualifier. Write "sustained capacity" with the figure, or "berthing" with the facility. The glossary definitions (:x[OSC-SYS-GLO]) have been amended to agree.

The monthly reserve position (:x[OSC-RSV-IDX]) will be issued on this basis from the July 2007 position. The Facility Registry (:x[OSC-FAC-IDX]) is not affected.

The point of contact is Stores Control, Logistics & Reserves, ALPHA-05, extension 5-2210.
