## Holdings

The collection holds :c[reserves.items.FOOD-SEED.value] samples in two cold rooms on Level 8. A sample is one accession: a single seed lot of one taxon from one source, received on one date.

The collection is selective. Accessions are taken against the Collection List approved by the Chief, Records Division, and material outside the List is returned to the sender or destroyed on receipt. The List is reviewed every two years; the current List was approved in September 2006.

## Accession numbering

Accession numbers take the form **SC-YY-NNNNNN**: SC for the seed collection, YY the year of receipt, and a six-digit serial within the year issued in order of receipt. Numbers are not reused. A regenerated lot keeps the number of its parent with a suffix (-R1, -R2). The accession number is written on the packet, the box label and the catalogue entry, and is the only identifier used in correspondence.

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Accession | SC-YY-NNNNNN, suffix for regenerations |
| Taxon | Genus, species, and cultivar or landrace name where known |
| Origin | State or country of collection, and the supplying holding |
| Received | Date received in the Level 8 lobby |
| Viability test | Date of the last germination test and the result as a percentage |
| Location | Cold room, rack, shelf and box (for example 2/C-17/4/031) |

## Storage conditions

1. Seed is dried to 5 to 7% moisture content in the drying room (15% relative humidity, 15 C) before packing.
2. Each accession is packed in heat-sealed foil laminate packets, normally two packets per accession, and boxed by rack position.
3. Cold rooms are held at -18 C, with an alarm at -15 C and a recorded excursion at -16 C. Doors are not to stand open for longer than two minutes. Cold-weather clothing is issued in the Level 8 lobby and returned there.
4. Germination is tested at ten-year intervals, or at five-year intervals for short-lived taxa (onion, carrot, pepper and others on the List). The first full cycle of tests began in 2007. Accessions below 85% of their initial germination are regenerated.
5. Accessions are not opened for any purpose other than testing and regeneration. Requests to draw seed for any other purpose are referred to the Chief, Records Division.
