This register lists every installation carried by the Office, :c[headline.facilities] in all, by class. It is kept by the FE Registry Section at ALPHA-02 from the facility returns and is revised after each quarter-end pay run. Definitions of terms used in the register are in :x[OSC-SYS-GLO]. The class standards are :x[OSC-FAC-CLS-ALPHA], :x[OSC-FAC-CLS-BETA], :x[OSC-FAC-CLS-GAMMA] and :x[OSC-FAC-CLS-DELTA].

Columns are released by tier and compartment. Designation and class are shown for every installation. Location, cover identity and connections are shown for the reader's own installation and the installations it connects to directly; the remainder are held under SEXTANT. Function lines are held at Tier 2.

## 5. Notes

1. Staff figures are posted strength from the facility returns. Personnel carried centrally are not shown by site; see :x[OSC-PER-IDX].
2. Berthing is surge berthing; sustained capacity is stated in :x[OSC-MEM-07-0612] (:c[headline.capacitySustained]).
3. Positions on the registry map are computed from the location entry of each installation (distance and bearing from the named town). They are for orientation and are not survey positions.
4. A site whose return was not received by the cut-off is shown at its previous figure. No returns were outstanding at this revision.
