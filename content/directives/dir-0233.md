## Purpose

This directive is the governing payroll issuance for personnel accounting. It defines the pay status POSTED, sets the form and timetable of the monthly strength return, governs the reconciliation of each pay run to the strength returns, and sets the procedure for personnel queries.

## Applicability

This directive applies to Personnel & Vetting, to the Comptroller in respect of payroll disbursement, to the command section of every staffed facility, and to every division head.

It applies to every person who holds a personnel number, whatever their tier, posting or status. Strength at Tier 1 is accounted by the Council Secretariat and enters the reconciliation as a single line without breakdown.

## Policy

Every person who holds a personnel number is in exactly one pay status at the close of each pay period.

Personnel accounting is the monthly reconciliation of tier strength against posted strength, by pay status. Tier strength is the number of personnel numbers in the pay run. Posted strength is the sum of the facility strength returns.

The pay run is the authority for tier strength. The strength returns are the authority for posted strength. Neither is amended to agree with the other.

A personnel number is issued once and is not reissued. It leaves the pay run only on a record of separation entered by the Director, Personnel & Vetting.

The strength figures produced by Pay & Strength Accounting from the reconciliation are the figures of record. Figures produced by any lookup utility are for information only and are not quoted in correspondence.

## Status POSTED

A person is POSTED when they are entered on the strength return of a facility by its command section.

POSTED includes personnel on annual, sick or administrative leave; personnel in a probationary period; personnel removed from the seal roster under :x[OSC-DIR-0204]; and personnel on temporary duty at another facility for thirty days or less, who remain on the strength return of their parent facility.

Personnel on temporary duty for more than thirty days are posted to the host facility for the period by amendment to both returns.

Personnel in transit under transfer orders remain on the return of the losing facility until the day they report, and are entered on the return of the receiving facility from that day.

A person is POSTED at one facility only. A person entered on two returns is reported by Pay & Strength Accounting to both command sections, and the losing facility's entry is withdrawn.

Cover facility staff employed by the cover agency hold no personnel number and are not counted in any return.

## The Monthly Strength Return

Each facility command section renders a strength return as at 2359Z on the last day of the month. Changes known to take effect before that time are entered as forecasts.

The return lists personnel numbers by tier and by function line of the standard ratio model. It does not list names.

The return is rendered to Pay & Strength Accounting by 2000Z on the last working day of the month. A return with no changes from the previous month is still rendered.

The return is signed by the Facility Commander or the Deputy Commander. A return signed for either of them is accepted where the signatory's specimen signature is held by Pay & Strength Accounting.

## Pay Run Reconciliation

Pay & Strength Accounting reconciles each pay run against the strength returns for the same period, by personnel number, within five working days of the pay run.

Differences are listed by personnel number and by pay status. A difference accounted for by a pay status entered in the pay run requires no reconciliation action.

Remaining differences are cleared with the command section concerned. A difference not cleared within the five working days is raised as a personnel query under section 8.

The completed reconciliation is reported to the Deputy Director, Personnel & Vetting, and to the Comptroller. The strength summary for the period, :x[OSC-PER-IDX], is published from it.

## Personnel Queries

A personnel query is raised by memorandum to the Deputy Director, Personnel & Vetting, and is given a PV-Q number by the Personnel & Vetting registry on receipt.

The memorandum states the pay run and the returns concerned, the personnel numbers or counts in question, and the issuance the originator considers governs the matter.

A query is answered or closed within ten working days of receipt.

A query is closed when the Deputy Director determines that no reconciliation action is required. The closure memorandum is sent to the originator and their division head. A closed query is not reopened by the originator. A further query on the same matter requires new evidence and is countersigned by the originator's division head.

Queries about the pay status of a named individual are made in person to Pay & Strength Accounting and not by correspondence.

## Responsibilities

The Director, Personnel & Vetting, owns this directive, enters records of separation, and approves changes of pay status.

The Deputy Director, Personnel & Vetting, receives the reconciliation, and answers or closes personnel queries.

Pay & Strength Accounting keeps the personnel number register, receives strength returns and status declarations, carries out the reconciliation, and publishes the strength summary.

The Comptroller disburses pay against the pay run as certified by Personnel & Vetting and reports any disbursement that does not match it.

Facility command sections render strength returns and clear differences within the timetable in section 7.

Division heads notify the command section of any posting, temporary duty or departure within one working day.

## Procedures

Changes of pay status are entered by Personnel & Vetting only, on the authority of a posting order, an amendment for temporary duty, a record of separation, or a status declaration received by Pay & Strength Accounting.

Status declarations received at a facility on :x[OSC-F-131] are forwarded under cover to Pay & Strength Accounting on the day of receipt. They are not opened or copied at the facility, and they are not entered on the facility strength return.

The month-end timetable is: strength returns by 2000Z on the last working day; reconciliation within five working days of the pay run; strength summary published on completion of the reconciliation.

Strength returns, reconciliations, closed queries and status declarations are retained permanently under :x[OSC-DIR-0354].
