## Purpose

This directive sets the menu cycle, dining hours and special diet arrangements for food service at all staffed facilities, and links the cafeteria cycle to the rotation of long-term ration stocks.

## Applicability

This directive applies to food service supervisors and staff, Logistics & Reserves stores control, site medical officers, and all personnel who take meals in a facility dining room.

Cover facility staff are fed by the cover facility's own canteen. OSC personnel on duty at the surface may use that canteen on the cover agency's terms. Cover facility staff do not use facility dining rooms.

## Policy

Each staffed facility serves from a six-week menu cycle issued by Logistics & Reserves. The cycles are numbered 1 to 6 and run in step at every facility, so that each facility is serving the same cycle in the same week.

The cycle is aligned network-wide so that the drawdown of ration stocks is uniform across facilities and the stock position reported for each facility can be compared with every other.

A hot meal is served in each dining room in the ninety minutes before and after each watch change. A cold service is available from the servery at all other times. On federal holidays and during minimum manning under :x[OSC-DIR-0189] the dining room keeps holiday hours, posted at the dining room door.

Personnel take meals in the dining room serving their berthing level. Facilities with two dining rooms allocate them by watch.

Long-term ration stocks approaching the end of their rotation life are drawn into the cafeteria cycle and replaced from new stock, so that no lot is held beyond its rotation date. The rotation draw is planned by stores control against the reserve position published in :x[OSC-RSV-FOOD]. It is not to exceed one meal in three in any week.

Special diets for medical reasons are provided on the written recommendation of the site medical officer. Diets for religious observance are provided from the standard stock lines where they allow it, and otherwise on the same basis as medical diets.

No food is taken from the dining room to berthing, to records areas, or to any controlled area. Hot beverages are permitted in offices in lidded cups.

## Responsibilities

The Director, Logistics & Reserves, issues the menu cycle, approves substitutions that affect more than one facility, and reports ration drawdown to the reserves position.

Stores control at each facility plans the rotation draw, issues stock to the kitchen against the cycle, and records each lot drawn.

The food service supervisor runs the dining room to the cycle, posts the menu cards for the week by the preceding Friday, keeps the special diet register and reports substitutions.

The site medical officer considers special diet requests and reviews each special diet at the individual's annual medical readiness certification under :x[OSC-DIR-0204].

## Procedures

A request for a special diet is made to the site medical officer by appointment. If supported, the site medical officer sends a recommendation to the food service supervisor stating the diet and its duration. The diet starts within five working days of the recommendation.

A request for a diet for religious observance is made directly to the food service supervisor. The supervisor confirms within three working days which meals can be provided from standard stock and refers anything further to the site medical officer.

Menu cards are posted in the dining room and on MERIDIAN. The card shows the cycle number, the week, and for each day the hot meal, the alternative and the cold service.

When a supplier substitution or a plant outage changes the menu, the food service supervisor posts a substitution notice in the dining room and on MERIDIAN before the meal is served. A substitution that runs for the whole of a cycle is approved by the Director, Logistics & Reserves.

Kitchen hood suppression systems are inspected under :x[OSC-DIR-0156]. During an inspection the dining room serves the cold service only, and the notice under paragraph 5.4 says so.

Ration lots drawn into the cycle are inspected before issue. A lot found unfit is withdrawn, recorded in the stores log with its lot number, and reported to stores control the same day. It is not served.
