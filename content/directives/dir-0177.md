## Purpose

This directive sets the end-of-shift clean desk requirement, the handling of burn bags from issue to collection, and the standard for shredders used to destroy classified waste.

## Applicability

This directive applies to all personnel at every staffed facility who handle classified material in any form, including working papers, drafts, carbon and typewriter ribbons, printer output, and removable media.

The destruction of records that have been accessioned is governed by :x[OSC-DIR-0354] and not by this directive. Classified waste is material that has never been a record: drafts, spoiled copies, notes made in the course of work, and superseded rosters and schedules.

## Policy

At the end of each shift every workspace is left clear of classified material. Material is returned to its container, the container is locked, and the container check sheet is initialled with the time.

Clean desk checks are made without notice, at the end of a shift or during it, by the Resident Assurance Officer, the facility security officer, or a person either of them names. A finding is recorded as a security violation against the person to whom the workspace is assigned, whether or not they left the material there.

Classified waste is placed in a burn bag and nowhere else. It is not placed in general waste, torn by hand, or kept in a desk drawer for later disposal.

Burn bags are issued numbered. Each bag is sealed when two-thirds full, or at the end of the working week if not filled, and is logged on collection. A bag is never opened once sealed.

Shredders may be used in place of a burn bag only where they are accredited for classified waste. An accredited shredder bears its accreditation label and the date of its last inspection. Shredders without the label are used for unclassified paper only.

## Responsibilities

The Director, Office of Internal Assurance, owns this directive and receives the monthly summary of clean desk findings from each facility.

The Resident Assurance Officer at each ALPHA-class facility, and the facility security officer at other facilities, makes the clean desk checks, records findings, and audits the burn bag log each month.

Communications Security accredits shredders, inspects them every six months, and withdraws the label from any shredder that fails.

The division head ensures each section has burn bags in stock, a container check sheet on each container, and a named person for each burn bag station.

Each person clears their own workspace and places their own waste in a burn bag.

## Procedures

Burn bags are drawn from the facility security office in packets of ten. The serials issued are recorded against the section. A spoiled or torn bag is returned for destruction under its own serial and is not discarded.

A bag in use is kept at a named burn bag station inside the section's controlled area. It is not left in a corridor, a copier room or a lift lobby.

When a bag is two-thirds full, the person who finds it so seals it with the issued seal, writes the date and their initials across the seal, and enters the bag's serial and the time in the station log.

Sealed bags are collected at 1100Z and 1900Z on working days, local times posted at each station. The collector signs the station log for each bag. Bags are taken directly to the destruction point and are not stored overnight outside it.

A burn bag whose serial does not appear in the station log, or whose seal is broken, is reported to the Resident Assurance Officer or the facility security officer before it is moved.

Removable media, ribbons and printer cartridges are placed in the media bin at the burn bag station, not in a burn bag. The media bin is collected weekly by Communications Security.

Superseded access rosters and duty rosters are placed in a burn bag on the day they are superseded, as :x[OSC-DIR-0115] requires.

A clean desk finding is notified to the person concerned in writing within one working day. A person who finds material of their own left out after the end of a shift reports it under :x[OSC-DIR-0378] before anyone else does so.
