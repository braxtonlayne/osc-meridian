## Purpose

This directive states the requirement that a person be briefed into a compartment before any record held in that compartment is displayed to them, read by them, discussed with them or placed in their custody. It sets out the ways in which a person is read in, and the handling of compartmented records on paper and at MERIDIAN workstations.

It is the reference cited by the workstation whenever a record, or part of a record, is withheld from the person requesting it.

## Applicability

This directive applies to all OSC personnel of every tier, to contract personnel holding unescorted access, and to every accredited MERIDIAN workstation.

It applies to Council records only to the extent that the Council Secretariat has not issued its own handling instruction for them.

## Policy

No compartmented content may be displayed to a person who has not been briefed into the compartment. Tier establishes eligibility; the briefing establishes access. Neither stands in for the other, and a person of higher tier is not by that fact briefed into any compartment.

A person who holds a compartment does not by holding it learn the name, scope or holdings of any other compartment. Compartment names are not used in correspondence, on routing slips or in conversation with persons who do not hold them.

An acting credential issued under :x[OSC-DIR-0395] confers the access of the higher tier but not the briefings associated with it.

Withheld material is withheld in full. A summary, paraphrase, confirmation or denial of withheld text is itself withheld text.

## Read-in

A person is read into a compartment in one of the following ways, and in no other:

<ol type="a">
<li>

**On posting.** Where a compartment is attached to the post, the holder of the post is briefed in the first week of posting and acknowledges the briefing at the workstation or in person.

</li>
<li>

**On request sponsored by a holder.** A request is made on :x[OSC-F-126] and sponsored by a person who holds the compartment, who certifies the requester's need-to-know. Only compartments designated in the classification guide (:x[OSC-ASR-CG]) as approvable on request are granted in this way. A request for any other compartment is returned "Not approvable on request".

</li>
<li>

**When making a report classified in the compartment.** A report made under :x[OSC-DIR-0378] that concerns compartmented material is classified in that compartment on receipt, and the reporter is read in to the extent needed to complete, sign and answer for the report.

</li>
<li>

**As a declarant or witness.** A person who makes a declaration held in a compartment, or who is interviewed in an inquiry held in a compartment, is read in for that purpose.

</li>
<li>

**By distribution of a work item.** A person added to the distribution of a work order, tasking or accession held in a compartment is read in for the purposes of that item.

</li>
<li>

:r[On the instruction of the Council, without briefing and without entry in the briefing record, for the period the Council states.]{tier=1 auth="CS" ref="CS-R-01"}

</li>
</ol>

Read-in under subparagraphs (c), (d) and (e) is recorded in the same way as any other briefing. It is not withdrawn when the report, declaration or work item is closed unless the withholding authority directs a debriefing.

A person who believes they have been read in under (c) to (e) without being told so should check the briefing record at the workstation (`clearance`) before asking their supervisor.

## Briefing and acknowledgement

Each compartment has a numbered briefing in the OSC-CB series. The briefing is read in full, at the workstation or in person, and acknowledged by signature or by token. A briefing that is not acknowledged within 24 hours of issue lapses and is given again.

The briefing record shows, for each compartment held, the briefing number, the date and the means of read-in. It does not show compartments the person does not hold.

## Debriefing and withdrawal

A person is debriefed from a compartment on transfer, on separation, or on any change of duty that removes the need-to-know, and signs the termination statement. The obligations of the briefing continue after debriefing.

Admission to a compartment may be withdrawn as it was given (:x[OSC-CNL-CHR-V]). Withdrawal by the Office of Internal Assurance or on Council instruction takes effect when entered and is not notified in advance.

## Handling and display of compartmented records

On paper, a compartmented record carries a cover sheet at all times outside an accredited container. The compartment is shown on the cover sheet by its codeword only. Records of different compartments are not filed in one folder; a file that comes to contain material of two compartments is handled as both until it is split by Records Division.

In a shared workspace, compartmented material is turned face down or put away and the workstation is locked when a person who does not hold the compartment enters. This applies to supervisors, escorts and maintenance staff alike.

At the workstation, material is withheld at the level of the block, the sentence or the field. Withheld text is not sent to a workstation whose session does not satisfy it, and cannot be recovered from the display, the print stream or the search index. A withholding mark shows the withholding authority and its reference and nothing else. Examining a withholding mark is recorded and is not a violation.

Where a person may not open a record at all, the workstation displays the following refusal in place of the record:

> Requires Tier 2 compartment briefing. Reference DIR-0412.

A record or part of a record released to a person after briefing is displayed to them from that time without further action. Earlier copies, printed or noted, are not brought up to date and are handled at the marking they bore when made.

No person shall ask a holder to read out, describe or confirm withheld text, and no holder shall do so.

## Refusals and their record

Each refusal is entered in the audit trail of the requesting workstation with the document number and the time.

Repeated refusals are recorded. At the fifth refusal in one session the workstation displays a notice to that effect. The notice is not a finding against the user and requires no action of them.

Refusal entries are reviewed with the rest of the audit trail by the Resident Assurance Officer. A pattern of refusals is not of itself a reason for inquiry.

## Responsibilities

The Director, Office of Internal Assurance, is the office of primary responsibility for this directive, maintains the briefing record and determines disputes over the compartment in which a report, declaration or inquiry is held.

Each withholding authority decides what it withholds, under which reference, and when withheld text is released. Only the withholding authority, or the Council, may release withheld text.

Supervisors of record sponsor requests under para 4(b) only where they hold the compartment and can state the duty that requires it.

Resident Assurance Officers give briefings in person on request, review refusal entries and arrange debriefing on transfer and separation.

The Director, Communications Security, ensures that accredited workstations withhold and refuse in accordance with para 7 and para 8, and that the refusal text is not altered locally.

Every holder of a compartment is personally responsible for the material in their custody and for the conduct required by para 7.

## Procedures

To request a compartment under para 4(b):

1. Identify a sponsor who holds the compartment. The personnel directory shows holdings to the extent your tier permits.
2. Complete :x[OSC-F-126], stating the duty that requires access and citing at least one record within the compartment's scope.
3. Submit the form at the workstation. The receipt number is your reference.
4. On approval, read and acknowledge the briefing. Access is not effective until the acknowledgement is recorded.

To report an occurrence concerning material you do not hold, report it under :x[OSC-DIR-0378] in the ordinary way. Do not wait to be read in before reporting.
