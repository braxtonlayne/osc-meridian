## Purpose

This directive sets the annual security refresher required of all personnel, the suspense date by which each division completes it, the reporting of training compliance, and the consequences of non-completion.

## Applicability

This directive applies to all OSC personnel of every tier at every site, including personnel on leave, on probation or on temporary duty away from their site. Contract personnel complete the refresher under the terms of their contract.

## Policy

Every person completes the annual security refresher once in each calendar year by the suspense date set for their division. Completion is recorded against the personnel number at the end of the final module.

The refresher is completed at the workstation in duty time. It is not completed on behalf of another person, and modules are not completed by two people at one workstation.

Personnel who transfer during the year complete the refresher by the suspense date of the gaining division. Where that date has passed at the time of transfer, the refresher is due 30 days after the transfer date.

## The refresher

The 2008 refresher consists of six modules, completed in order:

| Module | Title | Approx. duration |
|---|---|---|
| SR-1 | Markings, cover sheets and handling | 25 min |
| SR-2 | Compartments, briefings and need-to-know | 30 min |
| SR-3 | The workstation: tokens, passphrases, audit trail | 20 min |
| SR-4 | Reporting occurrences and self-reporting | 20 min |
| SR-5 | Cover facilities and conduct at the surface | 25 min |
| SR-6 | Records: printing, reproduction and destruction | 20 min |

Each module ends with a short check. A module whose check is not passed is repeated from the beginning.

## Compliance reporting

Training compliance is reported to each division head on the third working day after that division's suspense date, by personnel number. Personnel on probationary transfer are shown separately. For 2008, the Records Division report is due 12 MAY 2008.

The report lists each person not complete at the suspense date, the modules outstanding, and whether the person was on leave or temporary duty during the 30 days before the suspense date.

Division heads return the report with a completion date for each person listed within five working days.

## Consequences of non-completion

From the suspense date until completion, the person's print release and removable media privileges are suspended (:x[OSC-DIR-0319]). The suspension is lifted automatically when the final module is recorded.

When the refresher is more than 30 days overdue:

<ol type="a">
<li>

the person is not eligible for an acting credential (:x[OSC-DIR-0395]) or for designation as an escort;

</li>
<li>

compartments held other than those granted on posting are suspended until completion, and records in them are withheld as though the compartment were not held;

</li>
<li>

the overdue refresher is entered on any probation review then due (:x[OSC-DIR-0247]);

</li>
<li>

the person's name is reported to the Resident Assurance Officer.

</li>
</ol>

A person who is overdue because of documented leave, sickness or temporary duty without workstation access has 30 days from return before para 7.2 applies.

## Responsibilities

The Director, Personnel & Vetting, is the office of primary responsibility, sets the modules and suspense dates each year, and produces the compliance reports.

Division training coordinators keep track of their division's completion and remind those outstanding at 30 days and at 7 days before the suspense date.

Supervisors allow duty time for the refresher and ensure probationers complete it within their first 30 days where the division's suspense date falls within that period.

Every person completes the refresher by the suspense date without further reminder.

## Procedures

1. Open the refresher from the Directory under Training.
2. Complete modules SR-1 to SR-6 in order. Progress is saved at the end of each module.
3. At the end of SR-6, note the completion number shown.
4. Suspensions under para 7.1 are lifted without further action. If print release is not restored by the next working day, call the MERIDIAN Service Desk with the completion number.
