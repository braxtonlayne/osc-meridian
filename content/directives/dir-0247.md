## Purpose

This directive governs the probationary period that follows every transfer between facilities: its length, the supervisor of record, the interim and final reviews, and the conditions that apply while it runs.

The transfer rules themselves, including eligibility and the transfer request, are set out in :x[OSC-PER-TRF].

## Applicability

This directive applies to all personnel of Tiers 2, 3 and 4 who transfer from one facility to another, to the supervisors of record named for them, and to the division heads and command sections of the receiving facilities.

A temporary duty of thirty days or less is not a transfer and starts no probation.

## Policy

A transfer is followed by a probationary period of ninety days. Day 1 is the day the individual reports to the receiving facility.

Before opening any compartmented record at the receiving facility, the individual receives the compartment briefings required for the post under :x[OSC-DIR-0412]. Briefings held at the losing facility do not carry over.

The posting order names a supervisor of record for the probationary period. The supervisor of record is changed only by the head of the receiving division, with notice to Personnel & Vetting.

The probationer is reviewed at day 30 and at day 60 (interim reviews) and at day 90 (final review). A review falling on a day that is not a working day is held on the next working day.

While the probationary period runs:

- (a) the probationer may not hold an acting credential, except as provided in :x[OSC-DIR-0395] para 3(b);
- (b) the probationer may not request a further transfer. A request on :x[OSC-F-122] is returned to the requester unactioned, citing this directive;
- (c) leave is approved by the head of the receiving division and not by the supervisor of record, under :x[OSC-DIR-0189];
- (d) the probationer is not designated as an escort under :x[OSC-DIR-0163];
- (e) the probationer is not eligible for a named parking space under :x[OSC-DIR-0107].

The medical readiness certification, training record and security record of the probationer transfer with them and remain current to their original dates.

## Responsibilities

The Director, Personnel & Vetting, owns this directive, approves extensions of probation and decides the outcome of any final review that does not confirm the probationer.

The head of the receiving division names or changes the supervisor of record, approves the probationer's leave, and countersigns each review.

The supervisor of record writes the day-30, day-60 and day-90 reports, discusses each with the probationer, and forwards it through the Deputy Commander to Personnel & Vetting.

The Deputy Commander of the receiving facility sees each report before it is forwarded and may add remarks.

The probationer reads and signs each report. Signature records that the report has been read and not that it is agreed.

## Procedures

Each report covers conduct and performance in post, the compartment briefing record, training compliance under :x[OSC-DIR-0288], medical readiness under :x[OSC-DIR-0204], and the security record.

The day-30 report is forwarded within five working days of day 30. The day-60 report is forwarded within five working days of day 60. Interim reviews recommend continuation, or extension, and give reasons.

Overdue training or a medical readiness certification due to expire within the probationary period is noted in the report with the date by which it will be cleared.

An interim review may record that operational necessity exists in the probationer's post, for the purposes of :x[OSC-DIR-0395] para 3(b). The record is not itself a certification. Any certification is made by the Deputy Commander under that directive.

The final review at day 90 records one of three outcomes: CONFIRMED; EXTENDED, for a further period of up to ninety days, by the Director, Personnel & Vetting; or NOT CONFIRMED.

A probationer who is not confirmed is returned to the losing facility or posted as the Director, Personnel & Vetting, directs.

Reports and outcomes are kept on the individual's personnel file by Personnel & Vetting. The supervisor of record keeps no copy after the final review.
