## Purpose

This directive sets the rules for allocating office space, surface parking at the cover facility, and the electric cart bays below grade at ALPHA-class facilities.

It also fixes the composition and calendar of the allocation board at each facility, and the procedure for appeals.

## Applicability

This directive applies to all personnel posted to an ALPHA-class facility, to personnel on temporary duty there for more than five working days, and to contract staff who hold an entry on the facility access roster.

It does not apply to cover facility staff. Their parking is arranged by the cover facility's own administrative office under the procedures of the cover identity, and OSC personnel do not use spaces marked for them.

## Policy

Surface parking at the cover facility is a cover matter before it is a convenience. The number of vehicles parked at the surface on any working day is to remain consistent with the published staffing of the cover facility.

Allocation is therefore set to the vehicle ceiling for the facility and not to demand. The ceiling for each facility is :r[taken from the cover-identity catalogue entry for the facility and confirmed with Cover Operations at each cover review]{tier=2 auth="CVO" ref="OSC-ASR-COV"}. The ceiling is not posted with the allocation.

Surface spaces are allocated by grade and by shift, in this order of precedence: (a) the command section and the duty officer; (b) personnel whose watch begins or ends between 2200Z and 0600Z; (c) personnel holding a parking recommendation from the site medical officer; (d) all others by grade, then by date of posting.

No surface space is allocated by name for longer than one quarter. Personnel without an allocation park at the remote lot and use the facility shuttle, which runs to the published timetable at each watch change.

Personnel in a probationary period draw from the overflow lot. They are not eligible for a named space until the final review of their probation has been recorded under :x[OSC-DIR-0247].

Surface spaces allocated to OSC personnel are not reserved, marked or signed in any way that would distinguish them from the spaces of cover facility staff. Vehicles display the cover facility's own parking permit and no other.

Electric carts below grade are pool vehicles allocated to functions, not to individuals. Cart bays at the lift heads and on each level are assigned by the board; carts are signed out at the level control point and returned to the bay from which they were drawn. Personal vehicles are not taken below the vehicle lift head.

Office space below grade is allocated by grade to the standards in the table below. A request for space above the standard is supported by the division head and is decided by the board.

| Grade | Office standard | Surface parking |
|---|---|---|
| Command section | Private office, 150 sq ft | Named space, lot A |
| Tier 2 | Private office, 120 sq ft | Named space, lot A or B |
| Tier 3 | Workstation position, 64 sq ft | Lot B by shift, otherwise remote lot |
| Tier 4 | Shared position | Lot B by shift, otherwise remote lot |
| Probationary personnel, any tier | As for grade | Overflow lot |

## Responsibilities

The Facility Commander approves the allocation at each facility and decides appeals.

The Deputy Commander chairs the quarterly allocation board. When the Deputy Commander is absent the Chief of Staff chairs it, and the board record notes the substitution.

The facility administrative officer keeps the allocation register, the vehicle ceiling and the cart pool log; prepares the board papers; and posts the allocation at the personnel lift head and in MERIDIAN.

The facility security officer reports vehicles parked out of allocation and checks surface permits against the access roster kept under :x[OSC-DIR-0115].

Supervisors confirm the shift pattern of each of their personnel to the administrative officer by the fifteenth day of the month before each board.

## Procedures

The board sits in the first two weeks of February, May, August and November. Requests on the facility space request sheet reach the administrative officer by the last working day of the preceding month. Requests received later are considered at the following board.

The board considers the vehicle ceiling, the duty roster for the coming quarter, medical recommendations, changes of posting and requests carried over. It does not consider requests based on length of commute.

The allocation takes effect on the first Monday after the board sits. A holder of a named space who will not use it for five or more consecutive working days, including leave taken under :x[OSC-DIR-0189], releases it to the overflow pool through the administrative officer for the period.

A space vacated because its holder has left the posted strength of the facility is returned to the pool at the next board. It is not reallocated in the interim.

Appeals are made in writing to the Facility Commander within ten working days of the allocation being posted. The allocation stands while an appeal is considered.

Cart faults are reported at the level control point. A cart out of service is tagged at the bay and is not used until Facilities Engineering removes the tag. Fire lanes and the routes to refuge chambers fixed under :x[OSC-DIR-0156] are not obstructed by any cart bay or parked cart.
