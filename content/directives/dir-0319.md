## Purpose

This directive governs printing from MERIDIAN workstations, the release of held print jobs, cover sheets, and the reproduction of records by any means.

## Applicability

This directive applies to all MERIDIAN account holders, to every accredited printer and copier, and to any person who copies, prints, scans or photographs an OSC record.

## Policy

Every print job is held at the print server until released at the device by its owner. Nothing prints unattended.

Printing a document is reproducing it. A printed copy is a controlled document from the moment it leaves the device and carries the marking of the record it was printed from.

No record is reproduced except as this directive allows.

## Print release

Each job receives a print queue number in the form PQ-NNNN when submitted. The owner releases it by presenting the smart token at the device and selecting the job.

Jobs not released within four hours are purged from the print server. The purge is logged against the owner's account with the job number and document number. A purged job is not recoverable; it is submitted again.

Print release is suspended for any account whose annual security refresher is overdue (:x[OSC-DIR-0288]) until the refresher is completed.

During scheduled print server maintenance, jobs are held and released when the server returns. Maintenance windows are announced by the Service Desk.

## Cover sheets

Every printed document carries a cover sheet, printed first, in the colour of its marking:

| Marking | Cover sheet |
|---|---|
| TOP SECRET//SCI//OSC | Slate |
| UMBRA//OSC RESTRICTED | Ochre |
| UMBRA//OSC COMPARTMENTED | Oxblood |
| UMBRA//OSC UNRESTRICTED | Deep violet |

The cover sheet shows the document number, the marking, the job number, the time of printing and the account that printed it. It is kept on the document while it is outside a container.

Cover sheets for documents marked UMBRA//OSC COMPARTMENTED and above are printed in two parts. The second part, the cover sheet copy, is returned to the site registry counter on the day of printing and forms the print return for the site.

## Reproduction

A record is copied, printed in more than one copy, transferred to removable media or extracted in bulk only on an approved :x[OSC-F-142]. The request names the document, the number of copies and the purpose. Each copy is numbered.

A reproduction request is approved by the originator of the document, or by the division head of the requester where the originator has delegated approval.

Records whose distribution list states physical distribution only are not reproduced.

## Material marked UMBRA//OSC EYES ONLY

Material marked UMBRA//OSC EYES ONLY is not reproduced in any form. It is not photocopied, printed, scanned, photographed, transcribed, summarised or extracted, and it is not entered into MERIDIAN or any other information system, in whole or in part, by any person.

A request on OSC-F-142 for such material is refused and the refusal recorded. No cover sheet colour is assigned to it, because no printed copy of it can lawfully exist.

A reproduction of such material, wherever found, is not copied, forwarded or destroyed by the finder. It is reported under :x[OSC-DIR-0378] and left where it is.

## Responsibilities

The Director, Communications Security, is the office of primary responsibility, accredits printers and copiers, and sets the purge interval.

Information system security officers keep the device register, collect print returns and report unreleased-job patterns to the Resident Assurance Officer.

Registry counters receive cover sheet copies and file the print return daily.

Every account holder releases only their own jobs, collects them at once, and keeps the cover sheet on the document.

## Procedures

To print:

1. Select Print in the document window. Note the PQ number.
2. Go to the device. Present the token and release the job.
3. Collect all pages and the cover sheet before leaving the device.
4. For UMBRA//OSC COMPARTMENTED and above, return the cover sheet copy to the registry counter the same day.

Detailed instructions are in :x[KB-0019].
