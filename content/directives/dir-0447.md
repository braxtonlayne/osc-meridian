## Purpose

This directive sets the addressing of MERIDIAN mail, the handling of mail accounts of personnel no longer on a site, telephone practice below grade, and what users do when a link of the key distribution network is degraded or down.

## Applicability

This directive applies to all users of MERIDIAN mail and of OSC telephones at all staffed facilities.

## Policy

All OSC communications are carried on the key distribution network, which is independent of every other communications system (:x[OSC-ASR-COMM]). No OSC business is conducted on a cover facility telephone, a public telephone or any device not issued by Communications Security.

MERIDIAN mail is official correspondence. It is retained, it is subject to the audit trail, and it may be produced in an inquiry.

## Mail addressing

Personal addresses take the form `<initial>.<surname>@<site>.<division>.mer`, all in lower case, where the site is the short form of the facility identity (for example `a03`) and the division is the division code of the holder's post. Command sections use `cmd` in place of a division code.

Where two holders at one site and division would share an address, the second takes the next initial of their given names, and so on. Addresses are not changed on promotion.

An address belongs to the post-holder's account and moves with them on transfer. The old address is closed or retained under para 5.

For example, the Director of the issuing division is addressed `m.szabo@a02.cms.mer`.

Controlled mail is addressed only to persons eligible for its marking. The message transfer system refuses delivery to an ineligible address and tells the sender.

## Retained accounts

Accounts of personnel no longer on a site may be retained and do not accept mail. The decision to retain an account rests with the division that held it, and is not notified to correspondents.

Mail sent to a retained account is returned by the message transfer system with a non-delivery notice. It is not forwarded, held or read. The sender does not resend it to another address unless instructed by the division.

A retained account remains in the address list. Its entry is not marked.

## Telephones below grade

Below Level 0, telephones are answered with the extension number and section, for example "Six-two-one-seven, Stores Control". The facility name, the cover agency and the caller's name are not given.

Calls are not made from below grade to the cover facility's outside lines. Cover facility staff are reached through their own internal extension, and only on cover business.

Telephone directories are issued by the command section of each site and list that site only. A directory for another site is obtained through the switchboard, extension by extension, and is not copied.

Voice mail is not provided. Messages are taken in writing and placed face down.

## Key distribution network and link outages

The link state of each circuit is shown in the MERIDIAN notification area as UP, DEGRADED or DOWN.

When a link is DEGRADED, controlled mail to the sites beyond it is queued, not lost, and is delivered in order when the link recovers. Users do not resend queued mail.

When a link is DOWN, telephone calls to the sites beyond it are refused by the switch. Urgent business is passed through the duty officer, who holds alternative arrangements.

Links may be taken DOWN by Communications Security for maintenance with notice in the facility calendar, or without notice :r[on key compromise or on Council instruction, in which case the link state is shown as DEGRADED until the key is replaced]{tier=2 auth="CMS" ref="CMS-07-0109"}.

## Responsibilities

The Director, Communications Security, is the office of primary responsibility, operates the key distribution network and the message transfer system, and issues mail addresses.

Divisions decide whether accounts of their former personnel are closed or retained, and inform the Network Operations section.

Command sections issue and keep the site telephone directory.

Users address mail correctly, answer telephones as set out in para 6, and report a mis-delivered message under :x[OSC-DIR-0378].

## Procedures

1. To find an address, use the address list in the mail application. Do not guess an address from the scheme.
2. On a non-delivery notice, check the address. If it is correct, the account is closed or retained; contact the division concerned by telephone.
3. On a DEGRADED link state, carry on; mail is queued.
4. On a DOWN link state, call the duty officer for urgent business only.
