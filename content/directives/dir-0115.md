## Purpose

This directive governs the issue, wear, recovery and revocation of personnel badges; the access roster for each controlled area; the procedure when a badge is lost; and the issue of temporary escort badges.

## Applicability

This directive applies to all OSC personnel at every staffed facility, to contract staff issued an OSC badge, and to visitors issued a temporary escort badge under :x[OSC-DIR-0163].

Cover facility staff carry the identification of the cover agency and are outside this directive, except where an individual also holds an OSC badge.

## Policy

A badge is issued to each person on posting. It bears the holder's photograph, personnel number, tier stripe and the code of the issuing facility. A badge is not an access credential on its own. Entry to a controlled area also requires the smart token and passphrase.

Below the surface cover facility the badge is worn above the waist with the photograph outward at all times. At the surface cover facility the badge is not worn or displayed. It is carried out of sight and is never left in a vehicle, a locker at the surface, or a bag left unattended.

A badge presented at a reader for which it holds no authorisation is logged as a badge reader exception. Three exceptions in any thirty-day period are referred to the facility security officer, who interviews the holder.

The access roster lists the persons authorised unescorted entry to each controlled area. It is reissued on the first working day of each month and on any change of more than five names. Superseded rosters are destroyed as classified waste on the day of reissue under :x[OSC-DIR-0177].

The loss of a badge is reported to the facility security officer within one hour of discovery. The report is made in person or by telephone. A report made by MERIDIAN mail alone does not meet this requirement.

A second loss by the same holder within twelve months is a security violation and is reported under :x[OSC-DIR-0378].

A temporary escort badge is issued to a visitor admitted under :x[OSC-DIR-0163] and to a holder awaiting a replacement badge. It is valid for one duty period, opens no reader, and is returned to the issuing desk at the end of that period. Its holder is escorted at all times.

## Responsibilities

The Commandant, Security Forces Command, sets the badge specification and the reader configuration for all facilities.

The facility security officer keeps the access rosters and the badge register for the facility, receives loss reports, revokes and reissues badges, and holds the stock of temporary escort badges.

Supervisors notify the facility security officer within one working day of any posting into or out of their section, and of any change that affects an individual's entry on an access roster.

Each holder keeps their badge in their possession, wears it as required by paragraph 3.2, reports its loss as required by paragraph 3.5, and surrenders it when required.

## Procedures

On posting, the holder reports to the badge office with the posting order and the smart token issued by Communications Security. The photograph is taken at the badge office. The badge is issued against signature in the badge register.

The photograph is retaken every three years, and sooner when the holder's appearance has changed enough that the entry control point asks for it. Retakes are booked through the command section.

A damaged badge is exchanged at the badge office. The damaged badge is surrendered and destroyed in the presence of the holder.

On transfer, the badge is surrendered to the facility security officer of the losing facility on the last working day. The receiving facility issues a new badge on arrival. The holder does not travel with two badges.

A badge whose holder has left the posted strength of the facility for any reason is recovered by the facility security officer. Where the badge cannot be recovered it is revoked at the readers and entered on the access roster as NOT RECOVERED. The entry remains on each reissued roster and is not removed.

When a badge is lost:

1. The holder reports the loss to the facility security officer within one hour of discovery, stating when and where the badge was last seen.
2. The facility security officer revokes the badge at the readers and records the time of revocation.
3. The holder is issued a temporary escort badge and an escort for the remainder of the duty period.
4. The holder completes a security incident report on :x[OSC-F-114] before the end of the duty period.
5. If the badge has not been found after three working days, a replacement is issued. The replacement carries a new serial; the personnel number is unchanged.
6. A lost badge that is later found is handed to the facility security officer and is not used again, whether or not a replacement has been issued.
