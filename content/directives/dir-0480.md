## Purpose

This directive prohibits the use of tobacco below grade, in order to protect air handling plant, filter media and the sealed-condition air reserve.

## Applicability

This directive applies to all persons at every OSC facility with levels below grade, including visitors, contract personnel and persons in transit.

## Policy

Smoking, and the use of any tobacco product, is prohibited below Level 0. This includes cigarettes, cigars, pipes, chewing tobacco and snuff.

Lighters and matches are not taken below Level 1. They are surrendered at the portal security point on entry, against a numbered tag, and returned on exit.

Tobacco is used only in the designated areas at the surface.

## Designated areas

Each cover facility has one designated outdoor area, chosen by the facility commander with the cover facility manager so that it is consistent with the practice of the cover agency. Its location is posted at Level 1.

OSC personnel using the designated area do so as cover facility personnel would. They do not gather in numbers, do not discuss OSC business there, and do not remain there in working dress that is not worn by the cover facility's own staff.

No designated area is provided at any facility with no surface cover facility.

## Disciplinary scale

| Occurrence within 24 months | Action |
|---|---|
| First | Written advice from the supervisor, copied to the facility security officer |
| Second | Written warning from the division head; entered on the personnel record |
| Third | Referred to the facility commander; withdrawal of surface access privileges for 90 days |
| Any occurrence during a sealed condition | Referred directly to the facility commander |

Tobacco found below grade is confiscated and destroyed.

## Responsibilities

The Chief Engineer, Facilities Engineering, is the office of primary responsibility.

Facility commanders designate the surface area and enforce this directive.

Portal security points hold surrendered lighters and matches and keep the tag register.

Supervisors deal with first occurrences and report them.

## Procedures

On entry at Level 1, place lighters and matches in the tray and take the numbered tag. On exit, return the tag and collect them.
