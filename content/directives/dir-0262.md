## Purpose

This directive governs the booking and conduct of passenger journeys by rail and tram between OSC facilities.

## Applicability

This directive applies to all personnel travelling as passengers, to the officials who authorise their travel, and to platform staff. It does not apply to engineering personnel travelling within a possession, who travel under the possession arrangements.

## Policy

Travel between facilities by rail or tram is authorised by Transit Control on :x[OSC-F-109]. Surface travel is authorised by the division head and is not covered by this directive.

Passengers travel on the journey booked and no other. A passenger who misses a booked journey is rebooked; they do not board a later movement without a booking.

Passengers are not told the consist in advance. The consist identity, car and seat are given at the platform gate at boarding and not before. Booking confirmations state the platform, the departure time and the priority class only.

## Booking

A journey is requested on OSC-F-109 not less than two working days before travel, and approved by the traveller's division head before submission. Requests made at shorter notice are accepted on the authority of the division head's duty officer, stating the reason.

The form offers the destinations available from the traveller's site. A destination not offered on the form is not bookable by the traveller, and the Passenger Bookings desk will not discuss it.

Journey times are published in the working timetable in force and are calculated at :c[transitTech.railSpeed] for rail and :c[transitTech.tramSpeed] for tram. Passengers should allow 45 minutes at each terminal for the gate.

A booking is confirmed by mail with a receipt number. Changes are made by cancelling the booking and submitting a new form.

## Priority and seat allocation

Seats are allocated by priority class, and within a class in order of approval. A booking in a higher class may displace a booking in a lower class up to the time of boarding. A displaced passenger is notified at the gate and rebooked on the next available movement.

| Class | Applies to | Displaceable |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | :r[Movements on Council instruction, which are not shown in the passenger manifest]{tier=2 auth="TO" ref="TO-07-262"} | No |
| 1 | Command staff travelling on facility command business | By class 0 only |
| 2 | Duty travel with a suspense date: inquiries, inspections, briefings in person | By class 0 and 1 |
| 3 | Postings and transfers, with baggage | By class 0 to 2 |
| 4 | Training, conferences, routine liaison | By class 0 to 3 |
| 5 | Personal travel on approved leave, where permitted | By any higher class |

Medical evacuation is not booked. It is arranged by the site medical officer directly with Network Control and takes precedence over all classes.

## Baggage

| Item | Limit |
|---|---|
| Personal baggage, classes 1, 2, 4 and 5 | One bag, 30 lb, carried by the passenger |
| Personal baggage, class 3 (posting or transfer) | Two bags, 60 lb in total, plus one container sent as freight |
| Classified material | In a locked courier case, carried by the passenger, cover sheet inside |
| Medicines | In original packaging, declared at the gate |

Baggage is screened at the gate. Food, open liquids, lighters, matches and tobacco products are not carried (:x[OSC-DIR-0480]). Firearms are carried only by security forces personnel on duty, under their own post orders.

## Boarding and conduct in transit

Passengers present their badge and booking receipt at the gate. Platform staff confirm the booking against the passenger manifest and give the consist identity, car and seat.

Passengers remain seated with seat belts fastened while the consist is moving. They do not use the end cars unless directed, do not move between cars, and do not ask train staff about the route, other passengers or other movements.

Classified material is not read in transit.

Journeys over four hours are provided with a ration pack and water. Toilet facilities are in the centre car of each rail consist; tram consists have none.

## Responsibilities

The Director, Transit Operations, is the office of primary responsibility for this directive and for the working timetable.

The Passenger Bookings desk, Network Control, processes OSC-F-109, allocates seats and keeps the passenger manifest.

Division heads approve travel requests for their staff and assign the priority class.

Platform staff check passengers at the gate, give the consist and seat, and screen baggage.

## Procedures

1. Complete OSC-F-109 with origin, destination, date, purpose and priority class.
2. Obtain the division head's approval and submit the form.
3. On receipt of the booking confirmation, note the platform and departure time.
4. Report to the platform gate not later than 45 minutes before departure with badge, receipt and baggage.
5. Board as directed by platform staff.
