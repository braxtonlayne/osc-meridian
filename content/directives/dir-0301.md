## Purpose

This directive sets the rules for use of MERIDIAN workstations, the handling of smart tokens and passphrases, the documented terminal commands, and the accreditation of workstations by Communications Security.

## Applicability

This directive applies to every holder of a MERIDIAN account and to every workstation, printer and terminal connected to MERIDIAN at any site. It is acknowledged at first logon after each revision.

## Policy

MERIDIAN is provided for official business only. Every action at a workstation is recorded in the audit trail and attributed to the account in session. The audit trail may be read by its subject.

A workstation is used only by the holder of the account in session. Accounts are not shared, and a session is not left open for another person to use, including a supervisor or a member of Service Desk staff.

No software, media or device is connected to a workstation except by Communications Security. Removable media are prohibited except under a signed reproduction request (:x[OSC-DIR-0319]).

MERIDIAN has no connection to any network outside the OSC. Users do not attempt to reach one.

## Sessions, tokens and locking

A session is opened by inserting the smart token and entering the operator ID and passphrase. A temporary access card may be used in place of a token during the first 90 days of an account.

Removing the token locks the session at once. The session also locks after fifteen minutes without input. Session state is preserved while locked, and the session is reopened by the same operator only.

Before leaving the workstation, for any length of time, the user removes the token and takes it with them. A token left in a workstation is surrendered to the facility security officer and reissued only after the holder has been seen by them.

A lost token is reported to the MERIDIAN Service Desk and to the facility security officer within one hour.

## Passphrases

Passphrases are memorised. They are not written down except on the issuing envelope or temporary access card, which is kept on the person and returned when the permanent token is issued.

A passphrase is not told to anyone, including Service Desk staff, who will not ask for it.

A passphrase believed to be known to another person is changed at once at the workstation and the occurrence is reported under :x[OSC-DIR-0378].

## Documented terminal commands

The terminal is provided for lookup and navigation. The documented commands are listed by `help` and described by `man <command>`:

| Command | Use |
|---|---|
| `help` | List the documented commands |
| `whoami` | Show operator ID, tier, compartments held and site |
| `sites` | List the facilities shown to the user's tier |
| `site <id>` | Show the registry entry for one facility |
| `rail status` | Show the current state of the rail network board |
| `find <term>` | Search the library index |
| `open <docno>` | Open a document in its application |
| `mail` | Show the message summary |
| `audit tail` | Show the latest entries in the user's own audit trail |
| `clearance` | Show tier, compartments held and the briefing record |
| `man <command>` | Describe a command |

Each command is described in :x[KB-0026]. Output is subject to the same withholding as the applications (:x[OSC-DIR-0412]).

## Division lookup utilities

Lookup utilities provided by divisions (work orders, accessions, strength) remain available at the command line and are not listed in help. They are documented by the owning division in its own issuances.

Use of a lookup utility is recorded in the audit trail in the same way as any other command. A utility returns only what the user's tier and compartments permit; where it adds the user to the distribution of an item, the owning division's issuance says so.

## Accreditation

No workstation is connected to MERIDIAN until accredited by Communications Security. Accreditation is renewed every 24 months, and after any relocation, repair involving the case, or change of assigned user.

A workstation is accredited to one site and one room. It carries a label with its workstation number, in the form A02-6-031 (site, level, serial). The label is not removed or covered.

Accreditation records, including :r[the key-loading schedule for each token series and the workstations accredited to receive it]{tier=2 auth="CMS" ref="CMS-07-0204"}, are held by the Accreditation Branch.

## Responsibilities

The Director, Communications Security, is the office of primary responsibility, accredits workstations, issues tokens and passphrases, and maintains the documented command list.

Information system security officers at each site keep the workstation register, investigate unaccredited connections and recover tokens left in workstations.

The MERIDIAN Service Desk answers queries on use of the workstation and the documented commands. It does not answer queries on division lookup utilities, which go to the owning division.

Division heads ensure that their staff have read this directive and that workstations in their space are used only by their assigned users.

## Procedures

On first logon:

1. Insert the temporary access card or token and enter the issued operator ID and passphrase.
2. Read and acknowledge this directive and :x[OSC-DIR-0412].
3. Read :x[KB-0001], Getting Started with MERIDIAN 4.1.7.

On leaving the workstation, remove the token. On finding a workstation unlocked and unattended, do not use it. Press the lock key without touching anything else, then tell the facility security officer.
