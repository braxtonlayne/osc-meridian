## Purpose

This directive establishes that notebooks and other personal notes kept in the course of duty are official records, provides for their surrender and accession, and prohibits the private recording of operational indications.

## Applicability

This directive applies to all OSC personnel at all sites, on and off duty, and to notes made in any form: notebooks, loose sheets, diaries, calendars, the margins of other documents, and notes kept on any device.

It applies to notes begun before its effective date.

## Policy

Notes made by any person in the course of their duty, or about their duty, are official records of the OSC, whoever bought the notebook and wherever it is kept. They carry the marking of the most highly marked information in them, whether or not it is written on them.

Such notes are surrendered on request. A request may be made by the person's division head, the Resident Assurance Officer, or the Office of Internal Assurance, and need not give a reason.

The private recording of operational indications is prohibited. An operational indication is any reading, display state, board indication, log entry, movement or occurrence observed in the course of duty. Operational indications are recorded in the official log for the post and nowhere else.

A person who observes an indication that they consider irregular reports it under :x[OSC-DIR-0378]. Keeping a private record of it is not a substitute for reporting it and does not protect the person who keeps it.

## Notes permitted

The following are permitted, provided they contain no operational indication and no information about another site, its personnel or its connections:

- personal appointments, leave dates and shift patterns;
- telephone extensions within the person's own site;
- notes made during training, destroyed when the module is complete;
- working notes for a document being drafted, kept with the draft and destroyed or filed with it.

Permitted notes are still official records under para 3.1 and are still surrendered on request.

## Surrender and accession

On request, the person surrenders all notes within the scope of this directive at once and in full, including notes kept at their berth or in their locker. The person signs a list of the items surrendered.

Surrendered notes are transferred to Records Division and accessioned under :x[OSC-DIR-0336]. They are not returned to the person. Retention is under the series of the post's official log (:x[OSC-DIR-0354]) unless the Office of Internal Assurance directs otherwise.

Notebooks surrendered under this directive are :r[reviewed by the Office of Internal Assurance before accession, and entries corresponding to official log entries are compared with them]{tier=2 auth="OIA" ref="OIA-08-024"}.

Surrender is not of itself a finding against the person, and is not entered on the personnel record.

## Responsibilities

The Director, Office of Internal Assurance, is the office of primary responsibility.

Division heads ensure that every person in their division is advised of this directive. Where personnel keep a watch log, each watchkeeper is advised individually and signs an acknowledgement.

Resident Assurance Officers receive surrendered notes and transfer them to Records Division.

Records Division accessions surrendered notes on the day of receipt.

## Procedures

1. Read this directive and sign the acknowledgement presented by the supervisor or the Resident Assurance Officer.
2. Review any notes you hold. Notes containing operational indications are surrendered to the Resident Assurance Officer within five working days of advice. Do not destroy them.
3. Thereafter, record operational indications in the official log only.
4. On a request to surrender, hand over all notes at once and sign the list.
