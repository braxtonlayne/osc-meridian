## Purpose

This directive sets out who may be admitted to a facility under escort, how many visitors one escort may accompany, how escorts are designated and trained, and how visits are requested and recorded.

## Applicability

This directive applies at every staffed facility to all personnel who request, approve, record or escort a visit, and to every person admitted below the surface cover facility who is not on the access roster for the areas entered.

## Policy

A visitor is any person below the surface cover facility who does not hold an entry on the access roster for the area they are in. OSC personnel from another facility are visitors until entered on the roster of the facility they are visiting.

The following may be admitted under escort: OSC personnel of any tier on official business; contract staff holding a current clearance and a work order for the facility; and other persons named in writing by the Facility Commander. Cover facility staff are not admitted below the shaft or portal head under escort or otherwise.

Every visit is requested on :x[OSC-F-118] by the sponsoring division head not less than three working days in advance. The facility security officer approves or refuses the request and returns the receipt to the sponsor.

Escort ratios are fixed by area, as follows:

| Area | Visitors per escort |
|---|---|
| Controlled area (operations floors, records stacks, power plant, armory) | 1 |
| General areas below grade (corridors, offices, dining rooms) | 3 |
| Contract work party under a work order | 4, with Security Forces present |
| Surface cover facility, OSC visitors in transit to the lift head | 5 |

An escort remains within sight of each person escorted at all times and is accountable for them until they are signed out. An escort may not hand a visitor to another escort except at an entry control point, with both signing the visitor log.

Escorts are designated by the facility security officer from personnel posted to the facility for not less than six months who have completed escort training. Personnel in a probationary period are not designated as escorts.

:::redact{tier=2 auth="SFC" ref="OSC-ASR-PSEC"}
Escort of consists. A consist carrying any person who is not on the access roster of the receiving facility runs under Security Forces escort from platform to platform. The escort commander holds the passenger list and hands it to the facility security officer of the receiving facility at the platform; the passengers are entered in the visitor log there and not at the despatching facility. Guard personnel rotating to DELTA-class sites travel under Security Forces Command movement orders. They are not entered in the visitor log of any facility through which they pass, and their rotation dates and destinations are set in post orders and are not published in this directive or on the facility calendar beyond the changeover time.
:::

## Responsibilities

The sponsoring division head requests the visit, names the escort, and is answerable for the purpose of the visit.

The facility security officer approves visits, designates escorts, keeps the list of designated escorts, keeps the visitor log at each entry control point, and issues temporary escort badges under :x[OSC-DIR-0115].

Security Forces Command sets the escort training syllabus and provides escort training at each facility at least twice a year.

The escort meets the visitor at the entry control point, stays with them for the whole of the visit, and signs them out.

## Procedures

On arrival the visitor's identity is checked against the approved :x[OSC-F-118]. The visitor is entered in the visitor log with the time of entry, the escort's name and the areas approved, and is issued a temporary escort badge.

The visitor log is a bound register. Entries are made in ink and are not erased; a mistake is struck through once and initialled. The completed register is retained by the facility security officer for five years.

On departure the escort and the visitor both sign the log with the time of exit, and the temporary escort badge is returned. A visit that ends without a signed exit is reported by the facility security officer to Security Forces Command before the end of the duty period.

A visitor who becomes separated from their escort stops where they are and waits. The escort reports the separation to the facility security officer at once.

Escort training covers this directive, the escort ratios, separation and evacuation with a visitor under :x[OSC-DIR-0156]. Certificates are issued on completion and recorded by the facility security officer. Designation lapses if the escort has not escorted a visitor in twelve months.
