## Purpose

This directive sets the period for which each series of OSC records is kept, the placing and lifting of archive holds, the approved methods of destruction, and the witnessing and certification of destruction.

## Applicability

This directive applies to all records held by any division at any site, in any form, from the date they are created or received. It does not apply to numbered Council copies (para 8).

## Policy

Records are kept for the period in the schedule at para 4 and then destroyed, unless an archive hold is in place. Records are not kept longer than the schedule allows for convenience, and are not destroyed early to save space.

No record is destroyed except by an approved method, in the presence of a witness, and with a certificate signed at the time.

## Retention schedule

| Series | Description | Retention | At end of period |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Directives, charters and standing instructions | Permanent | Transfer to Stacks A |
| R2 | Personnel records, including vetting files | Permanent | Transfer to Stacks A |
| R3 | Workstation audit trails | Life of the individual's access plus seven years | Destroy |
| R4 | Security incident and inquiry files | As directed by the Office of Internal Assurance | Return to OIA |
| R5 | Divisional correspondence and memoranda | Ten years | Review, then destroy or transfer |
| R6 | Engineering drawings and segment sheets | Life of the asset plus ten years | Review |
| R7 | Work orders | Five years after closure | Destroy |
| R8 | Visitor logs and access rosters | Three years | Destroy |
| R9 | Dispatcher logs and incident logs | Ten years | Transfer to Stacks B |
| R10 | Burn bag and destruction logs | Two years after the last entry | Destroy |
| R11 | Leave, travel and training records | Four years | Destroy |
| R12 | Cafeteria, parking and space allocation records | One year | Destroy |
| R13 | Superseded registry media | Until listed and reviewed at processing | Review |

A record that falls in more than one series is kept for the longest of the periods.

## Archive holds

An archive hold suspends the scheduled destruction of a record or series pending review. Only Records Division places or lifts an archive hold, on its own motion or at the request of a division head, a withholding authority or the Office of Internal Assurance.

Holds are reviewed by the archive hold review board. Holds older than 24 months are confirmed or lifted at each review.

## Destruction

Approved methods are cross-cut shredding to the accredited particle size, pulping, and incineration. Burn bags are destroyed by incineration only. Registry media are degaussed and then physically destroyed.

Destruction is carried out by Records Division at ALPHA-03 or by the facility security officer at other sites, using equipment accredited for the highest marking of the material.

## Witnessing and certification

Each destruction is witnessed by a second person who is cleared for the material and is not the person destroying it. The witness observes the material from the time it is checked against the list until destruction is complete.

Destruction is certified on :x[OSC-F-137], signed by the destroying official and the witness at the time of destruction. No certificate is signed after the fact, and no certificate is signed by a person who did not see the destruction.

At ALPHA-03, witnessed destruction sessions are held on the second Wednesday of each month, 1400Z (1000 EDT) to 1600Z (1200 EDT), at the Records Division counter. Material for destruction is delivered to the counter by 1300Z (0900 EDT) on the day with its list.

Certificates are filed with Records Division against the accession number and kept under series R10.

## Council copies

The destruction of numbered Council copies, including on succession of Council membership under :x[OSC-CNL-CHR-II], is administered by the Secretariat and is not certified on OSC-F-137. Records Division does not witness, schedule or record the destruction of Council copies, and does not hold their certificates.

The Secretariat's certificate is held :r[in the register of distribution copies, by copy number and seat, and is entered before the successor's copy is issued]{compartment=QUARRY auth="CS" ref="CS-R-04"}.

A Council copy found in any place other than its holder's custody is not destroyed by the finder or by Records Division. It is reported in person to the Office of Internal Assurance.

## Responsibilities

The Chief, Records Division, is the office of primary responsibility, maintains the retention schedule and chairs the archive hold review board.

The Disposal Section of Records Division schedules destruction, provides witnesses on request and files certificates.

Division heads ensure that records due for destruction are listed and delivered, and that nothing is destroyed outside this directive.

Facility security officers carry out and certify destruction at sites other than ALPHA-03.

## Procedures

1. The holding division lists the records due for destruction by accession number and series.
2. The list is checked by the Disposal Section against holds.
3. The records and list are delivered to the counter by 1300Z (0900 EDT) on the session day.
4. The records are destroyed in the presence of the witness.
5. The destroying official and witness sign OSC-F-137 at once. The certificate number is entered against each accession.
