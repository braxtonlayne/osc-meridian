## Purpose

This directive sets the standard of medical readiness required of personnel serving below grade, the annual certification against that standard, and the consequences of a certification lapsing.

## Applicability

This directive applies to all OSC personnel posted to or on temporary duty at a staffed facility, whatever their tier.

## Policy

Medical readiness is the certification that an individual is fit for extended duty below ground and under seal. It is valid for twelve months from the date of the examination and expires at 2359 site-local time on the last day of the month in which it falls due.

The certification examination covers general fitness, hearing, lung function and respirator fit, vision, dental condition, and a structured interview on tolerance of confinement. It is recorded on :x[OSC-F-152] and signed by the site medical officer.

The seal roster for each facility lists the personnel who will remain at their posts if the facility goes to SEALED. Only personnel holding a current medical readiness certification are carried on it.

:r[Personnel not on the seal roster at the time a facility goes to SEALED are not admitted after the seal is set, and are directed by the duty officer to the alternative arrangements held by the command section.]{tier=2 auth="MS" ref="OSC-ASR-THR-02"}

When a certification lapses, the individual is removed from the seal roster on the following day. If thirty days pass without a new certification, the individual's access to below-grade posts is suspended until one is issued, and they report for duty at the surface or as their division head directs.

A certification issued at one facility is valid at every other. On transfer it is carried to the receiving facility and remains valid to its original expiry date. A transfer does not restart the twelve months.

## Responsibilities

The Director, Medical Services, owns this standard and approves any change to the content of the examination.

The site medical officer conducts examinations, issues certifications, keeps the medical readiness register for the facility and notifies expiries under paragraph 5.1.

The facility command section maintains the seal roster from the register and removes and restores names as certifications lapse and are issued.

Division heads arrange cover for personnel attending examinations and for personnel suspended under paragraph 3.5.

Each person keeps their own certification current and books their examination in time.

## Procedures

The site medical officer notifies each individual, and their division head, sixty days and again thirty days before their certification expires. The notice gives the dates on which appointments are available.

The individual requests an appointment on :x[OSC-F-152] not later than fourteen days before expiry. Appointment days are scheduled at least once a month at each facility with a clinic, and in the last two weeks of any month in which more than ten certifications expire.

The examination takes about three hours. Personnel attend in working clothes, bring their badge and any spectacles, and do not eat for two hours beforehand.

Where the site medical officer cannot certify, the individual is told the reason at the time and given a date for re-examination. The division head is told only that certification is deferred.

A certificate is not backdated. A certification issued after expiry runs for twelve months from the new examination.

Dosimetry for personnel working in reactor spaces is governed by :x[OSC-DIR-0216] and is reviewed at the certification examination. Special diets are reviewed under :x[OSC-DIR-0141].
