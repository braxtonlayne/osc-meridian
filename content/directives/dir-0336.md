## Purpose

This directive governs the receipt of records into the custody of Records Division at ALPHA-03, the assignment of accession numbers, the issue of custody receipts, the processing queue, and the tracing of a record's chain of custody.

## Applicability

This directive applies to Records Division and to every divisional registry that transfers records to it. It applies to paper records, registry media, personal notebooks surrendered as official records, and any other item received into the stacks.

## Policy

Every item received into the stacks is accessioned on the day of receipt. Nothing is held in Records Division space without an accession number, including items awaiting a decision on whether they should have been sent.

Custody passes to Records Division on signature of the custody receipt and not before. Until then the item remains the responsibility of the transferring registry, wherever it physically is.

Accessioning an item is not a judgement on its content, marking or provenance. Those are established at processing.

## Accession numbers

Accession numbers take the form RD-ACC-YY-NNNNN, where YY is the calendar year of receipt and NNNNN a serial issued in order of receipt, beginning at 00001 each January. A number is issued once and is not reused, including where the item is later returned, transferred or destroyed.

The accession number is written on the item, or on its container where the item cannot be marked, in pencil at the lower right corner of the first sheet.

Accessions for :r[the Special Collections Annex, Level 11,]{tier=1 auth="RD" ref="RD-07-0011"} are numbered in the same series and are not distinguished in it.

## Receipt

Items are received at the Records Division counter, Level 5, between 1300Z (0900 EDT) and 2000Z (1600 EDT) on working days. Items arriving by tram outside these hours are held in the receipt cage on the platform level under seal until the counter opens.

The receiving technician checks the transfer list against the items, enters the receipt and issues the accession number. Discrepancies are noted on the transfer list in ink and initialled by both parties.

Consignments of superseded registry media from divisional registries are accessioned as a single lot under one number. Their contents are listed at processing, not on receipt, and the transfer list need state only the number of containers and the originating registry.

## Custody receipts

A custody receipt is issued to the transferring registry for each accession, quoting the accession number, the transfer list reference and the number of items or containers. The registry retains it for the life of the transferred records.

Where an accession is later transferred, returned or destroyed, a further receipt is raised and linked to the first. The linked receipts form the chain of custody.

## Processing queue, Stacks C

Accessions received from 2006 onward are shelved in Stacks C, Level 7, in order of receipt pending processing. Processing comprises listing, checking the marking against the content, assigning a retention series under :x[OSC-DIR-0354], and shelving to the stack location.

The processing queue is worked in accession order. An accession is not taken out of order except on the instruction of the Chief, Accessions & Review Branch.

Stacks C is kept at 18 C and 40% relative humidity. Containers are not opened on the queue shelving; they are taken to the processing tables.

## Held accessions

An accession may be held on the instruction of the withholding authority for its content, or of the Office of Internal Assurance. A held accession is marked HELD on the queue with the reference under which it is held, and is not processed, moved or opened except as the holding authority directs.

A held accession remains on the library index while held so that access to it can be recorded (:x[OSC-DIR-0378]). Its catalogue entry is not amended until the hold is lifted.

## Chain of custody

The chain of custody of an accession may be traced at the workstation (`trace <accession>`). The trace lists each custody receipt in order, with date, transferring and receiving offices, and the hold status. Entries the user is not cleared to see are shown as withheld (:x[OSC-DIR-0412]).

Where the trace and the paper receipts disagree, the paper receipts govern and the discrepancy is reported to the Chief, Accessions & Review Branch.

## Responsibilities

The Chief, Records Division, is the office of primary responsibility for this directive.

The Chief, Accessions & Review Branch, manages the processing queue, assigns processing, and places and records holds.

Records technicians receive, accession and process items, and keep the counter log.

Divisional registries prepare transfer lists, keep custody receipts, and answer queries on the provenance of items they have sent.

## Procedures

To transfer records to Records Division:

1. Prepare a transfer list in duplicate. For superseded registry media, state the number of containers and the originating registry.
2. Seal the containers and deliver them to the counter during counter hours, or by tram under seal.
3. Obtain the accession number and custody receipt before leaving the counter.

To trace an accession, enter `trace` followed by the accession number at the terminal, for example `trace RD-ACC-08-00401`.
