## Purpose

This directive provides for the temporary elevation of an individual to the access of the next higher tier, for the duration of a named duty, by means of an acting credential.

## Applicability

This directive applies to Tier 3 and Tier 4 personnel at all staffed facilities. Elevation from Tier 2 is not made under this directive.

## Policy

An acting credential is issued only where a named duty cannot be performed at the holder's own tier and no person of the higher tier is available to perform it within the time the duty allows. It is not a reward, a step towards promotion, or a means of resolving a records request.

The following are not eligible to hold an acting credential:

<ol type="a">
<li>

Personnel serving a probationary period following transfer (:x[OSC-DIR-0247]), except as provided in subparagraph (b).

</li>
<li>

A probationer for whom the Deputy Commander of the gaining facility certifies in writing that the duty is an operational necessity and that no eligible person is available. The certification names the duty and is attached to the application. The interim review under :x[OSC-DIR-0247] is taken into account.

</li>
<li>

Personnel whose annual security refresher is more than 30 days overdue (:x[OSC-DIR-0288]).

</li>
<li>

Personnel who are the subject of an open inquiry under :x[OSC-DIR-0378], unless the inquiry officer consents in writing.

</li>
</ol>

The facility commander issues acting credentials and may delegate issue to the Deputy Commander. Issue is not delegated further.

## Effect of an acting credential

An acting credential confers the clearance of the higher tier. It does not confer the briefings associated with the higher tier, and the holder is not read into any compartment by reason of it (:x[OSC-DIR-0412]).

Records whose withholding requires the higher tier alone are displayed to the holder. Records whose withholding requires a compartment as well remain withheld unless the holder is separately briefed into that compartment.

The holder signs as "Acting" on any record they originate under the credential, and does not approve their own leave, transfer or training exemptions.

## Duration and lapse

An acting credential is issued for the named duty and lapses at the end of it, or at the end of 30 days, whichever is earlier. It may be reissued once for the same duty.

An acting credential lapses without notice at the end of the duty period :r[or on the instruction of the Office of Internal Assurance, which is not required to state a reason to the holder or the issuing officer]{tier=2 auth="OIA" ref="OIA-R-07"}.

On lapse, records displayed under the credential are no longer displayed. Notes, prints or extracts made under the credential are handled at the marking of the record from which they were made.

## Record

Issue, reissue and lapse are recorded in the audit trail and on the holder's personnel record. Every record opened under an acting credential is identified as such in the audit trail.

Acting credentials held are shown on the session panel of the holder's workstation while in effect.

## Application

Application is made on :x[OSC-F-126], selecting the acting designation in place of a compartment, and is sponsored by the applicant's supervisor of record. The sponsor states the named duty and why it cannot be performed at the applicant's tier.

An application may also be lodged at the workstation with the command `clearance request acting`, which forwards it to the supervisor of record for sponsorship. An application lodged in this way is refused by the workstation where the applicant is not eligible under para 3 at the time of lodging.

An applicant may withdraw an application at any time before issue. A refused application may be lodged again when the reason for refusal no longer applies.

## Responsibilities

The Director, Personnel & Vetting, is the office of primary responsibility for this directive and keeps the register of acting credentials.

Facility commanders, and Deputy Commanders where issue is delegated, decide applications and make the certification under para 3(b).

Supervisors of record sponsor applications only for duties they assign, and inform the Postings Section when the duty ends early.

Holders use the credential for the named duty only.

## Procedures

1. The supervisor of record identifies a duty that requires the higher tier.
2. The applicant applies under para 7.
3. For a probationer, the Deputy Commander's certification under para 3(b) is obtained before issue.
4. The issuing officer decides the application and enters issue or refusal. The applicant is notified by mail.
5. On completion of the duty the supervisor of record notifies the Postings Section, and the credential is lapsed.
