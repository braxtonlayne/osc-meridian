The two start-up years are stated on one sheet because the programme's account structure was not complete until the end of FY2002. FY2001 obligations total $:c[budget.fiscalYears.FY2001.totalM] million and FY2002 obligations $:c[budget.fiscalYears.FY2002.totalM] million.

Construction was initiated in January 2001 under Executive Order 13201 of 17 FEB 2001, whose operative provisions for the programme are in the Council's annex to it. The annex is a Council instrument and was not issued with the order. Obligations in FY2001 were for excavation, site survey and the first personnel lines only. In FY2002 tunnel boring began, and the first transit, equipment, DOE and reclassified obligations were made.

## Notes

1. The largest single obligation of FY2001 was :c[budget.lineItems.LI-001.fy.FY2001] million on LI-001, the alternate operating facility construction line, which carried the opening excavation contracts.
2. LI-018, Geotechnical Survey and Characterization, opened in FEB 2002 with :c[budget.lineItems.LI-018.fy.FY2002] million in the year.
3. LI-002, Facility Operations Staffing, obligated :c[budget.lineItems.LI-002.fy.FY2001] million in FY2001 and :c[budget.lineItems.LI-002.fy.FY2002] million in FY2002 for the first site caretaking posts. It is now among the larger personnel lines (:x[OSC-LDG-FY08]).
4. The first transit line in the register is LI-019, Guided Ground Transport Research. Transit obligations for FY2002 were for survey, tunnel lining procurement and conduit works ahead of boring. See :x[OSC-LDG-CAT-TRANSIT].
5. The account structure was completed in DEC 2002, when the DHS lines for FY2003 were prepared under transfer authority. See :x[OSC-LDG-FY03].
6. Lines opened in FY2002 were placed in parent accounts enlarged by the supplemental appropriations of :c[realEvents.RE-12.dateText] and :c[realEvents.RE-13.dateText]. Placement is set out in the Comptroller's note :x[OSC-MEM-02-0284].
