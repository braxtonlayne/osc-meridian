## Parent appropriations in the year

Two federal appropriations in the second half of the year affected the parent accounts of programme lines. Hurricane Katrina made landfall on 29 AUG 2005, and emergency supplemental appropriations for hurricane response followed in September. In DEC 2005, following the National Strategy for Pandemic Influenza, emergency funding for pandemic influenza preparedness was provided. Programme lines in the affected parent accounts posted in the ordinary way. No programme line received supplemental money.

:::redact{tier=2 auth="CVO" ref="CVO-07-104"}
Both appropriations were used as cover. The hurricane continuity line (LI-063, DOD) serves a BETA site presented as a FEMA hurricane continuity facility north of New Orleans. Surface activity at the site rose during the response and was kept at the higher level afterward; obligations on the line were raised from :c[budget.lineItems.LI-063.fy.FY2005] million in FY2005 to :c[budget.lineItems.LI-063.fy.FY2006] million in FY2006 and have risen since, inside the general increase in hurricane continuity spending. The medical countermeasures stockpile (LI-064) and the pharmaceutical shelf-life line opened this year (LI-120) were presented, where questioned, as part of the general growth in federal countermeasure procurement after December 2005. LI-064 continued on its planned profile; the Ledger records no change to it, and no question was received.
:::
