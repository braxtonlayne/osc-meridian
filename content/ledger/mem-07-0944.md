::stamp{kind="RECEIVED" date="2007-11-08" text="COMPTROLLER ALPHA-01" seed=3}

1\. In response to reference (a), the engineering lengths below are provided for the FY2008 transit cost tables. Lengths are surveyed route-miles at track centreline as closed out under reference (b). They are the figures used by Facilities Engineering for maintenance planning, possession booking and lining inspection intervals, and by Network Control for the dispatcher board.

2\. Rail segments. Lengths are given for all six segments, irrespective of revenue status. Segment 04 is included at its surveyed length (reference (c)).

::canon-table{source="railSegments" cols="no:Seg,a:From,b:To,routeNote:Corridor,miles:Route-miles,completed:Completed,status:Status"}

Rail, six segments: :c[headline.railEngineeringMi] route-miles.

3\. Regional tram networks. Network totals are the stated totals of the four network sheets and include every link, spur and BETA/GAMMA branch.

::canon-table{source="tramNetworks" cols="name:Network,statedTotalMi:Route-miles"}

Tram, four networks: :c[headline.tramEngineeringMi] route-miles.

Engineering total, rail and tram: :ink[:c[anomalies.TUNNEL_847.figures.engineeringTotalMi]]{mark=circle} route-miles.

4\. The certified mileage (:ink[:c[headline.tunnelCertifiedMi]]{mark=circle} miles) reported to the Council in the quarterly return by Transit Operations remains as returned and is not affected by this memorandum. Facilities Engineering does not prepare the certified return and does not hold its working papers. Engineering lengths are not to be substituted for it in Council material.

:::redact{compartment=KESTREL auth="FE" ref="FE-06-0311"}
5\. Portal environmental notes. Portal blocks at both ends of every segment are logged for CO2, temperature and relative humidity on the FE walk-down sheets and are not entered in the length tables. Segment 04 portal seals (ALPHA-02 portal and ALPHA-05 platform 7) are walked down monthly under possession booked by FE; readings during a possession are recorded against the possession number, not against a consist. Segment 04 ventilation runs on the possession schedule only. No change to the Segment 04 length results from the walk-downs.
:::

6\. Lengths are stated to the nearest whole mile and are not to be re-derived from schedule times or design speeds. Queries on the lengths go to the FE action officer, O. Garrity, Tunnel Structures Engineer, extension 2-3318. Queries on the certified figure go to Transit Operations. Suspense for the FY2008 cost tables is 30 NOV 2007, per reference (a).
