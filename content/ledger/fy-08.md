FY2008 is stated at $:c[budget.fiscalYears.FY2008.totalM] million: obligations posted to 31 MAR 2008 plus the apportioned balance of the year. Sixty-eight lines remain open. Every open line is expected to close by the end of FY2008 except those carrying operating costs, which will be transferred to sustainment accounts under the Phase 4 account structure.

Personnel Costs reached their highest year. Strategic Reserves rose again, as they have in every year since FY2003. Transit Network has been the largest category in each year since FY2007, as Facility Construction falls to alteration, planning and continuity site works.

## Notes

1. The largest obligation in the year is :c[budget.lineItems.LI-072.fy.FY2008] million on LI-072, Tunnel Boring Technology Demonstration, for the transfer of boring machines to storage and the closeout of their maintenance contracts.
2. The largest Facility Construction lines in the year are LI-035, Continuity Facilities Planning and Design (:c[budget.lineItems.LI-035.fy.FY2008] million), and LI-063, Hurricane Continuity Site Development (:c[budget.lineItems.LI-063.fy.FY2008] million).
3. The workstation environment line (LI-121) carries :c[budget.lineItems.LI-121.fy.FY2008] million in the year.
4. Pay runs for APR and MAY 2008 will be posted on the last working day of each month against the strength return certified for that month (:x[OSC-PER-IDX]). :r[Changes of pay category effective 31 MAY 2008 will be reflected in the second quarter statement without restatement of prior months.]{tier=2 auth="PV" ref="OSC-DIR-0233"}
5. The Phase 4 account structure (FY2009 to FY2012) is in preparation and is not stated here.
