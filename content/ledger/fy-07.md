FY2007 obligations total $:c[budget.fiscalYears.FY2007.totalM] million on 97 lines, the first fall in the programme's annual total. The last two rail segments were completed: segment 06 in FEB 2007 and segment 04 in MAR 2007 (:x[OSC-TRN-SEG-06], :x[OSC-TRN-SEG-04]). Boring finished during the year and tram works began to taper. Equipment & Systems had its highest year as fit-out followed structural completion. Twenty-nine lines closed at year end.
