FY2004 obligations total $:c[budget.fiscalYears.FY2004.totalM] million on 114 lines. Thirty-four lines were opened. All 43 Facility Construction lines were open during the year, for the first time. Rail segment 01 was completed in DEC 2004, the first segment to be completed (:x[OSC-TRN-SEG-01]).

## Notes

1. The largest obligation in the year was :c[budget.lineItems.LI-106.fy.FY2004] million on LI-106, Auxiliary Facility Construction, Strategic Forces, a FEMA line in its first year.
2. Traction power (LI-056) fell from its FY2003 opening level as the first substations were completed ahead of segment 01.

4. The segment 01 completion was accepted on engineering certificate. No cost was posted against acceptance.
