1\. This note records how the lines opened in FY2002 were placed in the parent accounts of the four departments, and why the placement does not need to be revisited after the supplemental signed on :c[realEvents.RE-13.dateText].

2\. Public record. :c[realEvents.RE-12.publicEvent] :c[realEvents.RE-13.publicEvent] The Office took no part in the preparation of either measure and holds no papers on them other than the published texts.

3\. Placement. Obligations for FY2002 total $:c[budget.fiscalYears.FY2002.totalM] million, against $:c[budget.fiscalYears.FY2001.totalM] million in FY2001. Thirty-three lines were opened in the year (:x[OSC-LDG-FY01]). Each was placed in a parent account that grew in the year by more than the line, so that in no account is a programme line the largest single movement. Lines of a homeland security character were placed on DOD and FEMA accounts, there being no department of homeland security. The rule followed is the one used for appropriations questions in :x[OSC-ASR-THR-05]: a parent department can answer for a line within its cover title without reference to the size of the account.

4\. Effect of the second supplemental. None. The placement made in the schedule of reference (a) remains within the growth of every account concerned. No line is to be moved.

:::redact{compartment=QUARRY auth="CMP" ref="CMP-01-0088"}
5\. The placement follows plan CMP-01-0088, Rev 1, prepared at the request of the Secretary to the Council and held with the papers of the delegation (:x[OSC-CNL-DEL-01-04]). The plan assumed parent account growth of the size that followed and named the accounts to be used. Reference (a) was drawn from it unchanged.
:::

:::redact{tier=1 auth="COUNCIL" ref="CC-ART-III"}
6\. Plan CMP-01-0088 is dated 22 JUN 2001.
:::

7\. Queries on placement come to the Comptroller. Cover titles are not to be changed to follow the accounts; the accounts follow the titles. Suspense for the FY2003 opening schedule is 15 NOV 2002.
