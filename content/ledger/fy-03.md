FY2003 obligations total $:c[budget.fiscalYears.FY2003.totalM] million on 80 lines. Thirty-four lines were opened, the most in any year, among them the first eleven DHS lines and the first Strategic Reserves lines.

## DHS lines and transfer authority

The Department of Homeland Security began operations on 24 JAN 2003. Its first full appropriation was for federal fiscal year 2004. Programme amounts on DHS lines in FY2003 were therefore obligated under transfer authority from predecessor accounts, at the sub-accounts the department later adopted. No obligation was recorded on a DHS line before 24 JAN 2003.

FEMA was transferred into the department on 01 MAR 2003. The FEMA lines open at that date continued on the same accounts and keep the FEMA label in the register (:x[OSC-LDG-SRC-FEMA], note 1). Two BETA cover identities changed parent department on paper with the transfer. Nothing changed at either facility, and no line was moved.

## Notes

1. The largest obligation in the year was :c[budget.lineItems.LI-056.fy.FY2003] million on LI-056, Traction Power Substations, a DHS line obligated under transfer authority.
2. The first reserves lines (blood products, water treatment consumables, seed collection preservation, stocks, commodity rotation and the medical countermeasures stockpile) opened as the first facility storage became available. See :x[OSC-LDG-CAT-RESERVES].
3. During the northeastern power outage of 14 AUG 2003, construction-phase diesel plant at two shelter sites ran for the duration without load-shedding. Fuel was charged to the site accounts. The event was later cited in the FY2005 justification for the diesel generation plant line.
4. Long-lead equipment deliveries to two BETA sites in MAY 2003 were scheduled in the week of a national counter-terrorism exercise in the same regions. Freight charges were posted in the ordinary way.
