FY2006 obligations total $:c[budget.fiscalYears.FY2006.totalM] million, the highest of any programme year. The last ALPHA construction contracts closed out during the year, and three rail segments were completed: segment 03 in AUG, segment 05 in SEP and segment 02 in NOV 2006 (:x[OSC-TRN-SEG-03], :x[OSC-TRN-SEG-05], :x[OSC-TRN-SEG-02]). Transit Network had its highest year. Eighteen lines closed at year end, eleven of them Facility Construction.
