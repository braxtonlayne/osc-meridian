## Summary

Facility Video shows pictures from the facility's security cameras, live or recorded, on your workstation. The cameras belong to the site security office. You can watch them and, where your access allows, move the pan-tilt-zoom (PTZ) cameras to a stored preset. You cannot record, delete or export video from the client.

What you may view is set by :x[OSC-DIR-0474]. A camera you may not view is shown in the camera list with **no access** after its name. Selecting it is refused and the refusal is recorded.

## The window

- **Camera list** (left). Cameras are listed by site, then by level. Choose **View, Cameras by recorder** to list them by recorder unit and channel instead. Double-click a camera, or select it and press Enter, to put it on the selected channel of the video wall. Double-click a site to fill the wall with that site's cameras.
- **Video wall** (centre). Choose 1, 4, 9 or 16 channels from the toolbar or with Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+4. Click a channel to select it; double-click to show it alone. The selected channel has a blue border.
- **Camera control** (lower left). Presets and direction buttons for the selected camera, when it is a PTZ camera.
- **Status bar.** The recorder channel of the selected camera, your access to it, and LIVE or PB (playback) with the picture time.

The picture refresh rate drops as you add channels: six pictures a second on a single camera, two a second on a 16-way wall. This is normal. The client also stops drawing when its window is minimised or hidden, and resumes when you return to it.

## Reading the picture

The recorder writes the camera title, the date and the site time into every picture. The time is the camera site's local time, not your workstation's. Tags in the top right corner:

- **REC**: the channel is recording.
- **PB x4**: you are watching recorded video at four times normal speed.
- **ALM 1**: the recorder's alarm input is active. At facility sites this input is wired to the seal alarm.

A channel that shows **NO LINK** cannot be reached across the network, usually because the site is isolated. **NO RECORDED VIDEO** in playback means the recorder holds nothing for that interval. **NO PERMISSION** means your access does not cover the camera.

## Pan-tilt-zoom cameras

PTZ cameras have stored presets. Preset 1 is HOME. Recall a preset with its button, from the **Camera** menu, or by pressing its number with the video wall selected. The arrow keys pan and tilt in small steps, and + and - zoom.

Moving a camera moves it for everyone watching it, including the site security control room. A camera left off HOME returns there by itself after ten minutes. Every move is recorded with your operator number.

## Playback

Choose **Playback** on the toolbar, or press Space with the video wall selected. Playback starts five minutes back on every channel of the wall. The recorders keep the last hour available to the client; older video is held by the site security office.

- Click in the time bar to go to that moment. The marks in the bar are recorder events on the selected channel: thin marks for motion, heavier marks for alarms, doors and trains. A shaded band is an interval with no recorded video.
- Click an event in the list to go to it.
- Change speed with the **Speed** box or the **Playback** menu. Press Esc or choose **Live** to return to live video.

## Video stills

Choose **Still** on the toolbar (Ctrl+S) to capture the selected channel as it is shown. The still is given a number (VS-08-) and can be printed. Printed stills are controlled records: collect them from the printer at once and hand them to your section registry. The site security office is told of each still you print.

## When to call the service desk

Call the MERIDIAN Service Desk if every channel shows NO LINK while the network is not isolated, or if the picture time on a channel differs from the clock by more than a minute.
