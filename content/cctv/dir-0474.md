## Purpose

1. This directive sets who may view facility security video at a MERIDIAN workstation, and how cameras, recorded video and video stills are handled there. It applies to every account holder with the Facility Video client.

## Ownership

2. The cameras and recorders at each site belong to that site's security office. The security control room has first call on every camera. A workstation viewer watches the security office's cameras with its permission and gives way to the control room without argument.

3. Security video is not a record of the viewer's section. It is not copied, recorded or described in a section record except as allowed in para 7.

## Display of video

4. Video is displayed only on the workstation screen. It is not shown to a person not entitled to view it under para 5, whether by turning the screen or by any other means.

5. Access to video from a camera is decided per camera, in this order:

   a. **Level.** A camera viewing a level held at a given tier is displayed only to an operator holding that tier or higher. This applies at the operator's own site as at any other.

   b. **Own site.** An operator may view the cameras of the site to which the account is posted. Tier 4 accounts may view their own site's cameras and no others.

   c. **Hub portal and platform feeds.** Tier 3 operators at an ALPHA site may view the portal and rail platform cameras of the other ALPHA sites, so that movements between the hub sites can be followed.

   d. **Other cameras at other sites.** Interior cameras at another site are displayed only to an operator holding Tier 2, or briefed into the SEXTANT compartment under :x[OSC-DIR-0412].

   e. **Review.** Assurance officers holding review access may view any camera for review. Review access is view only.

6. A request the rules do not allow is refused by the client and the refusal is recorded. Repeated refusals are reviewed by the Office of Internal Assurance.

## Control of cameras

7. PTZ cameras are moved from a workstation only at the operator's own site, or by a Tier 2 operator, and only to a stored preset or in small steps. Moves are recorded. A camera left off its home preset returns there on its own. Video stills printed from the client are controlled records: each is numbered, its printing is notified to the site security office, and it is handed to the section registry on the day it is printed.

## Recording interruptions

8. A recorder that stops recording on any channel enters the interval in its event list. The interval shows as NO RECORDED VIDEO in playback. Interruptions not caused by maintenance are reported to the site security office and the site command on the day, with the channel, the times and the recorder's event text.

## Camera schedule

9. Every recorder channel in use has a line in the camera schedule (Annex A, held by each site security office and loaded into the client). A channel without a line in the schedule is a spare and carries no picture. Security offices report any picture on a spare channel to Computing and Media Services, ALPHA-02, who maintain the recorders.

10. Access under para 5 is set from the camera schedule. Video maintenance staff confirm with the security office before a camera is added to a recorder.

## Annex A

11. Annex A (camera schedule) is distributed to site security offices only. Each site's schedule lists its cameras by recorder unit and channel, with the level, location, type and the tier of the level the camera views.
