## 1. Threat

**:c[threatDetail.INT-WHISTLEBLOWER.label].** A person admitted to the OSC discloses, or prepares to disclose, the Office or its facilities to a person not admitted to it.

## 2. Contingency

2.1 Personnel vetting and monitoring protocols minimise risk. The OSC supplement to the SSBI covers multi-generational family citizenship, financial independence from foreign-connected entities, psychological assessment for ideological reliability and social network mapping (:x[OSC-ASR-PSEC]).

2.2 Contingency protocols exist.

:::redact{compartment=BRIAR auth="OIA" ref="OIA-PROT-06"}
2.3 Dispositions under the protocol are recorded in personnel accounting as a change of category.
:::

:::redact{tier=1 auth="COUNCIL" ref="CC-ART-IV"}
2.4 Disposition is determined by the Director, Office of Internal Assurance, on the assessment of the Resident Assurance Officer, and is not subject to review below the Council. Personal effects, mail accounts and personnel numbers are retained.
:::
