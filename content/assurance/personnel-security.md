## 1. Purpose

This protocol states how the Office of Internal Assurance and Personnel & Vetting establish and keep confidence in the persons admitted to the OSC: before admission, on posting and transfer, and for as long as access is held. It is read with the vetting supplement (:x[OSC-PER-VET]) and the transfer rules (:x[OSC-PER-TRF]), which it does not repeat.

## 2. Investigation

2.1 :c[security.personnel.investigation] The supplement is applied in addition to the standard investigation and is completed before admission at any tier. Its four focus areas are:

| Focus area | Investigating office | Re-examined |
|---|---|---|
| Multi-generational family citizenship verification | Personnel & Vetting | On admission only, unless a reportable change occurs |
| Financial independence from foreign-connected entities | Personnel & Vetting | At periodic reinvestigation, and on any reportable change |
| Psychological assessment for ideological reliability | Personnel & Vetting, Medical Services | At periodic reinvestigation |
| Social network mapping | Personnel & Vetting, Office of Internal Assurance | Continuously (section 6) |

2.2 Investigation files are held by Personnel & Vetting at ALPHA-01. They are not held at the individual's site and are not available to the individual's supervisor.

## 3. Compartmentation

3.1 :c[security.personnel.compartmentation]

3.2 Each tier is cleared to the marking shown and to no higher marking. Compartments are additional to tier and are granted only under :x[OSC-DIR-0412].

::canon-table{source="tiers" cols="tier:Tier,name:Designation,clearance:Clearance,knowledgeScope:Scope of awareness"}

3.3 Knowledge acquired by a person in the course of duty that lies outside the scope of their tier is not to be recorded, discussed or acted on, and is reported to the Resident Assurance Officer. Such a report is not a security violation.

## 4. Transfer and probation

4.1 A transfer between facilities is treated as a new admission to the receiving facility. The individual receives the receiving facility's compartment briefing before taking up duty and is on probation for 90 days from the date of transfer. Probation and its interim reviews are governed by :x[OSC-PER-TRF] and :x[OSC-DIR-0247].

4.2 During probation the individual may not hold an acting credential, approve leave for others, sponsor a compartment request, or act as an escort.

4.3 The supervisor of record completes the day-30 and day-60 reports. The Resident Assurance Officer contributes a paragraph to each report from the audit trail review in section 5. The individual is not shown that paragraph.

## 5. Workstation audit trail review

5.1 Every MERIDIAN workstation writes an audit trail of windows opened, searches made, documents printed and redactions examined (:x[OSC-DIR-0301]). The trail is retained for the life of the individual's access plus seven years and may be read by its subject.

5.2 Resident Assurance Officers review the audit trails of their site weekly. Audit trails of probationary personnel are reviewed daily.

5.3 Where review shows access to an item under inquiry, or to material outside the reader's apparent need, the Resident Assurance Officer may write to the individual directly. Such a message is a record of access, not an allegation. It is copied to the supervisor of record.

5.4 Self-reports made under :x[OSC-DIR-0378] before a review identifies the matter are considered in mitigation.

:::redact{compartment=BRIAR auth="OIA" ref="OIA-PROT-06"}
5.5 Review is not limited to the audit trail. Mail, print release records, badge transactions at controlled doors, terminal history and the content of searches are available to the Resident Assurance Officer and are correlated by personnel number.

5.6 The audit trail as shown to its subject omits entries made by the Office of Internal Assurance in the course of review.
:::

## 6. Continuous evaluation

6.1 The following changes are reported by the individual to Personnel & Vetting within five working days, on the form shown. Reports are routed through the Resident Assurance Officer.

| Change | Report on |
|---|---|
| Marriage, divorce, cohabitation, or a new member of the household | PV notification (no form; by mail to Personnel & Vetting) |
| Foreign travel by the individual or a member of the immediate family | PV notification |
| Contact with a foreign national beyond incidental contact | PV notification |
| Debt in default, garnishment, bankruptcy, or a large unexplained receipt | PV notification |
| Arrest, charge or summons | PV notification, same day |
| Loss of a badge, token or controlled key | :x[OSC-DIR-0115]; lost badge procedure |
| Any security violation by the individual | OSC-F-160 under :x[OSC-DIR-0378] |
| Any irregular occurrence observed by the individual | OSC-F-114 under :x[OSC-DIR-0378] |

:::redact{compartment=BRIAR auth="OIA" ref="OIA-PROT-06"}
6.2 Social network mapping continues after admission. Contacts are drawn from mail traffic, visitor logs, travel authorizations and the declarations above, and the map is refreshed at each periodic reinvestigation and whenever a person on it is the subject of an inquiry.
:::

6.3 Failure to complete the annual security refresher (:x[OSC-DIR-0288]) suspends print release until completed. It is not otherwise reportable.

## 7. Debriefing

7.1 On release from the OSC, or on withdrawal of a compartment, the individual is debriefed by the Resident Assurance Officer and signs an acknowledgement that the obligations of service continue. The acknowledgement is filed by Personnel & Vetting.

7.2 Persons whose category changes other than by posting are debriefed by the Office of Internal Assurance and not by their site.
