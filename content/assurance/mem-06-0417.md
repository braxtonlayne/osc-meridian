::stamp{kind="RECEIVED" date="2006-09-27" text="OIA ALPHA-01" seed=6}

1\. Intelligence & Analysis summaries for the quarter record a further increase in published commentary on the events of :c[realEvents.RE-10.dateText], around the fifth anniversary. The commentary is produced by independent researchers: books, pamphlets, films offered for sale, public meetings, and a large number of web sites. Public observances of the anniversary are not monitored and are excluded from the count.

2\. The commentary attributes the events to departments and agencies of government, to foreign governments and to persons in public life. It does not name the Office, a facility, a cover identity or a programme line item. It has not referred to the continuity build, the network or the Council in any form, and the IAD summaries have not reported an item under threat register entry 07 (:x[OSC-ASR-THR-07]) arising from it.

3\. The researchers disagree with one another more than with the official account. Each new claim is published, answered and found wanting in public, generally within weeks. The effect over five years has been to make the question itself a mark of the people who ask it. Serious inquiry into the period now has to begin by distinguishing itself from them, and mostly does not attempt to.

4\. Two cover agencies have received requests under the public records statutes that mention the events of that date. Both were answered by the cover agencies in the ordinary way, within the published cover story, and neither touched a site.

5\. Recommendation. No action is to be taken to discourage independent researchers. No statement, correction, contact or approach is to be made to any of them, by any office, in any form.

we should encourage these guys
:::

:::redact{compartment=BRIAR auth="OIA" ref="OIA-PROT-07"}
6\. No prepared statement is held under OSC-CP-07 for these events and none is to be prepared. Where a published account comes near the programme, the protocol's ordinary rule applies: the account is answered by further publication, and the more accounts there are, the less any one of them is worth (:x[OSC-ASR-THR-07] para 2.3). The present commentary does that work without assistance.
:::

:::redact{tier=1 auth="COUNCIL" ref="CC-ART-III"}
7\. The Secretary has been told of this recommendation. The delegation has not been asked to consider it and is not to be. OSC-CP-08 is not to be cited on this file.
:::
