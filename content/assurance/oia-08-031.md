## Description (as reported)

Reported in person to the Resident Assurance Officer, 05 MAY 2008 1448Z (1048 EDT), by T. Barraclough, and entered by the action officer from the reporter's statement:

"During the custody receipt check of the Stacks C processing queue I opened accession RD-ACC-08-00412, a consignment of superseded registry media. Among the routine registry images was a scanned image of four sheets of a Council numbered-copy document marked UMBRA//OSC EYES ONLY, titled Facility Inventory and Logistics Network, Revision 2008-Q2. Parts of the sheets have been blacked out by hand before scanning. The image had been loaded to the library index with the rest of the consignment. I closed it at the title sheet and did not read further. I reported it to my branch chief and to the RAO."

The item is an electronic image of a record distributed only in numbered physical copies, with no electronic copies permitted. The provisions engaged are :x[OSC-ASR-COMM] section 4 (no EYES ONLY material in any information system) and :x[OSC-DIR-0319] (no reproduction of EYES ONLY material in any form).

## Handling direction

Direction of the Director, Office of Internal Assurance, 07 MAY 2008:

The item remains on the library index pending the inquiry so that access to it can be recorded. It is not to be removed, renamed, re-marked further or destroyed by Records Division or the MERIDIAN Service Desk. Accession RD-ACC-08-00412 is held and is not to be processed. Opening the item is not prohibited to readers otherwise entitled to the index; each opening is recorded.

Persons who have opened the item and have not been interviewed may report it on OSC-F-160 (:x[OSC-F-160]). Self-reports under :x[OSC-DIR-0378] are considered in mitigation.
