Owning office: :c[threatDetail.INT-CONGRESS.ownerDivision]. The owning office is not a division of the OSC; it is constituted by Council resolution and draws its staff from the Council Secretariat. Correspondence for it is addressed through the Secretariat.

## 2. Contingency

2.1 Legal frameworks are established. Classification authorities prevent disclosure even under subpoena. Congressional oversight committees possess no awareness of programme existence.

2.2 Questions arising from appropriations review are answered at line-item level by the parent department, within the cover title of the line item concerned (:x[OSC-LDG-REG]). The Comptroller supplies the parent department with figures for the line item only. No OSC office corresponds with a committee or its staff.

:::redact{compartment=BRIAR auth="OIA" ref="OIA-PROT-05"}
2.3 Personnel called to give evidence in their cover employment give it in that employment. They are briefed beforehand on the scope of that employment and debriefed afterward. The debriefing is entered on the individual's vetting file (:x[OSC-PER-VET]) and is not otherwise reported.

2.4 Where a question put to a witness cannot be answered within the cover employment, the witness answers that the matter is outside their duties. Counsel accompanying the witness is provided through the cover agency and is not briefed.
:::

## 3. Handling of appropriations questions

| Step | Action | Office | Suspense |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Question received by the parent department and passed to the line-item account holder. | Parent department | As set by the department |
| 2 | Account holder requests figures for the line item. | Parent department to Comptroller, by the cover channel | 5 working days |
| 3 | Comptroller supplies figures for that line item only, in the format of the department's own returns. | Comptroller (CMP/ALPHA-01) | 3 working days |
| 4 | Parent department answers within the cover title. | Parent department | As set by the department |
| 5 | Question and answer filed against the line item; copy to this register. | Comptroller | On completion |

Programme totals are not supplied to any parent department in any form (:x[OSC-LDG-IDX]).
