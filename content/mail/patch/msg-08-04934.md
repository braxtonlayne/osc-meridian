COMMUNICATIONS SECURITY\
Network Operations, ALPHA-02

1. A repatch of a trunk or network circuit has been made at a hub patch bay from a MERIDIAN position that does not hold repatch authority. No circuit order was held. Report made under :x[OSC-MEM-07-1186] para 5.

2. The cord is in place and tagged NO ORDER on the bay drawing. The bay, the jacks, the time and the operator are in the bay log, which hub command can read in the Hub Circuit Patch Panel (View, Bay log) for any bay.

3. Decision is requested: raise an order for the repatch, or return the circuit to its own pair. Network Operations will not act on the cord before a decision is given. Orders of record are in :x[OSC-MEM-08-0244].

4. Further repatches without order are entered in the bay log and are not reported by separate message.

Watch Supervisor\
Network Operations, Communications Security
