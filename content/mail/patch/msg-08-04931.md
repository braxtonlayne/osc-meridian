COMMUNICATIONS SECURITY\
Network Operations, ALPHA-02

1. At {time} on {date} a repatch was made at bay {bay} from your position: {jacks}. The circuits concerned are {circuits}. No circuit order was held for it. This copy is addressed to the patch positions list and copied to your position.

2. The cord has been left in place and is tagged NO ORDER on the bay drawing. It has been entered in the bay log and reported to the hub command, as :x[OSC-MEM-07-1186] para 5 requires. Network Operations will decide whether an order is raised for it or the circuit is returned to its own pair. Until then, do not remove it, and do not make a second repatch on the same route.

3. If the repatch was made to restore a circuit with a fault, say so to the test desk on extension 4400 at ALPHA-02 and quote the test number. A fault report is not withdrawn because a repatch has been made. Circuit designations and orders of record are in :x[OSC-MEM-06-0291].

4. Positions other than hub command do not hold repatch authority. A repatch without an order is not in itself a security incident and is not reported to the Office of Internal Assurance by this office.

Watch Supervisor\
Network Operations, Communications Security
