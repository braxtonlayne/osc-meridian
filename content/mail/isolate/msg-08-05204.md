COMMUNICATIONS SECURITY\
Network Operations, ALPHA-02

NETWORK NOTICE

1. The key distribution network has been isolated by manual initiate for the second time from the same control. Every remote hub circuit is DOWN.

2. Under :x[OSC-MEM-07-0233] para 3 the circuits are held down for the full line-check period. They may be restored earlier only on a Circuit Restoration Certificate approved by the Deputy Commander, ALPHA-02.

3. Mail is queued. Telephone calls beyond the local hub are refused. Movements are holding. The arrangements in our notice of the first isolation continue to apply.

4. Account holders are reminded that the control is not to be operated except under :x[OSC-DIR-0507] para 3.1.

This notice was issued to all MERIDIAN account holders at hub sites.
