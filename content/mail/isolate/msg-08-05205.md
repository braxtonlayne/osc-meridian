COMMUNICATIONS SECURITY\
Network Operations, ALPHA-02

1. Control HS-0001 at workstation A03-5-114, ALPHA-03, has been operated for the second time. All remote hub circuits are down.

2. Under :x[OSC-DIR-0507] para 6.2 you are the certifying officer for restoration, the issuing switch being at this hub. From this initiation, circuits may be restored before the end of the line-check period only on a Circuit Restoration Certificate approved by you. From the third initiation there is no other means of restoration.

3. Certificates are raised at the control and routed to you with the operator's statement. They are decided at the restoration display of the Network Isolation application, which is available at your workstation. Approval restores the circuits at once. Return leaves them down.

4. Approval of a certificate does not reduce the escalation level of the control and does not close any report to the Office of Internal Assurance.

NETWORK OPERATIONS\
Communications Security, ALPHA-02
