COMMUNICATIONS SECURITY\
Network Operations, ALPHA-02

NETWORK NOTICE

1. Control HS-0001 has been operated for the fifth time. Under :x[OSC-DIR-0507] para 5.4 the control is withdrawn from service in the actuated position and the isolation is held.

2. Restoration of the hub circuits is reserved to the Council Secretariat. It is not made by Network Operations and is not made from any workstation. No date is given.

3. Until restoration, each site works on its local hub. Mail beyond the local hub is queued and is not lost. Telephone calls beyond the local hub are refused. Urgent business is passed through the duty officer (:x[OSC-DIR-0447] para 7).

4. No further notice will be issued until the circuits are restored.

This notice was issued to all MERIDIAN account holders at hub sites.
