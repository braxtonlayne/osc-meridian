**OFFICE OF INTERNAL ASSURANCE**\
**FINDING**

Subject: operation of control HS-0001 (Network Isolation, Manual Initiate)\
Initiation: {ref}, {time}, operator {operator}

1. The Office finds that control HS-0001 was operated for the third time otherwise than under :x[OSC-DIR-0507] para 3.1, and that the operation isolated the key distribution network.

2. The finding is entered on the security record of the initiating operator. It is a finding of fact. It is not a disciplinary decision, which rests with the operator's division.

3. Workstation A03-5-114 is placed under review with effect from the initiation. Activity at the workstation is reviewed in full. The lock screen of the workstation records the review. The workstation remains assigned.

4. The circuits remain down until a Circuit Restoration Certificate is approved by the Deputy Commander, ALPHA-02. On restoration a tamper seal will be fitted to the cover of the control.

5. Representations on this finding are made in writing to the Resident Assurance Officer, ALPHA-03, within five working days. They are not made by reply to this message (:x[OSC-DIR-0378]).
