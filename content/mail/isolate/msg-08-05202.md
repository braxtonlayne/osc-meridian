COMMUNICATIONS SECURITY\
Network Operations, ALPHA-02

NETWORK NOTICE

1. The key distribution network has been isolated by manual initiate. Every remote hub circuit is DOWN. The notification area of each workstation shows the state of each hub.

2. Controlled mail to sites beyond the local hub is queued and will be delivered in order when the circuits are restored. Do not resend it. Telephone calls beyond the local hub are refused by the switch. Urgent business is passed through the duty officer (:x[OSC-DIR-0447] para 7).

3. Rail and tram movements are holding at the next station. Patch circuits between sites are dead.

4. Line checks are in progress. The circuits will be restored without further notice. Do not call Network Operations to ask when.

This notice was issued to all MERIDIAN account holders at hub sites.
