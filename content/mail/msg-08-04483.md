TRANSIT OPERATIONS, NETWORK CONTROL, ALPHA-01

1. Your Security Incident Report on :x[OSC-F-114] concerns an indication on the network and has been referred by the Office of Internal Assurance to Transit Operations for disposition. A report on network indications is classified SEXTANT. Under :x[OSC-DIR-0412] para 4(c) the reporting officer is read into the compartment of the report, to the extent necessary to complete the report. You are read into SEXTANT (OSC-CB-013) on that basis from receipt of this message.

2. Report particulars as entered:

| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| Form | OSC-F-114, Security Incident Report |
| Reporting officer | OPR-A03-1147, Records Division, ALPHA-03 |
| Referred by | Resident Assurance Officer, ALPHA-03 |
| Classified | SEXTANT |
| Disposition authority | Director, Transit Operations |

3. Disposition. The indication reported is attributable to possession. Segments under possession for engineering are shown occupied on the board whether or not a party is detected in a given block, and an occupancy shown during a possession is not an irregular indication. Compare log entry :x[OSC-TRN-INC-08-017], closed on the same basis 14 MAY 2008 (possession :x[OSC-TRN-POS-08-044]). :r[Movement on the segment during possessions is under Transit Control instruction TC-08-0011.]{tier=2 auth="TO" ref="TO-08-011"}

4. The report is closed. No further action. The reporting officer is not advised further, and a further report on the same indication is not required. Suppressed consist identifiers on the board are explained in :x[KB-0042].

5. The read-in stands until you are debriefed on transfer. Network geography released to you under it is for your reference at the workstation and is not to be reproduced, drawn or summarised for any person not holding SEXTANT. The duty dispatcher is copied for the log.
