I am writing ahead of your interim review rather than waiting for it, because it affects the Stacks C queue this month.

The branch is short a Tier 2 reviewer until further notice. Items in the accessions review queue that carry Tier 2 material currently wait for me, and I am the only Tier 2 reviewer left in the branch. That is not a good use of either of us.

Your access record shows a number of compartment read-ins since your day-30 report. That is unusual for a probationer, and it means most of the queue is already within your briefings. I would like you to apply for an acting designation at Tier 2 so that you can clear those items yourself.

Probationers are not normally eligible (:x[OSC-DIR-0247]; :x[OSC-PER-TRF]). :x[OSC-DIR-0395] makes an exception where operational necessity is certified, and I am prepared to say that it is. The application goes on :x[OSC-F-126] under DIR-0395 para 7:

- Compartment requested: ACTING-T2
- Sponsor PN: P-2-04-0218 (myself, as supervisor of record)
- Justification: Tier 2 review backlog, Accessions & Review Branch, Stacks C

It is subject to the Deputy Commander's certification, which the command section arranges once I have endorsed it. You do not need to approach the command section yourself.

Two things to be clear about. An acting designation lets you read at the higher tier; it does not brief you into anything you are not already briefed into. And it does not shorten or alter your probation. Your day-60 and day-90 reports stand as scheduled.

If you would rather not, say so and I will note it on the review. Nobody will hold it against you.
