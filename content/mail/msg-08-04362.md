SUBJECT: MINIMUM MANNING, FEDERAL HOLIDAY (MEMORIAL DAY), 26 MAY 2008

REFERENCES: (a) :x[OSC-DIR-0189], Leave, Federal Holidays and Minimum Manning; (b) Facility calendar, MAY 2008; (c) :x[OSC-MEM-08-0187], Reconciliation of April 2008 Pay Run to Facility Strength Returns

1. Monday 26 MAY 2008 is observed as a federal holiday. ALPHA-03 will operate at minimum manning from 0400Z 26 MAY (0000 EDT 26 MAY) to 0400Z 27 MAY (0000 EDT 27 MAY).

2. Division chiefs are to return the holiday duty roster to the Command Section by 1600Z (1200 EDT) Friday 16 MAY 2008. Rosters show, for each person on duty, name, personnel number, post and watch. Nil returns are required.

3. Posts to be filled for each watch:
    - power plant watch;
    - life support watch (air handling and water);
    - Portal Security, Levels 0 and 1;
    - operations desk duty officer;
    - medical officer on call;
    - Records Division stacks environmental check, Levels 6 to 8 (one round per watch).

3a. Rosters are checked against the facility strength return, not the pay run. Persons carried on the April pay run who do not appear on the strength return are not to be rostered; queries on the difference go to Pay & Strength Accounting, PV, and not to the Command Section (reference (c)).

4. Probationary transfers may be rostered but are not to be designated in charge of a watch.

5. Leave for 23 MAY or 27 MAY is requested on OSC-F-104 through the normal channel. Minimum manning is not waived for either day, and leave already approved for personnel subsequently rostered will be honoured only if a substitute is named on the roster.

6. Dining on the holiday: brunch 1300Z to 1500Z (0900 to 1100 EDT), dinner 2200Z to 2330Z (1800 to 1930 EDT). The duty officer's contact will be posted at the operations desk. The Lot C shuttle does not run.

7. Point of contact: B. Shoal, Command Section, ext. 1104.
