NETWORK CONTROL\
Transit Operations, ALPHA-01

1. A movement entered from your desk has been brought to a stand under network isolation. Every movement on the network stood at the same moment.

2. The movement will remain at a stand after hub circuits are restored. It is released at the desk that entered it, train by train (:x[OSC-TRN-DSP-01] section 6). Network Control does not release desk movements.

3. Do not ask Network Control for a release while hub circuits are down. It cannot be given and the request is not recorded.

4. Consignments on a held consist remain in the custody of the train crew. Passengers are not detrained in section.

NETWORK CONTROL\
