TRANSIT OPERATIONS\
ALPHA-01

{date}

1. Your workstation attempted live working at a Dispatcher Workstation. Live working requires the desk key, which is issued with the Transit Control desk qualification (course TC-2) and to facility command posts (:x[OSC-TRN-DSP-01] section 2).

2. Course TC-2 is not open to staff on probation or to staff outside Transit Operations. Applications are not accepted on this message.

3. The training simulator TS-2 on the same workstation is open to Tier 3 operators and may be used for familiarisation. Use of the simulator is not entered on your training record and does not count toward the course.

4. No action is required of you.
