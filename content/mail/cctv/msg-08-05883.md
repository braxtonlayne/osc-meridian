SECURITY FORCES COMMAND\
Electronic Security Section

At {time} on {date} camera {camera} ({facility}, {location}) was moved from a workstation under operator {operator}.

The control room saw the move on its own monitors. A camera moved from a workstation is moved for everyone, including the post watching it. Workstation moves are allowed under :x[OSC-DIR-0474] para 7 to stored presets and in small steps, and the camera returns to its home preset by itself.

If the control room needs the camera it will take it back without notice. Please do not move it again while it is being steered from the control room.

Duty Controller\
Electronic Security Section, SFC
