OFFICE OF INTERNAL ASSURANCE\
Monitoring Section

On {date} the Facility Video client recorded {count} refused requests for video under operator {operator}. The most recent, at {time}, was for camera {camera} ({facility}, {location}).

Refused requests are reviewed under :x[OSC-DIR-0474] para 6. Access to video is set per camera by the level the camera views and the site it is at; the client applies those rules and does not display the picture when they are not met. Repeating the request does not change the result.

If your duties need video from cameras outside your access, your section head applies to the site security office, with the camera numbers and the reason. A compartment briefing, where one is needed, is arranged under :x[OSC-DIR-0412].

This message is for your information. It is entered in the monitoring log with the refusals.
