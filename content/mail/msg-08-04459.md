**SERVICE DESK NOTICE: PLANNED MAINTENANCE**

| Item | Entry |
|---|---|
| Change | CHG-08-0644 |
| Site | ALPHA-03 (all print queues) |
| Service | MERIDIAN print release |
| Window | Friday 16 MAY 2008, 0600Z to 0800Z (0200 to 0400 EDT) |
| Impact | Print release unavailable. Printing to a queue is still accepted. |
| Work | Print server patching and certificate renewal. |

Jobs submitted during the window are held on the server and passed to the device queues at 0800Z (0400 EDT). They can then be released in the normal way.

This notice goes to every ALPHA-03 account. Night watch personnel please note the window falls in your watch.

**Reminder: print release (:x[KB-0019], :x[OSC-DIR-0319])**

- Release your job at the device with your badge and token. Do not release a job for another user, and do not ask anyone to release one for you.
- Unreleased jobs are purged four hours after they reach the device queue. Purges are logged against the account. Jobs held through the maintenance window count their four hours from 0800Z.
- A cover sheet prints with every job. It is mandatory. Do not detach it or discard it; it carries the print record for the copy and goes with the copy until the copy is filed or destroyed.
- UMBRA//OSC EYES ONLY material is not reproduced in any form, by print or otherwise (DIR-0319).
- Accounts suspended from print release because the annual security refresher is incomplete stay suspended. The maintenance does not change that and the Service Desk cannot lift it. Complete the refresher; release is restored automatically the next morning.

If the device shows HELD after 0815Z (0415 EDT), log a ticket rather than resubmitting. Resubmitted jobs print twice and both copies are logged.

Service Desk hours are 0600Z to 1800Z (0200 to 1400 EDT).
