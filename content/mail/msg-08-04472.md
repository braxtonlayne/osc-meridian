**MENU: CYCLE 4 OF 6, WEEK 3. MONDAY 19 MAY TO SUNDAY 25 MAY 2008**

| Day | Lunch | Dinner |
|---|---|---|
| Mon 19 | Beef and barley soup; turkey club; garden peas | Meatloaf with brown gravy; mashed potatoes; green beans |
| Tue 20 | Chicken noodle soup; grilled cheese; tomato salad | Pork chops; scalloped potatoes; buttered carrots |
| Wed 21 | Chili con carne; cornbread; coleslaw | Spaghetti with meat sauce; garlic bread; tossed salad |
| Thu 22 | Baked chicken *(see note 2)*; rice pilaf; broccoli | Salisbury steak; egg noodles; mixed vegetables |
| Fri 23 | New England clam chowder; tuna melt; potato salad | Breaded fish portion; french fries; hush puppies; coleslaw |
| Sat 24 | Brunch: scrambled eggs, sausage links, pancakes | Pot roast; roasted potatoes; corn |
| Sun 25 | Brunch: french toast, bacon, fruit cup | Roast turkey; stuffing; cranberry sauce; green beans |

**Notes**

1. Memorial Day, Monday 26 MAY: holiday hours, see the Command Section notice. Week 4 menu will follow next Friday.
2. Thursday fish is replaced by baked chicken for the whole of cycle 4 owing to a supplier substitution. Fish returns to Thursdays with cycle 5. The Friday fish portion is unaffected.
3. The salad bar will be closed Monday 19 MAY, 1500Z to 1700Z (0900 to 1100 MDT), for the kitchen hood suppression system inspection (SAF schedule). Hot meals unavailable for the period; cold service from the servery.
4. Special diets (medical, religious) are arranged through the site medical officer, not through the servery. Requests already approved carry over between cycles and do not need to be renewed.
5. Trays to the return belt, please. Cutlery has again been found in the berthing corridors.
