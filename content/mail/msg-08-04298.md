SUBJECT: SURFACE LOT ALLOCATION, QUARTER 3 (05 MAY TO 01 AUG 2008)

REFERENCE: :x[OSC-DIR-0107], Space and Parking Allocation at ALPHA-Class Facilities

1. The allocation board met on 29 APR 2008 and approved the surface lot allocation for Quarter 3. The allocation is posted on the board at Level 1 Portal Security from 1300Z (0900 EDT) Monday 05 MAY 2008 and takes effect that day. Quarter 2 hang tags are void from 2359Z (1959 EDT) 04 MAY.

2. Lots are allocated as follows:

    | Lot | Location | Spaces | Allocated to |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | A | Depository Administration Building, north apron | 64 | Command Section; watch supervisors; medical officer on call; division chiefs |
    | B | East lot, beside the gatehouse | 112 | By shift, from the shift lists returned by divisions |
    | C | Overflow (gravel), west of the vehicle lift head | 90 | Probationary transfers; temporary duty; personnel not on a shift list |
    | V | Front of building | 8 | Cover establishment use only |

3. Lot C is served by the gatehouse shuttle at 15 and 45 minutes past the hour, 1000Z to 0200Z. The shuttle does not run on federal holidays; minimum manning arrangements will be notified separately.

4. Lot V is for the cover establishment's visitors and its own staff. It is not to be used by facility personnel at any time, including for drop-off. Vehicles in Lot V without a cover establishment pass will be reported.

5. The apron in front of the vehicle bay is for armoured vehicle movements. Vehicles left on the apron are removed by the cover establishment's contractor at the owner's expense. This has happened twice this quarter.

6. Car pools of three or more personnel on the same shift may apply for Lot B spaces from the Lot C allocation. Applications on the allocation request slip to B. Shoal, Command Section, by 23 MAY 2008. Requests received after that date will be considered at the August board.

7. Divisions are reminded that individual allocations are made by the board and not by division chiefs. Correspondence about individual allocations will not be answered before the August board.
