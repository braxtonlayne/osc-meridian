**OFFICE OF INTERNAL ASSURANCE**\
**NOTICE OF READ-IN: COMPARTMENT BRIAR, BRIEFING OSC-CB-015**

Inquiry file: :x[OIA-08-031]\
Basis: :x[OSC-DIR-0378], self-reporting party\
Read-in limited to: inquiry file OIA-08-031 only

1. Your Security Violation Self-Report on :x[OSC-F-160] has been received by the Resident Assurance Officer, ALPHA-03, and entered on inquiry file OIA-08-031. Under :x[OSC-DIR-0378] a person who reports in respect of an open inquiry is read into the compartment in which the inquiry is held. Inquiry OIA-08-031 is held in BRIAR. You are read into BRIAR with effect from receipt of this notice.

2. The read-in is for the inquiry file OIA-08-031 only. It permits you to read the inquiry log and those portions of other records released to you in connection with it. It does not make you a holder of BRIAR for any other purpose, and it does not extend to the contingency protocols held by this Office. Portions of records released to you under it may cite those protocols by designation; that does not open them. :r[The inquiry file cross-refers to protocols OSC-CP-04 and OSC-CP-06. Both are held in BRIAR at Tier 2 and are not opened by this read-in.]{tier=2 compartment=BRIAR auth="OIA" ref="OIA-08-031"}

3. Your supervisor of record does not hold BRIAR. She has been informed that you have been read in and of nothing further. Matters within the compartment are not to be discussed with her, or with any member of the Records Division, except with the Resident Assurance Officer, ALPHA-03 (D. Prewitt, extension 3-3014).

4. Any further information you hold or come to hold on the provenance of the item under inquiry, including markings, copy numbers, custody, or the condition of the media on which it was received, is to be reported on :x[OSC-F-114]. It is not to be reported by reply to this message, by telephone, or on a further OSC-F-160. A self-report is made once.

5. Your self-report will be considered in mitigation under :x[OSC-DIR-0378]. No finding is made by this notice, and none is to be inferred from it.

6. The inquiry carries a suspense of 30 MAY 2008. You will be debriefed from BRIAR when the file is closed. Debriefing is recorded in the same manner as this read-in and is not separately notified.

**ACKNOWLEDGEMENT**

I acknowledge that I have been briefed into compartment BRIAR (OSC-CB-015) for inquiry file OIA-08-031 only; that I will not disclose its existence or content to any person not briefed; and that the obligations of the briefing continue after debriefing, transfer or release.
