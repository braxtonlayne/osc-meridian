Your workstation was added to the distribution of work order FE-08-0417 when the work order was looked up from it. Distribution of a work item reads its recipients into the compartment of the item (:x[OSC-DIR-0412] para 4(e)). You are therefore read into TALLOW (OSC-CB-014) for the duration of the work order. When FE-08-0417 is closed you will be removed from its distribution and debriefed. No further notice is sent.

What this gives you. TALLOW covers strategic reserve positions by site and reactor operations: plant status, refuelling schedules, fuel inventory and burn-down planning figures. In practice you will now see those fields on the reactor status pages, starting with :x[OSC-RSV-RX-A03], and the site columns on the reserve pages.

How the figures are stated. Every reserve and burn-down figure published by this Division is stated against the sustained capacity of :c[headline.capacitySustained] persons, as fixed in :x[OSC-MEM-07-0612]. Do not recompute burn-down against site strength or berth counts; the result will not agree with ours and is not to be quoted. :r[Reserve sufficiency at ALPHA-03 is computed for the designated persons listed in Annex D to the reserves basis, and not for site strength.]{tier=2 compartment=TALLOW auth="LR" ref="LR-R-07-12"}

For your information only, since it is on the same work order: the compressor maintenance on seed vault cold room 2 (Level 8) proceeds today, 20 MAY 2008, 1300Z to 1900Z (0900 to 1500 EDT), within the load restriction. Temperature logging continues under the vault custodian. Records Division staff do not need to take any action on the vault (:x[OSC-RSV-SEED]).

Reserve figures are not to be given in answer to dining, supply or stores enquiries at your site. Refer them to LR/ALPHA-05.
