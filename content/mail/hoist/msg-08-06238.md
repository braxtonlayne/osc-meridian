OFFICE OF INTERNAL ASSURANCE\
Case Intake, ALPHA-01

A winder house has notified this office of a shaft hoist emergency stop applied from a MERIDIAN workstation, as :x[OSC-DIR-0466] requires. The notification gives the hoist and the time only.

The hoist event recorder in Shaft Hoist Control gives the position and origin of the stop. The workstation that applied it can be identified from the recorder's origin column and its audit trail read under Audit review.

No inquiry is opened on a notification of this kind. It is filed for the pattern review. Further notifications from the same workstation are not sent separately; they appear in the recorder.

Case Intake\
Office of Internal Assurance, ALPHA-01
