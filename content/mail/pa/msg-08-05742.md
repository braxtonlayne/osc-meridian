NETWORK OPERATIONS, COMMUNICATIONS SECURITY

On {date} at {time} the command console at ALPHA-02 attempted a call on {zone} at {site}. The {site} controller accepts calls from its own consoles only, and the call was not carried.

A call needed at another site is passed to that site's command section by telephone or message, and that site makes it (:x[OSC-DIR-0458] para 4). Where a call is for every site, Network Control makes it on the network all-call.

This note is for your information and has not been copied.
