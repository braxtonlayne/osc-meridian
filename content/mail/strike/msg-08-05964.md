OPERATIONS AND CONTINUITY PLANNING\
Planning Cell, ALPHA-01

An estimate run from your workstation on {date} reads {site} as ISOLATED or worse, and the estimator lists segment {segment} in its egress basis.

The egress table in PM-3 is the inter-hub segment table as it stood at model revision 2 and is not re-issued between revisions (:x[OSC-MEM-07-0312] para 8). It is not a statement that a segment is available for egress on any day. The route length printed against it is the engineering figure (:x[OSC-TRN-ENG]).

Questions about the status of a segment are for Transit Operations. This office does not hold a date for revision 3.

Planning estimates that rely on the listed segment should say so in the remark when they are entered in the register.

This note is sent once to each workstation profile.

Planning Cell\
Operations and Continuity Planning, ALPHA-01
