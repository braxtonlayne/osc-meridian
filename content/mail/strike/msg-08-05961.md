OFFICE OF INTERNAL ASSURANCE\
Register Review, ALPHA-01

Entry {entry} in the PM-3 planning register, made from your workstation on {date}, places {site}, your installation of assignment, inside the outer contour of the estimate. The estimated condition recorded for {site} was {condition}.

Planning against an operator's own installation is reserved to the planning cell of its site command (:x[OSC-MEM-08-0166] para 4). The entry has been referred to this office as a matter of routine. It has not been treated as a violation and it has not been withdrawn.

You are asked to state, in one or two lines, the purpose for which the entry was made. A reply to this address is sufficient. If the entry was made in error, it may be withdrawn from the Register view of the Survivability Estimator; the withdrawal is recorded.

This office does not comment on the estimate. :x[OSC-MEM-07-0312] para 1 describes what PM-3 figures may be used for.
