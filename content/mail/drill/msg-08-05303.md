CIRCUIT OFFICE, COMMUNICATIONS SECURITY, ALPHA-02

Your query of {date} on drill {drill}, passed by Network Control.

```
Line queried       ACK {drill}, {site}
Time stamped       {ack} (drill time)
Order received     {receipt} (drill time)
Circuit            {circuit}
```

1. The acknowledgement was carried on {circuit}, not on the inter-hub circuit that carried the order to {site}. Order wires are time-stamped by the clock of the terminating station, not by Network Control. A stamp earlier than the receipt of the order is therefore a clock offset between stations.

2. The platform 7 order wire is maintained for the segment it serves. It is not patched to Network Control and is not part of the drill relay listed in :x[OSC-DIR-0433] Annex A.

3. No fault is recorded against the circuit. The circuit office does not hold the name of the operator who answered on it.

4. The query is closed. No further action.

CIRCUIT OFFICE
