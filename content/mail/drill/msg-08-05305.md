The Office of Internal Assurance observed the network seal drill ordered from your order position today, under :x[OSC-DIR-0433] para 7.

Observation, for the ordering command:

1. The order was given under the two-person rule. The read-back was entered and agreed.

2. The relay traffic in the coordinator's log agrees with Annex A of the directive in the order of sites. Where the log shows an acknowledgement entered before the site concerned received the order, the circuit office attributes the stamp to clock offset. OIA records such lines as received and makes no finding on them.

3. Sites carried as NO REPORT are a matter for the ordering command and Network Control. OIA will read the replies to Network Control's query when they are filed.

No finding is opened. This note will be kept with the drill register entry.
