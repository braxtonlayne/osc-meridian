OFFICE OF INTERNAL ASSURANCE\
Resident Assurance Officer, ALPHA-03

Audit reference {auditRef}

Between {firstTime} and {time} your workstation examined {count} withheld items, including items in {docNo}. Examining withholding marks is not itself a violation.

A withholding mark shows the authority and reference under which material is withheld, and nothing else. The authorities and reference formats are listed in :x[OSC-ASR-CG]. Where the withholding is by compartment, the material is released at the workstation when the compartment is briefed, in the ways set out in :x[OSC-DIR-0412] para 4. It is not released on enquiry to the withholding authority, and enquiries of that kind are not answered.

Each examination is recorded as a separate audit entry. No reply is required.
