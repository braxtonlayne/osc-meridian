DOOR RECORDS OFFICE\
Facilities Engineering, Plant Network Section

On {date} at {time} the interlock bypass switch on airlock {airlock} at {site} was turned from the board by operator {operator}. Key {key} was not in the switch. The interlock was not bypassed and the airlock is unaffected.

The bypass position is for maintenance of the leaf drives under a signed permit. The key is held by the site duty officer and is issued against the permit (:x[OSC-MEM-08-0147], para 6). It is not issued to the board to clear a refused cycle. A refused cycle means a leaf is not closed and locked, and the board names the leaf.

This notice is filed with the airlock's record. A further attempt is reported to the Office of Internal Assurance.
