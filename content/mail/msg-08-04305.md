All,

The particulate pre-filters on AHU-07 through AHU-12 will be changed on Monday 05 MAY 2008 between 1300Z and 1700Z (0900 to 1300 EDT). This is the regular 90-day change under :x[OSC-DIR-0128]. Work order FE-08-0391.

Each unit is isolated in turn for about 40 minutes. Zones served by a unit run at reduced supply while it is isolated. Approximate times:

| Unit | Serves | Isolated (approx.) |
|---|---|---|
| AHU-07 | Level 3 dining hall and servery | 1300Z to 1340Z (0900 to 0940 EDT) |
| AHU-08 | Level 3 clinic and post room | 1345Z to 1425Z (0945 to 1025 EDT) |
| AHU-09 | Level 5 Records Division offices and reading room | 1430Z to 1510Z (1030 to 1110 EDT) |
| AHU-10 | Level 6 stacks A-B | 1515Z to 1555Z (1115 to 1155 EDT) |
| AHU-11 | Level 7 stacks C | 1600Z to 1640Z (1200 to 1240 EDT) |
| AHU-12 | Level 4 berthing, corridors A and B | 1640Z to 1700Z (1240 to 1300 EDT) |

Points to note:

- **Reading room:** no supply air for about 40 minutes while AHU-09 is out. The doors will be wedged open. Please leave them that way. If the room gets close, the Branch has agreed people can work in the Level 5 conference room for that period.
- **Stacks:** the humidity log for Levels 6 and 7 will be checked when the work is finished. Please don't open the stack doors more than you have to while AHU-10 and AHU-11 are isolated.
- **Clinic:** the treatment room stays on its own extract and is not affected.
- Don't touch the room thermostats during the work. The controller resets the setpoints when each unit comes back, and adjusting them in the meantime just means a second visit.

There may be some filter dust on surfaces near the supply grilles afterwards. Custodial will wipe down the reading room tables at 1700Z (1300 EDT).

Any problems on the day, call the plant room on ext. 2207.
