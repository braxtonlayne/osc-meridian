REACTOR OPERATIONS SECTION\
Logistics & Reserves, ALPHA-05

{date}

At {time} your station operated a control on the live display of {unit} ({control}). The display is read only. Nothing reached the plant.

The live display shows a unit as its control room sees it. It is issued so that sites can see the state of their supply, not to operate it. Plant controls are worked from the unit's control room by a licensed operator on the current watch bill (:x[OSC-RSV-SIM-01], para 2.3). Your operator number is not on any watch bill.

Every attempt is recorded, and the host site's command is told when it is their unit. If you want to work a panel, set the PLANT LINK key switch to SIM and use the training simulator, which works every control.
