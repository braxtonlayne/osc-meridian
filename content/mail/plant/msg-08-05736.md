REACTOR OPERATIONS SECTION\
Logistics & Reserves

{date}

The simulator trip history shows {count} trips under your operator number, the latest on {unit}. The history is reviewed whenever trips start to collect at one station.

Nothing is wrong with trips on a trainer. That is what it is for. If the trips are on startup, they are almost always the startup rate: the rods are being withdrawn faster than power can follow. Withdraw in short pulses and let power settle between them. The quick reference (:x[OSC-RSV-SIM-02]) has the sequence on one page.

If you want a walk-through, reply and the section will arrange one.
