COUNCIL SECRETARIAT\
Office of the Secretary to the Council

1. The Secretariat has had from the Office of Internal Assurance the particulars you reported on :x[OSC-F-114] concerning the copy number of a Council record. The reconciliation of distribution copies against the Secretariat register is a matter for the Secretariat. So that you may assist in it to the extent your report requires, you are read into QUARRY (OSC-CB-016) for the purposes of the copy register reconciliation only.

2. The register of distribution copies is :x[OSC-CNL-SEAT-00]. Each seat holds the copy bearing its number, and on a succession the predecessor copy is destroyed before the successor copy is issued (:x[OSC-CNL-CHR-II]). Destruction of Council copies is administered by the Secretariat under its own procedure and is not certified on the Records Division form (:x[OSC-DIR-0354]). The succession to which your report relates is recorded in :x[OSC-CNL-MIN-06-04]. Where a certificate is shown as to follow, it follows in the ordinary course.

3. :r[Register comparison for copy 07 has been completed by the Secretariat and the result entered in the register.]{tier=1 auth="CS" ref="CS-06-104"}

4. The Secretariat is grateful for the care taken. No further reports on the matter are to be made, on OSC-F-114 or otherwise, and no enquiry is to be made of the Secretariat in respect of it. Notes or drafts you hold concerning it are to be handed to the Records Division counter as classified waste.

5. Council records released to you under this read-in are for reading at the workstation only. They are kept on paper by direction of the Council (:x[OSC-CNL-CHR-VII]), and what you see is a reading copy, not a copy for any other purpose.

6. Your acknowledgement on opening this message is accepted in lieu of signature. It will be entered in the Secretariat's paper register by the Minutes Clerk, who is copied. Attendance at the Secretariat counter at ALPHA-01 is not required while you are posted at ALPHA-03.

A. PENHALLOW\
Secretary to the Council
