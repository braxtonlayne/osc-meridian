All,

1. Annex lot permits for Quarter 3 are issued by the annex pass office, not by this facility, from Monday 02 JUN 2008. Quarter 2 permits are valid until 2359 site time on 01 AUG. Bring your annex identity card; the pass office will not accept an OSC badge and will ask what it is if you show one. Do not show one.
2. The Level 1 vehicle inspection hall keeps its bays as before: Security Forces and FE duty vehicles on the east side, the command section on the west. The command section bays are not available to visitors' drivers. Space allocation generally: :x[OSC-DIR-0107].
3. The parent installation towed four vehicles from the annex lot in April for expired permits. The owners paid. This facility cannot get a vehicle back from the parent installation's contractor and will not try.
4. Car pools: the annex pass office gives one permit per car pool of three. Apply to them directly.
