1. Surface Lot B will be closed for resurfacing from 0600 EDT Tuesday 27 MAY to 1800 EDT Thursday 29 MAY 2008. Lot B permit holders park in the overflow lot for those three days. Your Lot B hang tag is valid there; nobody else's is.
