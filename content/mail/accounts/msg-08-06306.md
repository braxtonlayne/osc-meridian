Ms Vance,

Your audit review of the A03-5 group shows on our side as a read of the current operator's trail at A03-5-114. That trail starts on 14 APR 2008, when the workstation was reissued.

The previous operator's profile was archived on reissue in the usual way: settings, mailbox and trail written to archive media, workstation cleared of the profile only (no media sanitisation; that is the accepted residual risk for the group). The archive is held with ticket SD-08-1187, which I closed on 07 MAY after passing the index access records to the RAO.

To restore the archived profile for review the Desk needs a new ticket raised by OIA under :x[OSC-DIR-0301], naming the profile by the previous operator's personnel number. Restoration takes one working day and is to a review workstation at ALPHA-01, not to A03-5-114. The current operator is not affected and is not told.

One point you may want for the file. The archived profile's mailbox has 3 messages flagged unsent. I have not opened them and will not unless the ticket says to.
