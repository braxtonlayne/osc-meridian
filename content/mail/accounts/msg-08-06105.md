Environmental watch,

A network seal drill can be ordered on any day without notice; drills are not booked in advance. When one is ordered, the order reaches this site from the adjacent hub, and the entry level desk calls the plant room on ext. 301 and on the panel buzzer.

When it comes:

1. Watch leader answers, repeats the order back, and writes the time in the log.
2. AHU-1 and AHU-2 to the drill setting on the panel. Makeup air closed. CBRN filtration on bank B (bank A stays off line while its change is outstanding).
3. Platform supply grilles closed. Plant room door G08-BD4 closed and dogged.
4. Call the desk with "plant ready" and the time. The desk reports the site sealed when the portals and the airlock are done.

The site is built to the times below. Acknowledge and report sealed are counted from receipt of the order; the door cycle is the time each seal door takes to close once the desk starts it, after the acknowledgement:

::canon-table{source="sealDrill.classes" classId="GAMMA" cols="ackMin:Acknowledge within (min),doorCycleMin:Door cycle (min),sealMin:Report sealed within (min)"}

Do not open a filter bank during a drill (:x[OSC-DIR-0128]). Stand down only when the desk calls it.
