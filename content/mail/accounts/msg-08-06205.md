Deputy Commander,

1. Under interim instruction OIA-II-08-04, inserted today in your copy of :x[OSC-DIR-0395] at para 3(b), you are named as certifying officer for requests from personnel of ALPHA-03 that need a deputy commander's certification. This includes compartment access requests on :x[OSC-F-126] and applications for acting credentials from probationers.
2. Requests will appear in your approvals queue (Forms, Action queue) from today. CMS has changed the routing table (CMS-08-0131).
3. Decide each request on the terms of the certifying officer's guidance with your copy of the form. Your decision is written to the applicant's mail at their next sign-in, in the registry's name.
4. The applicant is not told that the request has been routed to you. Do not contact the applicant or the applicant's supervisor about a request in your queue. Queries to this registry only.
5. The routing remains until this registry withdraws it. No end date has been set.

Compartment RegistryOffice of Internal Assurance, ALPHA-01
