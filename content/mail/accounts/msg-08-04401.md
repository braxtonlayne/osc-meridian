All,

The quarterly register return of incidents and dispositions is due to the Director on Friday 30 MAY 2008 at 2000Z (1600 EDT).

- Each case officer returns one line per open or closed case for the quarter: number, category, severity, status, disposition form (1 to 4) where closed, and the date of disposition.
- Disposition forms 3 and 4 are entered by the registry from the Director's decisions, not by case officers.
- Weekly register draws (incident logs, possession register, print release logs) for 14, 21 and 28 MAY are filed by 1200Z on the day of the draw. The 14 MAY draw is on file.
- Comments on the DIR-0378 Revision 5 draft go on the comment sheet by 23 MAY, not in the return.

The Level 4 photocopier is for unclassified work only. The cover sheet log by the release printer is not a notepad.
