COUNCIL SECRETARIAT

Director, Office of Internal Assurance

1. Your request of 13 MAY 2008, made under :x[OIA-08-031], for a comparison of the copy number on an image held by your Office with the register of distribution copies, has been put to the Secretary.
2. The Secretary has directed me to reply that inquiries into Council records are not within the Office's delegation, and that the comparison will not be provided.
3. The Secretary asks that the image and the media on which it is held be returned to the Secretariat registry, under custody receipt, at the close of your incident, and that they not be destroyed by your Office.
4. Further correspondence on this matter is to be addressed to the Secretary and not to the registry.

N. FenwickMinutes Clerk, Council Secretariat, for the Secretary to the Council
