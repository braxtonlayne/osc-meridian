All,

Transit Control at the adjacent hub has asked that the tram tunnel portal door, G08-BD3, be closed and held tomorrow, Tuesday 13 MAY 2008, from 2100Z to 0000Z (1600 to 1900 site time). No tram is booked for the period. No reason was given and none is needed.

- **Security Forces:** close G08-BD3 at 2058Z and log it. Platform camera G08-C04 stays on. Reopen at 0000Z on the desk's call, or at 0010Z on the standing instruction if the desk has not called.
- **Environmental watch (B):** close the platform supply grilles before 2100Z and reopen them after the door is back on its hooks. Expect a small change in the entry level differential while the door is shut. Log it; do not adjust for it.
- Nobody goes onto the platform while the door is closed.
