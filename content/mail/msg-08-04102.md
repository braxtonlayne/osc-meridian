Welcome to the Accessions and Review Branch.

You are posted to the Branch on a 90-day probationary transfer under :x[OSC-DIR-0247], beginning today, 14 APR 2008. I am your supervisor of record for the period. Interim reviews fall at day 30 and day 60 and the final review at day 90. You will have the day-30 report from me in writing; the review sessions are entered on the facility calendar.

Some practical points for the first week:

1. **Hours.** The Branch works 1200Z to 2030Z (0800 to 1630 EDT), Monday to Friday. The reading room counter is staffed from 1230Z (0830 EDT). Changes to your hours come through me, not the Command Section.
2. **Reading room.** Level 5, opposite the Branch offices. Compartmented files are read in the reading room or at your workstation. They do not go to Level 3, to berthing, or into the lift. Requests for the stacks on Levels 6 and 7 go through the request tray at the counter until your stacks escort period is signed off.
3. **Before you open any compartmented file**, read :x[OSC-DIR-0412]. It governs what you may be shown and how a briefing is recorded, and it is the reference on every refusal the system will give you. You are read into LANTERN on posting; Mr Harrow will send the acknowledgement once it is entered in the briefing register.
4. **Medical readiness.** Your certification carried over from your previous post. Keep it current. A lapsed certification takes you off the seal roster and it is not something I can sign around.
5. **Account.** The Service Desk will mail your operator ID and workstation separately. If the token does not seat, call the desk. Do not keep retrying; the reader locks after three attempts and Portal Security has to clear it.

Your desk is in the Branch open area on Level 5. My office is 5-021. I am in most mornings; if the door is shut, leave a note with the Branch clerk at the counter.

Glad to have you with us.
