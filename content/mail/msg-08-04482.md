Your Compartment Access Request on :x[OSC-F-126] has been endorsed by your sponsor and is approved. You are read into KESTREL under :x[OSC-DIR-0412] para 4(b) with effect from receipt. Your supervisor of record is copied for information only; the copy does not brief her.

**1. What KESTREL covers.** The compartment holds the engineering record of the transit tunnels. For your purposes that is the following.

| Record class | Held as | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Segment engineering sheets | :x[OSC-TRN-SEG-01] to :x[OSC-TRN-SEG-06] | Issued at revision. Where a segment has not been revised, the commissioning sheet governs. |
| Tunnel environmental data | :x[OSC-TRN-ENG] | Portal and block readings: temperature, CO2, humidity, seal differential. Logged continuously, reviewed by FE at the monthly walk-down. |
| Rolling stock technical records | :x[OSC-TRN-RS] | Build, allocation, overhaul and defect records for rail and tram consists. |
| Possession planning | :x[OSC-TRN-POS-08-044] and the possession register | FE requests; Transit Control grants and sets times. |
| Commissioning files | Held by FE/ALPHA-02, by segment | On request to the Tunnel Structures Engineer. |

**2. Handling.**

a. Engineering lengths, depths and readings are not to be given to anyone who does not hold KESTREL, including in answer to cost or scheduling enquiries. Refer such enquiries to FE/ALPHA-02.

b. Engineering figures are not to be substituted for reported figures in any return. Reported figures are a Transit Operations matter.

c. Segment sheets are not printed outside ALPHA-02 without my approval. Reading them at the workstation is permitted.

d. A reading on an environmental table is a measurement, not a report. If a reading concerns you, raise it with FE/ALPHA-02, who will advise whether it falls within a possession.

**3. What KESTREL does not cover.** Movement of consists is controlled by Transit Control and is outside this briefing. :r[Movement authority for consists numbered R04-900 to R04-999 rests with Transit Control under instruction TC-08-0011 and is not held by Facilities Engineering.]{tier=2 compartment=KESTREL auth="TO" ref="TO-08-011"}

**4. Acknowledgement.** Opening this message records your acknowledgement of OSC-CB-011. The briefing is held for the duration of your posting at ALPHA-03 and is withdrawn on transfer.
